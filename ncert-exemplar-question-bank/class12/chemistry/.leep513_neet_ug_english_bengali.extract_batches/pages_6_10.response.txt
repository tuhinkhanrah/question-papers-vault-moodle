\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\]<br/>Additionally, nitrogen is less electronegative than oxygen, so \(\mathrm{NH}_2^-\) donates electrons more readily than \(\mathrm{OH}^-\), and \(\mathrm{NH}_3\) is more
