Therefore, \(E_{\mathrm{Cell}}\) remains unchanged regardless of the size of the cell or the stoichiometric coefficients, making it an intensive property.</p><p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Hence, \(E_{\mathrm{Cell}}\) is an intensive property while \(\Delta_r G\) of the cell reaction is an extensive property.</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>
