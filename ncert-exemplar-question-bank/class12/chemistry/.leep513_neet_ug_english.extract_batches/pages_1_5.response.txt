A \(3^{\circ}\) amine has three carbon-containing groups bonded to the nitrogen atom and no \(\mathrm{N}-\mathrm{H}\) bonds.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Each Compound</strong><br/>1-methylcyclohexylamine contains an \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) group attached to a single carbon atom of the ring, making it a \(1^{\circ}\) amine.<br/>Triethylamine has the structure \((\mathrm{C_2H_5})_3\mathrm{N}\), where three ethyl groups are directly attached to the nitrogen atom, making it a \(3^{\circ}\) amine.<br/>tert-Butylamine has the structure \((\mathrm{CH_3})_3\mathrm{CNH_2}\), where the nitrogen atom is bonded to only one carbon atom, so it is a \(1^{\circ}\) amine.<br/>N-methylaniline has the structure \(\mathrm{C_6H_5NHCH_3}\), where the nitrogen atom is bonded to one phenyl group and one methyl group, making it a \(2^{\circ}\) amine.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Triethylamine is a \(3^{\circ}\) amine.</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

    <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>According to IUPAC nomenclature for secondary amines, the longest carbon chain attached to the nitrogen atom is chosen as the parent alkane/alkene, and the remaining alkyl group on the nitrogen atom is named as an \(N\)-substituted prefix. The principal functional group (\(-\mathrm{NH}-\)) gets the lowest possible locant.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Numbering and Naming</strong><br/>In \(\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CH_2-NH-CH_3}\), the longest carbon chain contains three carbon atoms with a double bond. Numbering starts from the carbon attached to the amine nitrogen so that the \(-\mathrm{NHCH_3}\) group gets locant 1: \(\overset{3}{\mathrm{C}}\mathrm{H_2}=\overset{2}{\mathrm{C}}\mathrm{H}-\overset{1}{\mathrm{C}}\mathrm{H_2}-\mathrm{NHCH_3}\). Thus, the parent chain is prop-2-en-1-amine, and the methyl substituent on nitrogen is denoted as N-methyl.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The correct IUPAC name is N-methylprop-2-en-1-amine.</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>
