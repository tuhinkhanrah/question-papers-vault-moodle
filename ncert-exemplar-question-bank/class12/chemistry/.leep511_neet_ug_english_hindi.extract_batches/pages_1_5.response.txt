  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The reaction of alcohols with \(\mathrm{HCl}\) in the presence of anhydrous \(\mathrm{ZnCl_2}\) (Lucas reagent) involves cleavage of the \(\mathrm{C{-}O}\) bond and proceeds through a carbocation intermediate (\(\mathrm{S_N1}\) mechanism).</p><p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>The rate of reaction depends directly on the stability of the carbocation formed after the protonated alcohol loses a water molecule.
