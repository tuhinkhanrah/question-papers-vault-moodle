  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Colloidal sol particles carry an electric charge, which is responsible for their stability against coagulation.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>The charge on sol particles originates from electron capture by sol particles (such as during electrodispersion in Bredig's arc method), preferential adsorption of common ions from the dispersion medium, and the resulting formation of the Helmholtz electrical double layer. Absorption of ionic species into the bulk does not produce surface charge on sol particles.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Therefore, absorption of ionic species from solution is not responsible for the presence of electric charge on sol particles.</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>
