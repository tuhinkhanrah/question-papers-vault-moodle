  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>3. A plot of volume \((V)\) versus temperature \((T)\) for a gas at constant pressure is a straight line passing through the origin.   <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>Fig. 5.1 shows four straight-line isobars of volume \(V\) (in \(\text{mL}\)) versus absolute temperature \(T\) (in \(\text{K}\)) passing through the origin, corresponding to pressures \(p_1, p_2, p_3,\) and \(p_4\).
