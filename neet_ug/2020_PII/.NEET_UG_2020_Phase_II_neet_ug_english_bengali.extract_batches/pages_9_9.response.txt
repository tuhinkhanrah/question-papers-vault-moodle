Thus, Bioreactor matches production of large quantities of products (ii).</li>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2020_PHOTOSYNTHESIS_MCQ_Q127 - Location and properties of RuBisCO</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>Which of the following statements is incorrect?</p>
<hr/>
<p>নিম্নলিখিত বিবৃতিগুলির মধ্যে কোনটি ভুল?</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Functional Properties and Spatial Distribution of RuBisCO</strong><br/>
RuBisCO (ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase-oxygenase) is the primary carbon-fixing enzyme in photosynthesis:
<ul>
  <li>It is a bifunctional enzyme capable of binding both \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) (carboxylase activity) and \(\mathrm{O}_2\) (oxygenase activity).</li>
  <li>Its substrate is ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP), a five-carbon keto-sugar.</li>
  <li>The reduction phase of the Calvin cycle (downstream of RuBisCO carboxylation) utilizes ATP and NADPH to reduce 3-PGA to triose phosphates.</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Spatial Separation in \(\mathrm{C}_4\) Plants</strong><br/>
In \(\mathrm{C}_4\) plants displaying Kranz anatomy, carbon fixation is divided spatially between two cell types:
<ul>
  <li><strong>Mesophyll cells:</strong> Possess PEP carboxylase (PEPcase) and completely lack RuBisCO. Initial fixation of atmospheric \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) occurs here to form oxaloacetic acid (\(\mathrm{C}_4\) acid).</li>
  <li><strong>Bundle sheath cells:</strong> Contain RuBisCO in abundance and lack PEPcase. Decarboxylation of \(\mathrm{C}_4\) acids releases high concentrations of \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) around RuBisCO within the bundle sheath cells.</li>
</ul>
Therefore, the site of RuBisCO activity in \(\mathrm{C}_4\) plants is the bundle sheath cell, not the mesophyll cell.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The statement "In \(\mathrm{C}_4\) plants, the site of RuBisCO activity is mesophyll cell" is incorrect.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: রুবিস্কো (RuBisCO)-এর বৈশিষ্ট্য ও বণ্টন</strong><br/>
রুবিস্কো হলো সালোকসংশ্লেষের প্রধান কার্বন স্থিতিকারী উৎসেচক:
<ul>
  <li>এটি একটি দ্বি-কার্যকরী উৎসেচক (কার্বক্সিলেজ ও অক্সিজিনেজ উভয় কাজ করে)।</li>
  <li>এর গ্রাহক বা সাবস্ট্রেট হলো একটি ৫-কার্বনযুক্ত যৌগ (RuBP)।</li>
  <li>ক্যালভিন চক্রে গ্লুকোজ তৈরির প্রক্রিয়ায় ATP এবং NADPH প্রয়োজন হয়।</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ২: \(\mathrm{C}_4\) উদ্ভিদে রুবিস্কোর অবস্থান</strong><br/>
ক্র্যাঞ্জ শারীরস্থানযুক্ত \(\mathrm{C}_4\) উদ্ভিদে মেসোফিল কোষে পেপকেস (PEPcase) থাকে এবং মেসোফিল কোষে রুবিস্কো সম্পূর্ণ অনুপস্থিত থাকে। রুবিস্কো উৎসেচকটি শুধুমাত্র নালিকা বান্ডিল আচ্ছাদী কোষে (bundle sheath cells) উপস্থিত থাকে।</p>

<hr/>
<p>ফসফরাস চক্র সম্পর্কে নিম্নলিখিত বিবৃতিগুলির মধ্যে কোনটি ভুল?</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Ecological Characteristics of the Phosphorus Cycle</strong><br/>
The phosphorus cycle is a classic sedimentary biogeochemical cycle:
<ul>
  <li>The natural reservoir of phosphorus is rock, which contains phosphorus in the form of phosphates.</li>
  <li>Weathering of rocks releases trace amounts of phosphates into soil solution, which are absorbed by plant roots.</li>
  <li>Phosphorus-solubilising bacteria assist in decomposing organic detritus and mineralizing phosphorus.</li>
  <li>Unlike the carbon or nitrogen cycles, there is no respiratory or gaseous release of phosphorus into the atmosphere; the atmospheric inputs through precipitation and gaseous exchanges are entirely negligible.</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Identification of the Incorrect Statement</strong><br/>
The statement claiming that "There is appreciable respiratory release of phosphorus into atmosphere" is factually incorrect because phosphorus does not enter or cycle through the atmosphere via respiration.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The incorrect statement is: "There is appreciable respiratory release of phosphorus into atmosphere."</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: ফসফরাস চক্রের বাস্তুতান্ত্রিক বৈশিষ্ট্য</strong><br/>
ফসফরাস চক্র হলো একটি পাললিক (sedimentary) ভূ-রাসায়নিক চক্র:
<ul>
  <li>ফসফেটের প্রধান প্রাকৃতিক আধার হলো শিলাস্তর।</li>
  <li>শিলা ক্ষয়প্রাপ্ত হয়ে ফসফেট মাটিতে মেশে এবং উদ্ভিদ তা শোষণ করে।</li>
  <li>ফসফেট দ্রবীভূতকারী ব্যাকটেরিয়া মৃত জীবদেহ থেকে ফসফরাস মুক্ত করতে সাহায্য করে।</li>
  <li>কার্বন বা নাইট্রোজেনের মতো শ্বসনের মাধ্যমে বায়ুমণ্ডলে ফসফরাস নির্গত হওয়ার কোনো সুযোগ নেই। বায়ুমণ্ডলীয় আদান-প্রদান এতে অত্যন্ত নগণ্য।</li>

According to astrophysical and geological estimates:
<ul>
  <li>The universe originated approximately \(20\text{ billion years}\) ago.</li>
  <li>The solar system and Earth formed about \(4.5\text{ billion years}\) ago.</li>
  <li>Life on Earth first appeared approximately \(4\text{ billion years}\) ago (about \(500\text{ million years}\) after the formation of the Earth).</li>

DNA fragments in a mixture are separated by electrophoresis.</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Population Density Changes</strong><br/>
Four basic processes govern fluctuations in population density (\(N\)):
<ul>
  <li><strong>Natality (\(B\)):</strong> Number of births during a given period (increases population density).</li>
  <li><strong>Mortality (\(D\)):</strong> Number of deaths during a given period (decreases population density).</li>
  <li><strong>Immigration (\(I\)):</strong> Number of individuals of the same species that enter a habitat from elsewhere during the considered time period (increases population density).</li>
  <li><strong>Emigration (\(E\)):</strong> Number of individuals that leave the habitat during the time period (decreases population density).</li>
</ul>
The equation for population growth is:
\[N_{t+1} = N_t + [(B + I) - (D + E)]\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Effect of Immigration</strong><br/>
Because immigration directly adds individuals into the habitat, it contributes positively to population size and density.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The impact of immigration on population density is positive.</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Oxidative Decarboxylation of Pyruvate</strong><br/>
During aerobic respiration, pyruvate formed by glycolysis in the cytoplasm enters the mitochondrial matrix, where it undergoes oxidative decarboxylation catalyzed by the multienzyme complex pyruvate dehydrogenase:
\[\text{Pyruvic acid} + \mathrm{CoA} + \mathrm{NAD}^+ \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Mg}^{2+},\text{ Pyruvate dehydrogenase}} \text{Acetyl CoA} + \mathrm{CO}_2 + \mathrm{NADH} + \mathrm{H}^+\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Essential Cofactors</strong><br/>
The catalytic activity of the pyruvate dehydrogenase enzyme complex requires magnesium ions (\(\mathrm{Mg}^{2+}\)) as a critical inorganic cofactor along with coenzymes such as thiamine pyrophosphate (TPP), lipoic acid, CoA, FAD, and \(\mathrm{NAD}^+\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Pyruvate dehydrogenase activity requires magnesium.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: পাইরুভেটের জারণধর্মী ডিকার্বক্সিলেশন</strong><br/>

\[\text{পাইরুভিক অ্যাসিড} + \mathrm{CoA} + \mathrm{NAD}^+ \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Mg}^{2+},\text{ পাইরুভেট ডিহাইড্রোজিনেজ}} \text{অ্যাসিটাইল CoA} + \mathrm{CO}_2 + \mathrm{NADH} + \mathrm{H}^+\]</p>

The process of evolution of different species in a given geographical area starting from a common ancestral point and literally radiating out to other geographical areas and ecological habitats is defined as adaptive radiation. Classic examples include Darwin's finches of the Galapagos Islands and Australian marsupials, where an ancestral stock radiated into diverse ecological niches developing distinct feeding habits and morphologies.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Distinction from Other Evolutionary Phenomena</strong><br/>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Saltation:</strong> Single-step large mutation leading to speciation (Hugo de Vries).</li>
  <li><strong>Co-evolution:</strong> Mutual evolutionary changes in two or more interacting species (e.g., pollinator and plant).</li>
  <li><strong>Natural selection:</strong> Differential survival and reproductive success of phenotypes.</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The described evolutionary phenomenon is adaptive radiation.</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Anatomical Organization of Cockroach Digestive System</strong><br/>
The alimentary canal of the cockroach (<em>Periplaneta americana</em>) is divided into foregut (stomodaeum), midgut (mesenteron), and hindgut (proctodaeum). The foregut is lined internally by an ectodermal cuticle.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Sequence of Organs within the Foregut</strong><br/>
From anterior to posterior:
<ol>
  <li><strong>Mouth:</strong> Opens into a short muscular pharynx.</li>
  <li><strong>Pharynx:</strong> Leads into a narrow, tubular oesophagus.</li>
  <li><strong>Oesophagus:</strong> Opens into a large, pear-shaped sac called the crop, used for storing food.</li>
  <li><strong>Crop:</strong> Opens into the gizzard (proventriculus), which possesses an outer layer of thick circular muscles and an inner cuticle forming six chitinous teeth for grinding food.</li>
</ol>
Thus, the correct sequence is: Mouth \(\rightarrow\) Pharynx \(\rightarrow\) Oesophagus \(\rightarrow\) Crop \(\rightarrow\) Gizzard.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The correct sequence of parts in the foregut is Mouth \(\rightarrow\) Pharynx \(\rightarrow\) Oesophagus \(\rightarrow\) Crop \(\rightarrow\) Gizzard.</p>

Hormones are categorized into four major chemical classes based on structure:
<ul>
  <li><strong>Peptides, polypeptides, and proteins:</strong> Hormones of the anterior and posterior pituitary (GH, TSH, ACTH, LH, FSH, oxytocin, vasopressin) as well as insulin and glucagon. Thus, Pituitary hormone matches (iii).</li>
  <li><strong>Biogenic amines (amino acid derivatives):</strong> Derivatives of tyrosine, such as epinephrine (adrenaline) and norepinephrine (noradrenaline), secreted by the adrenal medulla. Thus, Epinephrine matches (iv).</li>
  <li><strong>Neuropeptides:</strong> Endogenous opioid peptides produced in the brain and pituitary that act as neurotransmitters/neuromodulators, such as endorphins and enkephalins. Thus, Endorphins match (ii).</li>
  <li><strong>Steroids:</strong> Lipid-soluble hormones synthesized from cholesterol, such as cortisol, aldosterone, testosterone, estradiol, and progesterone. Thus, Cortisol matches (i).</li>
</ul></p>
