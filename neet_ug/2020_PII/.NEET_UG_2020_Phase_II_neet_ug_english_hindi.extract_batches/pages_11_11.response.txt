  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
      <p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>
      In recombinant DNA technology, each step has a specific biochemical method:
      1. Gel electrophoresis is an analytical technique used for the separation of DNA fragments based on their size, not for amplification.<br/>
      2. DNA ligase, not polymerase, is the enzyme responsible for joining the gene of interest with the vector DNA by forming phosphodiester bonds.<br/>
      3. Restriction endonuclease digestions are carried out by incubating purified DNA molecules with the specific restriction enzyme at optimum conditions of temperature and pH suited for that enzyme's activity.<br/>
      4. PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction) is used for the in vitro enzymatic amplification of a specific DNA sequence, not for isolation and physical separation.</p>
      <p><strong>Final Conceptual Answer:</strong><br/>
      Restriction enzyme digestions are performed by incubating purified DNA molecules with the restriction enzymes of optimum conditions.</p>
      <hr/>
      <p><strong>अवधारणात्मक सिद्धांत:</strong><br/>
      पुनर्योगज डीएनए प्रौद्योगिकी में प्रत्येक चरण की एक निश्चित जैव-रासायनिक प्रक्रिया होती है:
      1. जेल वैद्युतकणसंचलन का उपयोग डीएनए खंडों को उनके आकार के आधार पर अलग करने के लिए किया जाता है, प्रवर्धन के लिए नहीं।<br/>
      2. डीएनए लाइगेज एंजाइम वांछित जीन और संवाहक (वेक्टर) डीएनए को जोड़ने का कार्य करता है, पॉलिमरेज नहीं।<br/>
      3. प्रतिबंधन एंजाइम पाचन शुद्ध डीएनए अणुओं को विशिष्ट प्रतिबंधन एंजाइम के साथ अनुकूलतम तापमान और पीएच परिस्थितियों में रखकर किया जाता है।<br/>

    <text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2020_MCQ_Q156 - Maintenance of medullary interstitial osmolarity gradient</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
      <p>The increase in osmolarity from outer to inner medullary interstitium is maintained due to</p>
      <p>(i) close proximity between Henle's loop and vasa recta.<br/>
      (ii) counter current mechanism.<br/>
      (iii) selective secretion of \(\mathrm{HCO}_3^-\) and hydrogen ions in PCT.<br/>
      (iv) higher blood pressure in glomerular capillaries.</p>
      <hr/>
      <p>वृक्क के बाह्य मध्यांश से आंतरिक मध्यांश अंतराकाश में परासारिता (osmolarity) की वृद्धि को किसके द्वारा बनाए रखा जाता है?</p>
      <p>(i) हेनले पाश और वासा रेक्टा के बीच निकट संपर्क<br/>
      (ii) प्रतिधारा क्रियाविधि (counter current mechanism)<br/>
      (iii) पीसीटी (PCT) में \(\mathrm{HCO}_3^-\) और हाइड्रोजन आयनों का चयनात्मक स्राव<br/>
      (iv) ग्लोमेरुलर केशिकाओं में उच्च रक्तचाप</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
      <p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>
      In mammalian kidneys, the medullary osmotic gradient rises progressively from \(300\,\text{mOsmol L}^{-1}\) in the cortex to about \(1200\,\text{mOsmol L}^{-1}\) in the inner medullary interstitium. This steep osmotic gradient is established and maintained primarily by two interrelated anatomical and physiological features: the close physical proximity between Henle's loop and the vasa recta, and the counter-current flow of filtrate and blood operating within these parallel hairpin loops. Secretion in the PCT controls acid-base balance, and glomerular hydrostatic pressure mediates filtration, neither of which maintains the medullary interstitial gradient.</p>
      <p><strong>Final Conceptual Answer:</strong><br/>
      (i) and (ii)</p>
      <hr/>
      <p><strong>अवधारणात्मक सिद्धांत:</strong><br/>

    <text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2020_MCQ_Q157 - Type of immunity conferred by maternal colostrum</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
      <p>The yellowish fluid "colostrum" secreted by mammary glands of mother during the initial days of lactation has abundant antibodies (IgA) to protect the infant. This type of immunity is called as</p>
      <hr/>
      <p>स्तनपान के प्रारंभिक दिनों के दौरान माता की स्तन ग्रंथियों द्वारा स्रावित पीले तरल "कोलोस्ट्रम" (खीस) में शिशु की सुरक्षा के लिए प्रचुर मात्रा में प्रतिरक्षी (IgA) होते हैं। इस प्रकार की प्रतिरक्षा को क्या कहा जाता है?</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
      <p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>
      Immunity is broadly categorized as active or passive. Active immunity develops when an individual's own immune system produces antibodies following exposure to an antigen. Passive immunity occurs when ready-made, pre-formed antibodies are directly transferred from an external source to an individual. The yellowish fluid colostrum secreted during early lactation delivers maternal secretory IgA antibodies directly to the infant's digestive tract, providing natural passive immunity against pathogens.</p>
      <p><strong>Final Conceptual Answer:</strong><br/>
      Passive immunity</p>
      <hr/>
      <p><strong>अवधारणात्मक सिद्धांत:</strong><br/>
      प्रतिरक्षा को सक्रिय और निष्क्रिय श्रेणियों में विभाजित किया गया है। जब शरीर की अपनी प्रतिरक्षा प्रणाली प्रतिजन के संपर्क में आने पर प्रतिरक्षी का निर्माण करती है, तो इसे सक्रिय प्रतिरक्षा कहते हैं। जब शरीर को सीधे तैयार (पूर्व-निर्मित) प्रतिरक्षी दिए जाते हैं, तो इसे निष्क्रिय प्रतिरक्षा (Passive immunity) कहा जाता है। माता के कोलोस्ट्रम (खीस) में उपस्थित IgA प्रतिरक्षी सीधे शिशु को प्राप्त होते हैं, जो प्राकृतिक निष्क्रिय प्रतिरक्षा का उदाहरण हैं।</p>
      <p><strong>अंतिम अवधारणात्मक उत्तर:</strong><br/>
      Passive immunity</p>

  <name>
    <text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2020_MCQ_Q159 - Application of RNA interference in pest resistance</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
      <p>RNA interference is used for which of the following purposes in the field of biotechnology?</p>
      <hr/>
      <p>जैव प्रौद्योगिकी के क्षेत्र में आरएनए अंतर्क्षेप (RNA interference) का उपयोग निम्नलिखित में से किस उद्देश्य के लिए किया जाता है?</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
      <p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>
      RNA interference (RNAi) is a conserved eukaryotic gene-silencing mechanism triggered by double-stranded RNA (dsRNA), which leads to sequence-specific degradation of target mRNA. In biotechnology, this method was successfully deployed to protect tobacco plants against the parasitic root-knot nematode <em>Meloidogyne incognita</em>. Nematode-specific genes were introduced into the host plant via <em>Agrobacterium</em> vectors, producing complementary sense and antisense RNA that formed dsRNA, ultimately silencing critical nematode genes and conferring pest resistance.</p>
      <p><strong>Final Conceptual Answer:</strong><br/>
      To develop a pest resistant plant against infestation by nematode</p>
      <hr/>

      <p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>
      Total Lung Capacity (TLC) represents the maximum volume of air accommodated in the respiratory tract at the conclusion of a maximal, forced inspiration. It consists of the sum of all four primary respiratory volumes:
      \[\text{TLC} = \text{RV} + \text{ERV} + \text{TV} + \text{IRV}\]

      <p><strong>Final Conceptual Answer:</strong><br/>
      RV (Residual Volume); ERV (Expiratory Reserve Volume); TV (Tidal Volume); and IRV (Inspiratory Reserve Volume)</p>

      \[\text{TLC} = \text{RV} + \text{ERV} + \text{TV} + \text{IRV}\]
      जहाँ RV = अवशिष्ट आयतन, ERV = निःश्वसन सुरक्षित आयतन, TV = ज्वारीय आयतन, तथा IRV = अंतःश्वसन सुरक्षित आयतन है।</p>

      <p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>
      Cardiac output is the product of heart rate and stroke volume. Neural signals through the parasympathetic nervous system (via the vagus nerve) release acetylcholine, which decreases the heart rate, slows the conduction velocity of action potentials through the AV node, and consequently reduces cardiac output. In contrast, sympathetic nerves and adrenal medullary hormones (epinephrine and norepinephrine) increase heart rate, ventricular contractile force, and cardiac output.       Parasympathetic neural signals</p>
