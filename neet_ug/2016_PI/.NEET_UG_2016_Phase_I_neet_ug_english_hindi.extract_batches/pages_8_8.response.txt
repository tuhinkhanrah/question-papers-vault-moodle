  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>Identify the correct statement on 'inhibin'.</p>

<p>'इन्हिबिन' के संबंध में सही कथन की पहचान कीजिए।</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
Inhibin is a peptide hormone produced by the gonads that plays an important role in the negative feedback regulation of the reproductive endocrine axis. In females, it is synthesized and secreted by the granulosa cells of the developing ovarian follicles.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Mechanism of Action</strong><br/>
The primary physiological function of inhibin is to act directly on the gonadotroph cells of the anterior pituitary gland to inhibit the synthesis and secretion of follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH). It does not inhibit luteinizing hormone (LH) or prolactin secretion.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Conclusion and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Inhibin is produced by granulosa cells in the ovary and inhibits the secretion of FSH.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>

  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>It is much easier for a small animal to run uphill than for a large animal, because</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक बड़े जन्तु की तुलना में एक छोटे जन्तु के लिए चढ़ाई पर दौड़ना अधिक आसान होता है, क्योंकि</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
Basal metabolic rate per unit body mass is inversely related to body size across animal species. Smaller animals have a significantly larger surface area to volume ratio, leading to a much higher mass-specific metabolic rate.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Physiological Analysis</strong><br/>
Due to their higher mass-specific metabolic rate, smaller animals possess a greater rate of cellular respiration, faster ATP turnover, and greater relative energy mobilization per unit of body mass. Consequently, delivering energy to contracting muscles relative to their body weight is accomplished more rapidly during strenuous physical exertion such as running uphill.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Conclusion and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Small animals have a higher metabolic rate, making it easier for them to run uphill compared to large animals.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2016_MCQ_Q100 - Genotypic ratio of garden pea plant cross</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>A tall true breeding garden pea plant is crossed with a dwarf true breeding garden pea plant. When the \(F_{1}\) plants were selfed, the resulting genotypes were in the ratio of</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक लंबे तद्रूप प्रजननी उद्यान मटर के पौधे का एक बौने तद्रूप प्रजननी उद्यान मटर के पौधे से संकरण कराया गया। जब \(F_{1}\) पौधों का स्वपरागण कराया गया, तो प्राप्त जीनप्ररूपों (जीनोटाइप) का अनुपात था</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Parental generation consists of a homozygous tall true-breeding garden pea plant (\(TT\)) and a homozygous dwarf true-breeding garden pea plant (\(tt\)). These parents are crossed to produce the \(F_{1}\) generation, which is subsequently self-pollinated to generate the \(F_{2}\) progeny.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Genetic Principles</strong><br/>
Inheritance follows Mendel's law of segregation for a monohybrid cross, with stem height controlled by a single gene having alleles \(T\) (tall, dominant) and \(t\) (dwarf, recessive).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Principles and Working</strong><br/>
Crossing \(TT \times tt\) produces heterozygous \(F_{1}\) individuals of genotype \(Tt\).<br/>
Selfing the \(F_{1}\) generation (\(Tt \times Tt\)) yields gametes \(T\) and \(t\) in equal frequencies (\(1/2\) each).<br/>
The offspring in the \(F_{2}\) generation are:<br/>
\(1/4\) \(TT\) (tall homozygous),<br/>
\(2/4\) or \(1/2\) \(Tt\) (tall heterozygous),<br/>
\(1/4\) \(tt\) (dwarf homozygous).</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The genotypic ratio is \(1 : 2 : 1\); tall homozygous : tall heterozygous : dwarf.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आंकड़े और स्थितियां</strong><br/>
जनक पीढ़ी में एक समयुग्मजी लंबा शुद्ध प्रजननी उद्यान मटर का पौधा (\(TT\)) और एक समयुग्मजी बौना शुद्ध प्रजननी उद्यान मटर का पौधा (\(tt\)) शामिल हैं। इनका संकरण कराकर \(F_{1}\) पीढ़ी प्राप्त की जाती है, जिसका स्वपरागण कराकर \(F_{2}\) संतति प्राप्त की जाती है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: आनुवंशिक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
यह वंशागति मेंडल के विसंयोजन नियम पर आधारित एकसंकर संकरण है, जहाँ तने की लंबाई का नियंत्रण एक ही जीन के दो युग्मविकल्पियों \(T\) (लंबा, प्रभावी) और \(t\) (बौना, अप्रभावी) द्वारा होता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: चरणबद्ध आनुवंशिक गणना</strong><br/>
\(TT \times tt\) के संकरण से सभी \(F_{1}\) पौधे विषमयुग्मजी \(Tt\) प्राप्त होते हैं।<br/>
\(F_{1}\) पौधों के स्वपरागण (\(Tt \times Tt\)) से उत्पन्न होने वाले युग्मक \(T\) और \(t\) समान अनुपात (प्रत्येक \(1/2\)) में बनते हैं।<br/>
\(F_{2}\) संततियों का जीनप्ररूप अनुपात निम्न प्रकार प्राप्त होता है:<br/>
\(1/4\) \(TT\) (लंबा समयुग्मजी),<br/>
\(2/4\) \(Tt\) (लंबा विषमयुग्मजी),<br/>
\(1/4\) \(tt\) (बौना समयुग्मजी)।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: निष्कर्ष और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
प्राप्त जीनप्ररूपों का अनुपात \(1 : 2 : 1\); लंबा समयुग्मजी : लंबा विषमयुग्मजी : बौना है।</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2016_MCQ_Q102 - Characteristic genetic diversity feature of cropland</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>Which one of the following is a characteristic feature of cropland ecosystem ?</p>
<hr/>
<p>निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा एक कृषि-भूमि (फसल-क्षेत्र) पारितंत्र का एक अभिलाक्षणिक गुण है?</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
A cropland ecosystem is an anthropogenic (man-made) artificial ecosystem managed by humans for agricultural food and resource production.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of Characteristics</strong><br/>
Agricultural croplands typically practice monoculture, where a single selected crop variety or strain is cultivated over large geographic areas, resulting in the least genetic diversity compared to complex natural ecosystems. Weeds and soil organisms are actively present (despite management), and natural ecological succession is deliberately arrested by human intervention such as plowing and weeding.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Conclusion and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Least genetic diversity is a characteristic feature of a cropland ecosystem.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
कृषि-भूमि (फसल-क्षेत्र) पारितंत्र एक मानव-निर्मित (कृत्रिम) पारितंत्र है जिसे भोजन और अन्य संसाधनों के उत्पादन के लिए मनुष्यों द्वारा प्रबंधित किया जाता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: विशेषताओं का विश्लेषण</strong><br/>

<p>पादप पत्ती से जलवाष्प रंध्री छिद्र के माध्यम से बाहर निकलती है। प्रकाश संश्लेषण के दौरान उसी रंध्री छिद्र से कार्बन डाइऑक्साइड पादप में विसरित होती है। निम्नलिखित विकल्पों का उपयोग करते हुए उपरोक्त कथनों का कारण बताइए।</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Physical Principle of Diffusion</strong><br/>
The diffusion of any gas or vapour occurs independently of the diffusion of other gaseous molecules present in the same medium. Each molecular species moves according to its own partial pressure or concentration gradient, governed by Fick's first law of diffusion.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Application to Stomatal Gas Exchange</strong><br/>
During daytime when stomata are open, water vapour within the substomatal cavity has a higher vapor pressure than the ambient atmosphere, driving water vapour outward (transpiration). Simultaneously, photosynthetic carbon fixation inside mesophyll cells depletes internal \(\text{CO}_{2}\), causing atmospheric \(\text{CO}_{2}\) to diffuse inward down its own concentration gradient. Because water and carbon dioxide have different molecular weights and independent diffusion coefficients, both processes occur concurrently through the exact same stomatal pore without interference.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Conclusion and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Both processes can happen together because the diffusion coefficient of water and \(\text{CO}_{2}\) is different.</p>

  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>Which of the following is required as inducer(s) for the expression of <em>lac</em> operon ?</p>
<hr/>
<p><em>lac</em> प्रचालेक (ऑपेरॉन) की अभिव्यक्ति के लिए प्रेरक (इंड्यूसर) के रूप में निम्नलिखित में से किसकी आवश्यकता होती है?</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Gene Regulation</strong><br/>
The <em>lac</em> operon in <em>Escherichia coli</em> is an inducible operon system responsible for the transport and catabolism of lactose.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Molecular Mechanism of Induction</strong><br/>
In the absence of an inducer, the lac repressor protein encoded by the <em>i</em> gene binds to the operator locus, physically preventing RNA polymerase from transcribing the structural genes. When lactose is introduced into the growth medium, a small amount enters the bacterial cell and is converted to allolactose, which binds to the repressor protein. This binding triggers a conformational change that inactivates the repressor, releasing it from the operator and allowing transcription. Hence, lactose functions as the physiological inducer.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Conclusion and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Lactose is required as the inducer for the expression of the <em>lac</em> operon.</p>
<hr/>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
Various microorganisms are utilized in industrial biotechnology to yield bio-active molecules, enzymes, and organic acids for commercial and therapeutic purposes.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Microbe-Product Matches</strong><br/>
- <em>Monascus purpureus</em> (a yeast) produces statins, which act as competitive inhibitors of the enzyme responsible for cholesterol synthesis, functioning to lower blood cholesterol.<br/>
- <em>Streptococcus</em> produces the enzyme streptokinase, which is used clinically as a clot buster to dissolve blood clots in vessels.<br/>
- <em>Trichoderma polysporum</em> (a fungus) produces cyclosporin A, which serves as an effective immunosuppressive agent during organ transplantation.<br/>
- <em>Clostridium butylicum</em> is an anaerobic bacterium that produces butyric acid, not lipase. Lipases, used in detergent formulations to remove oily stains from laundry, are commercially obtained from organisms such as <em>Candida</em> and <em>Pseudomonas</em>.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Conclusion and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The match pairing <em>Clostridium butylicum</em> with lipase and removal of oil stains is incorrect.</p>
