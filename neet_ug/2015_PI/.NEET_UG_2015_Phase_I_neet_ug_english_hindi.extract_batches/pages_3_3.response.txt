<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q19 - Heat absorbed by gas along direct path AC</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>Figure below shows two paths that may be taken by a gas to go from a state A to a state C.</p>
<p><img src="@@PLUGINFILE@@/img-3.jpeg" alt="P-V diagram showing paths ABC and AC" /></p>
<p>In process AB, \(400\text{ J}\) of heat is added to the system and in process BC, \(100\text{ J}\) of heat is added to the system. The heat absorbed by the system in the process AC will be</p>
<hr/>
<p>नीचे दिया गया चित्र दो पथ दर्शाता है जिनके द्वारा किसी गैस को अवस्था A से अवस्था C तक ले जाया जा सकता है।</p>
<p>प्रक्रम AB में निकाय को \(400\text{ J}\) ऊष्मा दी जाती है तथा प्रक्रम BC में निकाय को \(100\text{ J}\) ऊष्मा दी जाती है। प्रक्रम AC में निकाय द्वारा अवशोषित ऊष्मा होगी</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
From the \(P\text{-}V\) graph:
<ul>
  <li>State A: \(P_A = 2 \times 10^4\text{ Pa}\), \(V_A = 2 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3\)</li>
  <li>State B: \(P_B = 6 \times 10^4\text{ Pa}\), \(V_B = 2 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3\)</li>
  <li>State C: \(P_C = 6 \times 10^4\text{ Pa}\), \(V_C = 4 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3\)</li>
  <li>Heat supplied in isochoric path AB: \(Q_{AB} = 400\text{ J}\)</li>
  <li>Heat supplied in isobaric path BC: \(Q_{BC} = 100\text{ J}\)</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
Internal energy \(U\) is a state function; hence, the change in internal energy \(\Delta U_{AC}\) is independent of the path. Work done by the system is the area under the \(P\text{-}V\) curve.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
First Law of Thermodynamics:
\[
\Delta Q = \Delta U + W
\]
For path AB (constant volume):
\[
W_{AB} = 0 \implies \Delta U_{AB} = Q_{AB} = 400\text{ J}
\]
For path BC (constant pressure):
\[
W_{BC} = P_B (V_C - V_B)
\]
For direct path AC (linear path):
\[
W_{AC} = \text{Area under trapezoid} = \frac{1}{2}(P_A + P_C)(V_C - V_A)
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Work done during path BC:
\[
W_{BC} = (6 \times 10^4\text{ Pa}) \times (4 \times 10^{-3} - 2 \times 10^{-3})\text{ m}^3 = 6 \times 10^4 \times 2 \times 10^{-3} = 120\text{ J}
\]
Change in internal energy during BC:
\[
\Delta U_{BC} = Q_{BC} - W_{BC} = 100\text{ J} - 120\text{ J} = -20\text{ J}
\]
Total change in internal energy from state A to state C:
\[
\Delta U_{AC} = \Delta U_{AB} + \Delta U_{BC} = 400\text{ J} + (-20\text{ J}) = 380\text{ J}
\]
Work done by the gas along path AC:
\[
W_{AC} = \frac{1}{2}\left(2 \times 10^4 + 6 \times 10^4\right)\text{ Pa} \times (4 \times 10^{-3} - 2 \times 10^{-3})\text{ m}^3 = \frac{1}{2}(8 \times 10^4)(2 \times 10^{-3}) = 80\text{ J}
\]
Heat absorbed in path AC:
\[
Q_{AC} = \Delta U_{AC} + W_{AC} = 380\text{ J} + 80\text{ J} = 460\text{ J}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The heat absorbed by the system along path AC is \(460\text{ J}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और शर्तें</strong><br/>
\(P\text{-}V\) आरेख से: \(P_A = 2 \times 10^4\text{ Pa}\), \(V_A = 2 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3\); \(P_B = 6 \times 10^4\text{ Pa}\), \(V_B = 2 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3\); \(P_C = 6 \times 10^4\text{ Pa}\), \(V_C = 4 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3\)। प्रक्रम AB में दी गई ऊष्मा \(Q_{AB} = 400\text{ J}\) तथा BC में \(Q_{BC} = 100\text{ J}\) है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: अभिधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>
आंतरिक ऊर्जा एक अवस्था फलन है, अतः \(\Delta U_{AC}\) पथ पर निर्भर नहीं करता।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
ऊष्मागतिकी का प्रथम नियम:
\[
\Delta Q = \Delta U + W
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
प्रक्रम AB में आयतन नियत है, अतः \(W_{AB} = 0 \implies \Delta U_{AB} = 400\text{ J}\)।<br/>
प्रक्रम BC में किया गया कार्य:
\[
W_{BC} = 6 \times 10^4 \times (4 - 2) \times 10^{-3} = 120\text{ J}
\]
\[
\Delta U_{BC} = Q_{BC} - W_{BC} = 100 - 120 = -20\text{ J}
\]
अवस्था A से C तक आंतरिक ऊर्जा में कुल परिवर्तन:
\[
\Delta U_{AC} = 400 + (-20) = 380\text{ J}
\]
प्रक्रम AC में किया गया कार्य समलंब के क्षेत्रफल के बराबर है:
\[
W_{AC} = \frac{1}{2}(2 \times 10^4 + 6 \times 10^4)(4 - 2) \times 10^{-3} = 80\text{ J}
\]
प्रक्रम AC में अवशोषित ऊष्मा:
\[
Q_{AC} = \Delta U_{AC} + W_{AC} = 380 + 80 = 460\text{ J}
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
अतः प्रक्रम AC में अवशोषित ऊष्मा \(460\text{ J}\) है।</p>
    ]]></text>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q20 - Heat absorbed by Carnot refrigerator</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>A Carnot engine, having an efficiency of \(\eta = \frac{1}{10}\) as heat engine, is used as a refrigerator. If the work done on the system is \(10\text{ J}\), the amount of energy absorbed from the reservoir at lower temperature is</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक कार्नो इंजन, जिसकी ऊष्मा इंजन के रूप में दक्षता \(\eta = \frac{1}{10}\) है, एक प्रशीतक (refrigerator) के रूप में प्रयुक्त किया जाता है। यदि निकाय पर किया गया कार्य \(10\text{ J}\) हो, तो निम्न ताप वाले कुंड से अवशोषित ऊर्जा की मात्रा है</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Carnot engine efficiency is \(\eta = \frac{1}{10}\). Work input to the refrigerator is \(W = 10\text{ J}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The cycle operates reversibly between two constant temperature reservoirs at absolute temperatures \(T_1\) (hot) and \(T_2\) (cold).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The relationship between efficiency \(\eta\) of a heat engine and coefficient of performance \(\beta\) of a refrigerator operating between the same two temperatures is:
\[
\beta = \frac{1 - \eta}{\eta}
\]
The coefficient of performance is also defined as:
\[
\beta = \frac{Q_2}{W}
\]
where \(Q_2\) is the heat extracted from the cold reservoir.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Calculate the coefficient of performance:
\[
\beta = \frac{1 - \frac{1}{10}}{\frac{1}{10}} = \frac{\frac{9}{10}}{\frac{1}{10}} = 9
\]
Using the definition of \(\beta\):
\[
Q_2 = \beta W = 9 \times 10\text{ J} = 90\text{ J}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Heat rejected to the hot reservoir is \(Q_1 = Q_2 + W = 90 + 10 = 100\text{ J}\), giving \(\eta = \frac{W}{Q_1} = \frac{10}{100} = \frac{1}{10}\), perfectly consistent. The energy absorbed from the cold reservoir is \(90\text{ J}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और शर्तें</strong><br/>
दक्षता \(\eta = \frac{1}{10}\), निकाय पर किया गया कार्य \(W = 10\text{ J}\) है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: अभिधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>
प्रशीतक उन्हीं दो कुंडों के बीच उत्क्रमणीय रूप से कार्य करता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
निष्पादन गुणांक \(\beta\) तथा दक्षता \(\eta\) में संबंध:
\[
\beta = \frac{1 - \eta}{\eta} = \frac{Q_2}{W}
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
\[
\beta = \frac{1 - 1/10}{1/10} = 9
\]
निम्न ताप के कुंड से अवशोषित ऊष्मा:
\[
Q_2 = \beta W = 9 \times 10 = 90\text{ J}
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
अतः अवशोषित ऊर्जा की मात्रा \(90\text{ J}\) है।</p>
    ]]></text>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q21 - Change in internal energy of diatomic gas</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>One mole of an ideal diatomic gas undergoes a transition from A to B along a path AB as shown in the figure.</p>
<p><img src="@@PLUGINFILE@@/img-4.jpeg" alt="P-V graph showing state transition from A to B" /></p>
<p>The change in internal energy of the gas during the transition is</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक मोल आदर्श द्विपरमाणुक गैस चित्र में दर्शाए अनुसार पथ AB के अनुदिश अवस्था A से B में परिवर्तित होती है।</p>
<p>संक्रमण के दौरान गैस की आंतरिक ऊर्जा में परिवर्तन है</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
<ul>
  <li>Amount of gas: \(n = 1\text{ mole}\) of ideal diatomic gas</li>
  <li>Initial state A: \(P_A = 5\text{ kPa} = 5 \times 10^3\text{ Pa}\), \(V_A = 4\text{ m}^3\)</li>
  <li>Final state B: \(P_B = 2\text{ kPa} = 2 \times 10^3\text{ Pa}\), \(V_B = 6\text{ m}^3\)</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
For an ideal diatomic gas with 5 degrees of freedom (translational and rotational), the molar heat capacity at constant volume is \(C_v = \frac{5}{2}R\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Change in internal energy of an ideal gas:
\[
\Delta U = n C_v \Delta T = n \left(\frac{5}{2}R\right)(T_B - T_A) = \frac{5}{2}\left(nRT_B - nRT_A\right)
\]
Using the ideal gas equation of state \(PV = nRT\):
\[
\Delta U = \frac{5}{2}\left(P_B V_B - P_A V_A\right)
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Evaluate the pressure-volume products:
\[
P_A V_A = (5 \times 10^3\text{ Pa}) \times 4\text{ m}^3 = 20 \times 10^3\text{ J} = 20\text{ kJ}
\]
\[
P_B V_B = (2 \times 10^3\text{ Pa}) \times 6\text{ m}^3 = 12 \times 10^3\text{ J} = 12\text{ kJ}
\]
Calculate \(\Delta U\):
\[
\Delta U = \frac{5}{2}\left(12\text{ kJ} - 20\text{ kJ}\right) = \frac{5}{2}\left(-8\text{ kJ}\right) = -20\text{ kJ}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Because the temperature decreases (\(P_B V_B < P_A V_A\)), the internal energy change is negative. The change in internal energy is \(-20\text{ kJ}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और शर्तें</strong><br/>
द्विपरमाणुक गैस के मोल \(n = 1\)। अवस्था A पर: \(P_A = 5\text{ kPa}\), \(V_A = 4\text{ m}^3\)। अवस्था B पर: \(P_B = 2\text{ kPa}\), \(V_B = 6\text{ m}^3\)।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: अभिधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>
द्विपरमाणुक गैस के लिए \(C_v = \frac{5}{2}R\) होता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
आंतरिक ऊर्जा में परिवर्तन:
\[
\Delta U = n C_v \Delta T = \frac{5}{2}(P_B V_B - P_A V_A)
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
\[
P_A V_A = 5 \times 10^3 \times 4 = 20\text{ kJ}
\]
\[
P_B V_B = 2 \times 10^3 \times 6 = 12\text{ kJ}
\]
\[
\Delta U = \frac{5}{2}(12 - 20)\text{ kJ} = -20\text{ kJ}
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
अतः आंतरिक ऊर्जा में परिवर्तन \(-20\text{ kJ}\) है।</p>
    ]]></text>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q22 - Specific heat ratio in terms of degrees of freedom</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>The ratio of the specific heats \(\frac{C_p}{C_v} = \gamma\) in terms of degrees of freedom (\(n\)) is given by</p>
<hr/>
<p>विशिष्ट ऊष्माओं का अनुपात \(\frac{C_p}{C_v} = \gamma\), स्वतंत्रता की कोटियों (\(n\)) के पदों में दिया जाता है</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
By the law of equipartition of energy, each degree of freedom of a gas molecule contributes \(\frac{1}{2}k_B T\) of energy. For one mole of an ideal gas having \(n\) degrees of freedom, the total internal energy is \(U = \frac{n}{2}RT\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Molar Specific Heat Capacities</strong><br/>
The molar specific heat capacity at constant volume is:
\[
C_v = \frac{dU}{dT} = \frac{n}{2}R
\]
By Mayer's relation (\(C_p - C_v = R\)), the molar heat capacity at constant pressure is:
\[
C_p = C_v + R = \frac{n}{2}R + R = \left(\frac{n}{2} + 1\right)R
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Ratio of Specific Heats</strong><br/>
Taking the ratio \(\gamma = \frac{C_p}{C_v}\):
\[
\gamma = \frac{\left(\frac{n}{2} + 1\right)R}{\frac{n}{2}R} = \frac{\frac{n}{2} + 1}{\frac{n}{2}} = 1 + \frac{1}{\frac{n}{2}} = 1 + \frac{2}{n}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The ratio of specific heats expressed in terms of degrees of freedom \(n\) is \(\left(1 + \frac{2}{n}\right)\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: सैद्धांतिक नियम</strong><br/>
ऊर्जा समविभाजन के नियम के अनुसार, \(n\) स्वतंत्रता की कोटि वाली एक मोल गैस की कुल आंतरिक ऊर्जा \(U = \frac{n}{2}RT\) होती है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मोलर विशिष्ट ऊष्मा धारिताएँ</strong><br/>
स्थिर आयतन पर मोलर विशिष्ट ऊष्मा:
\[
C_v = \frac{dU}{dT} = \frac{n}{2}R
\]
मेयर के सूत्र \(C_p = C_v + R\) से:
\[
C_p = \frac{n}{2}R + R = \left(\frac{n}{2} + 1\right)R
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: अनुपात की गणना</strong><br/>
\[
\gamma = \frac{C_p}{C_v} = \frac{\left(\frac{n}{2} + 1\right)R}{\frac{n}{2}R} = 1 + \frac{2}{n}
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q23 - Superposition of two perpendicular harmonic displacements</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>When two displacements represented by \(y_1 = a \sin(\omega t)\) and \(y_2 = b \cos(\omega t)\) are super-imposed, the motion is</p>
<hr/>
<p>जब \(y_1 = a \sin(\omega t)\) तथा \(y_2 = b \cos(\omega t)\) द्वारा निरूपित दो विस्थापनों को अध्यारोपित किया जाता है, तो गति होती है</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Two collinear displacements of identical angular frequency \(\omega\):
\[
y_1 = a \sin(\omega t), \quad y_2 = b \cos(\omega t)
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
Principle of superposition gives the resultant displacement \(y = y_1 + y_2\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Any linear combination of sinusoidal functions with the same frequency:
\[
y = a \sin(\omega t) + b \cos(\omega t)
\]
can be rewritten in canonical harmonic form \(y = A \sin(\omega t + \phi)\), where amplitude \(A = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\) and \(\tan \phi = \frac{b}{a}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Multiplying and dividing by \(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\):
\[
y = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} \left[ \frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}\sin(\omega t) + \frac{b}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}\cos(\omega t) \right]
\]
Setting \(\cos \phi = \frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}\) and \(\sin \phi = \frac{b}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}\):
\[
y = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} \sin(\omega t + \phi)
\]
This expression satisfies the differential equation of simple harmonic motion \(\frac{d^2 y}{dt^2} = -\omega^2 y\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The motion is simple harmonic with an amplitude of \(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और शर्तें</strong><br/>

समान आवृत्ति की दो आवर्ती गतियों का संयोजन:
\[
y = a \sin(\omega t) + b \cos(\omega t) = A \sin(\omega t + \phi)
\]
जहाँ परिणामी आयाम \(A = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\) होता है।</p>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q24 - Time period of SHM from position-velocity pairs</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>A particle is executing SHM along a straight line. Its velocities at distances \(x_1\) and \(x_2\) from the mean position are \(v_1\) and \(v_2\), respectively. Its time period is</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक कण एक सरल रेखा के अनुदिश सरल आवर्त गति कर रहा है। माध्य स्थिति से \(x_1\) तथा \(x_2\) दूरियों पर इसके वेग क्रमशः \(v_1\) तथा \(v_2\) हैं। इसका आवर्तकाल है</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
At displacement \(x_1\), speed is \(v_1\); at displacement \(x_2\), speed is \(v_2\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The motion is pure simple harmonic motion with amplitude \(A\) and angular frequency \(\omega\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Velocity as a function of displacement in SHM:
\[
v^2 = \omega^2 (A^2 - x^2)
\]
Time period:
\[
T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Setting up equations for both points:
\[
v_1^2 = \omega^2 (A^2 - x_1^2)
\]
\[
v_2^2 = \omega^2 (A^2 - x_2^2)
\]
Subtracting the second equation from the first:
\[
v_1^2 - v_2^2 = \omega^2\left[(A^2 - x_1^2) - (A^2 - x_2^2)\right] = \omega^2 (x_2^2 - x_1^2)
\]
Solving for \(\omega\):
\[
\omega^2 = \frac{v_1^2 - v_2^2}{x_2^2 - x_1^2} \implies \omega = \sqrt{\frac{v_1^2 - v_2^2}{x_2^2 - x_1^2}}
\]
Expressing the time period \(T\):
\[
T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{x_2^2 - x_1^2}{v_1^2 - v_2^2}}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The units under the square root are \(\text{m}^2 / (\text{m}^2/\text{s}^2) = \text{s}^2\), giving seconds as required. The time period is \(2\pi \sqrt{\frac{x_2^2 - x_1^2}{v_1^2 - v_2^2}}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और शर्तें</strong><br/>
दूरी \(x_1\) पर वेग \(v_1\) तथा दूरी \(x_2\) पर वेग \(v_2\) है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: अभिधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
सरल आवर्त गति में वेग:
\[
v^2 = \omega^2(A^2 - x^2)
\]
आवर्तकाल:
\[
T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
समीकरणों को घटाने पर:
\[
v_1^2 - v_2^2 = \omega^2 (x_2^2 - x_1^2) \implies \omega = \sqrt{\frac{v_1^2 - v_2^2}{x_2^2 - x_1^2}}
\]
आवर्तकाल:
\[
T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{x_2^2 - x_1^2}{v_1^2 - v_2^2}}
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
अतः आवर्तकाल \(2\pi \sqrt{\frac{x_2^2 - x_1^2}{v_1^2 - v_2^2}}\) है।</p>

  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>The fundamental frequency of a closed organ pipe of length \(20\text{ cm}\) is equal to the second overtone of an organ pipe open at both the ends. The length of organ pipe open at both the ends is</p>
<hr/>
<p>\(20\text{ cm}\) लम्बाई के एक बंद आर्गन पाइप की मूल आवृत्ति, दोनों सिरों पर खुले एक आर्गन पाइप के द्वितीय अधिस्वरक (second overtone) के बराबर है। दोनों सिरों पर खुले आर्गन पाइप की लम्बाई है</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Length of closed organ pipe: \(L_c = 20\text{ cm}\). Fundamental frequency of the closed pipe equals the second overtone of an open organ pipe of length \(L_o\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
End corrections are negligible, and the speed of sound \(v\) is identical in both pipes.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
<ul>
  <li>Fundamental frequency of a closed organ pipe:
  \[
  f_c = \frac{v}{4 L_c}
  \]</li>
  <li>Harmonics of an open organ pipe:
  \[
  f_n = n \left(\frac{v}{2 L_o}\right)
  \]
  where \(n = 1\) is fundamental, \(n = 2\) is 1st overtone, and \(n = 3\) is 2nd overtone:
  \[
  f_{o, 2\text{nd}} = \frac{3v}{2 L_o}
  \]</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Equating the two frequencies:
\[
\frac{v}{4 L_c} = \frac{3v}{2 L_o}
\]
Cancelling \(v\):
\[
\frac{1}{4 L_c} = \frac{3}{2 L_o} \implies 2 L_o = 12 L_c \implies L_o = 6 L_c
\]
Substituting \(L_c = 20\text{ cm}\):
\[
L_o = 6 \times 20\text{ cm} = 120\text{ cm}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Checking with values: \(f_c = \frac{v}{80}\) and \(f_o = \frac{3v}{240} = \frac{v}{80}\). Thus, the length of the open organ pipe is \(120\text{ cm}\).</p>

बंद पाइप की लम्बाई \(L_c = 20\text{ cm}\) है। बंद पाइप की मूल आवृत्ति खुले पाइप के द्वितीय अधिस्वरक के बराबर है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
बंद पाइप की मूल आवृत्ति: \(f_c = \frac{v}{4 L_c}\)<br/>
खुले पाइप का द्वितीय अधिस्वरक (तीसरा संनादी): \(f_o = \frac{3v}{2 L_o}\)</p>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q26 - Dielectric slab insertion into disconnected capacitor</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>A parallel plate air capacitor of capacitance \(C\) is connected to a cell of emf \(V\) and then disconnected from it. A dielectric slab of dielectric constant \(K\), which can just fill the air gap of the capacitor, is now inserted in it. Which of the following is incorrect?</p>
<hr/>
<p>धारिता \(C\) के एक समांतर पट्टिका वायु संधारित्र को \(V\) वि.वा.बल के एक सेल से जोड़ा जाता है और फिर इससे अलग कर दिया जाता है। परावैद्युतांक \(K\) की एक परावैद्युत पट्टिका, जो संधारित्र के वायु अंतराल को पूर्णतः भर सकती है, अब इसमें प्रविष्ट कराई जाती है। निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा कथन असत्य है?</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
When a capacitor is disconnected from its charging source, the electrical charge \(Q\) on the plates becomes isolated and remains strictly conserved:
\[
Q = CV = \text{constant}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Capacitance and Potential Difference</strong><br/>
Inserting a dielectric slab of dielectric constant \(K\) increases the capacitance to:
\[
C' = KC
\]
Since \(Q\) is constant, the new potential difference across the plates is:
\[
V' = \frac{Q}{C'} = \frac{Q}{KC} = \frac{V}{K}
\]
Thus, the potential difference decreases by a factor of \(K\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Evaluation of Energy Stored</strong><br/>
Initial energy stored:
\[
U_i = \frac{1}{2} C V^2 = \frac{Q^2}{2C}
\]
Final energy stored:
\[
U_f = \frac{Q^2}{2C'} = \frac{Q^2}{2KC} = \frac{U_i}{K}
\]
The energy stored decreases by a factor of \(K\). The change in energy stored is:
\[
\Delta U = U_f - U_i = \frac{U_i}{K} - U_i = \frac{1}{2} C V^2 \left(\frac{1}{K} - 1\right)
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Identification of the Incorrect Statement</strong><br/>
Because the plates are electrically isolated, the charge on the capacitor must remain strictly conserved. Therefore, stating that the charge on the capacitor is not conserved is factually incorrect.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The incorrect statement among the choices is that the charge on the capacitor is not conserved.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: सैद्धांतिक नियम</strong><br/>
जब संधारित्र को सेल से विच्छेदित कर दिया जाता है, तो पट्टिकाओं पर आवेश \(Q = CV\) विलगित हो जाता है और नियत (संरक्षित) रहता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: धारिता और विभवांतर</strong><br/>
परावैद्युत पट्टिका डालने पर नई धारिता \(C' = KC\) हो जाती है। विभवांतर \(V' = \frac{Q}{KC} = \frac{V}{K}\) होकर \(K\) गुना कम हो जाता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संचित ऊर्जा</strong><br/>
प्रारंभिक ऊर्जा: \(U_i = \frac{1}{2}CV^2\)<br/>
अंतिम ऊर्जा: \(U_f = \frac{U_i}{K}\)<br/>
ऊर्जा में परिवर्तन: \(\Delta U = \frac{1}{2}CV^2\left(\frac{1}{K} - 1\right)\)</p>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q27 - Enclosed charge in radial electric field using Gauss law</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>The electric field in a certain region is acting radially outward and is given by \(E = Ar\). A charge contained in a sphere of radius &#39;\(a\)&#39; centred at the origin of the field will be given by</p>
<hr/>
<p>किसी क्षेत्र में विद्युत क्षेत्र त्रिज्यीय रूप से बाहर की ओर दिष्ट है तथा \(E = Ar\) द्वारा दिया गया है। इस क्षेत्र के मूल बिंदु पर केंद्रित त्रिज्या &#39;\(a\)&#39; के एक गोले में परिबद्ध आवेश होगा</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Electric field is radially outward with magnitude \(E(r) = Ar\), where \(A\) is a constant and \(r\) is the radial distance. A spherical Gaussian surface of radius \(a\) is centered at the origin.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
Spherical symmetry applies. The electric field vector \(\vec{E}\) is collinear and parallel to the outward surface area normal vector \(d\vec{A}\) at every point on the sphere.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Gauss's Law of Electrostatics:
\[
\Phi_E = \oint \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{A} = \frac{q_{\text{enclosed}}}{\varepsilon_0}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
At radius \(r = a\), the magnitude of the field is \(E = Aa\).<br/>
Total surface area of the sphere of radius \(a\) is \(4\pi a^2\).<br/>
The total electric flux through the sphere is:
\[
\Phi_E = E \cdot (4\pi a^2) = (Aa) \cdot (4\pi a^2) = 4\pi A a^3
\]
Applying Gauss's law to solve for the enclosed charge:
\[
q_{\text{enclosed}} = \varepsilon_0 \Phi_E = 4\pi \varepsilon_0 A a^3
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Alternatively, using the differential form \(\nabla \cdot \vec{E} = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}\): in spherical coordinates, \(\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}(r^2 E) = \frac{1}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}(A r^3) = 3A\), giving uniform charge density \(\rho = 3\varepsilon_0 A\). Total charge is \(\rho \cdot \left(\frac{4}{3}\pi a^3\right) = 4\pi \varepsilon_0 A a^3\). Thus, the enclosed charge is \(4\pi \varepsilon_0 A a^3\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और शर्तें</strong><br/>
विद्युत क्षेत्र त्रिज्यीय दिशा में \(E = Ar\) है। गोले की त्रिज्या \(a\) है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: अभिधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>
गोलीय सममिति के कारण पृष्ठ के प्रत्येक बिंदु पर विद्युत क्षेत्र सदिश तथा क्षेत्रफल सदिश समांतर हैं।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
गाउस का नियम:
\[
\oint \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{A} = \frac{q}{\varepsilon_0} \implies q = \varepsilon_0 \Phi_E
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
त्रिज्या \(a\) के गोलीय पृष्ठ पर विद्युत क्षेत्र \(E = Aa\) है।<br/>
कुल विद्युत फ्लक्स:
\[
\Phi_E = E(4\pi a^2) = (Aa)(4\pi a^2) = 4\pi A a^3
\]
परिबद्ध आवेश:
\[
q = 4\pi \varepsilon_0 A a^3
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
अतः गोले में परिबद्ध आवेश \(4\pi \varepsilon_0 A a^3\) है।</p>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q28 - Series resistance for desired potentiometer potential gradient</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>A potentiometer wire has length \(4\text{ m}\) and resistance \(8\ \Omega\). The resistance that must be connected in series with the wire and an accumulator of emf \(2\text{ V}\), so as to get a potential gradient \(1\text{ mV per cm}\) on the wire is</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक विभवमापी तार की लम्बाई \(4\text{ m}\) तथा प्रतिरोध \(8\ \Omega\) है। इस तार तथा \(2\text{ V}\) वि.वा.बल के संचायक सेल के साथ श्रेणीक्रम में जोड़े जाने वाले प्रतिरोध का मान, जिससे तार पर विभव प्रवणता \(1\text{ mV per cm}\) प्राप्त हो सके, होगा</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
<ul>
  <li>Length of potentiometer wire: \(L = 4\text{ m} = 400\text{ cm}\)</li>
  <li>Resistance of wire: \(R_{\text{wire}} = 8\ \Omega\)</li>
  <li>Emf of accumulator: \(E = 2\text{ V}\) (internal resistance is negligible)</li>
  <li>Required potential gradient: \(k = 1\text{ mV/cm} = 10^{-3}\text{ V/cm} = 0.1\text{ V/m}\)</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The driver circuit forms a single series loop with total resistance \(R_{\text{total}} = R_{\text{wire}} + R_s\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Potential difference across the potentiometer wire:
\[
V_{\text{wire}} = k \cdot L
\]
Ohm's law for the wire current:
\[
I = \frac{V_{\text{wire}}}{R_{\text{wire}}}
\]
Total resistance in the series circuit:
\[
R_{\text{total}} = \frac{E}{I} \implies R_s = R_{\text{total}} - R_{\text{wire}}
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Potential difference across the wire of length \(400\text{ cm}\):
\[
V_{\text{wire}} = (1\text{ mV/cm}) \times 400\text{ cm} = 400\text{ mV} = 0.4\text{ V}
\]
Current required in the wire:
\[
I = \frac{V_{\text{wire}}}{R_{\text{wire}}} = \frac{0.4\text{ V}}{8\ \Omega} = 0.05\text{ A}
\]
Total resistance required in the circuit:
\[
R_{\text{total}} = \frac{E}{I} = \frac{2\text{ V}}{0.05\text{ A}} = 40\ \Omega
\]
The series resistance \(R_s\) is:
\[
R_s = R_{\text{total}} - R_{\text{wire}} = 40\ \Omega - 8\ \Omega = 32\ \Omega
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
With \(R_s = 32\ \Omega\), \(I = \frac{2}{32 + 8} = 0.05\text{ A}\). The potential drop is \(V_{\text{wire}} = 0.05 \times 8 = 0.4\text{ V}\), yielding a gradient of \(\frac{0.4\text{ V}}{4\text{ m}} = 0.1\text{ V/m} = 1\text{ mV/cm}\). Thus, the required series resistance is \(32\ \Omega\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और शर्तें</strong><br/>
तार की लम्बाई \(L = 4\text{ m} = 400\text{ cm}\), तार का प्रतिरोध \(R_{\text{wire}} = 8\ \Omega\), सेल का वि.वा.बल \(E = 2\text{ V}\), विभव प्रवणता \(k = 1\text{ mV/cm} = 0.1\text{ V/m}\)।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: अभिधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>
परिपथ में तार तथा बाह्य प्रतिरोध \(R_s\) श्रेणीक्रम में जुड़े हैं।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
तार पर कुल विभवांतर: \(V = k L\)<br/>
धारा: \(I = \frac{V}{R_{\text{wire}}} = \frac{E}{R_{\text{wire}} + R_s}\)</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
तार पर विभवांतर:
\[
V = 1\text{ mV/cm} \times 400\text{ cm} = 0.4\text{ V}
\]
आवश्यक धारा:
\[
I = \frac{0.4}{8} = 0.05\text{ A}
\]
कुल प्रतिरोध:
\[
R_{\text{total}} = \frac{2}{0.05} = 40\ \Omega
\]
श्रेणी प्रतिरोध:
\[
R_s = 40 - 8 = 32\ \Omega
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
अतः श्रेणीक्रम में जोड़े जाने वाले प्रतिरोध का मान \(32\ \Omega\) है।</p>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q29 - Comparison of voltmeter readings in series-parallel combination</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>A, B and C are voltmeters of resistance \(R\), \(1.5 R\) and \(3R\) respectively as shown in the figure. When some potential difference is applied between X and Y, the voltmeter readings are \(V_A\), \(V_B\) and \(V_C\) respectively. Then,</p>
<p><img src="@@PLUGINFILE@@/img-5.jpeg" alt="Circuit with voltmeter A in series with parallel combination of voltmeters B and C" /></p>
<hr/>
<p>चित्र में दर्शाए अनुसार A, B तथा C क्रमशः \(R\), \(1.5 R\) तथा \(3R\) प्रतिरोध के वोल्टमीटर हैं। जब X तथा Y के बीच कुछ विभवांतर लगाया जाता है, तो वोल्टमीटरों के पाठ्यांक क्रमशः \(V_A\), \(V_B\) तथा \(V_C\) हैं। तब,</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Resistance of voltmeter A is \(R_A = R\). Resistance of voltmeter B is \(R_B = 1.5R\). Resistance of voltmeter C is \(R_C = 3R\). Voltmeters B and C are connected in parallel with each other, and this combination is in series with voltmeter A across terminals X and Y.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The readings of voltmeters correspond to the potential difference across their respective terminals.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Parallel combination of resistances:
\[
R_{BC} = \frac{R_B \cdot R_C}{R_B + R_C}
\]
Since B and C are connected across the exact same two parallel nodes:
\[
V_B = V_C
\]
Voltage division in series: the potential difference across components in series is directly proportional to their equivalent resistances.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Calculate equivalent resistance of the parallel branch (B and C):
\[
R_{BC} = \frac{(1.5 R)(3 R)}{1.5 R + 3 R} = \frac{4.5 R^2}{4.5 R} = R
\]
Notice that:
\[
R_A = R_{BC} = R
\]
Because voltmeter A and the parallel combination (B and C) have equal resistance and carry the same total current, the potential difference across voltmeter A equals the potential difference across the parallel combination:
\[
V_A = V_{BC}
\]
Since \(V_B = V_C = V_{BC}\), it directly follows that:
\[
V_A = V_B = V_C
\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
All three voltmeters record identical voltage readings. Thus, \(V_A = V_B = V_C\).</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: अभिधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
समांतर संयोजन का तुल्य प्रतिरोध:
\[
R_{BC} = \frac{R_B R_C}{R_B + R_C}
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
B तथा C का तुल्य प्रतिरोध:
\[
R_{BC} = \frac{1.5R \times 3R}{1.5R + 3R} = \frac{4.5R^2}{4.5R} = R
\]
चूँकि \(R_A = R_{BC} = R\) है, अतः समान धारा प्रवाहित होने पर A का विभवांतर B और C के संयुक्त विभवांतर के बराबर होगा:
\[
V_A = V_{BC}
\]
समांतर क्रम में होने से \(V_B = V_C = V_{BC}\) होगा।<br/>
अतः:
\[
V_A = V_B = V_C
\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
तीनों वोल्टमीटरों के पाठ्यांक समान होंगे: \(V_A = V_B = V_C\)।</p>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q30 - Constant physical quantity in non-uniform metallic conductor</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>Across a metallic conductor of non-uniform cross-section, a constant potential difference is applied. The quantity which remains constant along the conductor is</p>
<hr/>
<p>असमान अनुप्रस्थ काट वाले एक धात्विक चालक के सिरों पर एक नियत विभवांतर लगाया जाता है। चालक के अनुदिश नियत रहने वाली राशि है</p>
    ]]></text>

  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
In a steady electric state, charge does not accumulate at any section of a current-carrying conductor. By the law of conservation of electric charge, the rate of charge entering any cross-section must precisely equal the rate of charge leaving it.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Physical Quantities Along the Conductor</strong><br/>
Let \(A(x)\) represent the varying cross-sectional area along the length of the conductor:
<ul>
  <li>Electric Current \(I\): In the steady state, the total current \(I\) passing through every cross-section is invariant.</li>
  <li>Current Density \(j\): Defined as \(j = \frac{I}{A(x)}\). Since area \(A(x)\) varies, \(j\) varies inversely with area.</li>
  <li>Electric Field \(E\): By Ohm's law in microscopic form (\(j = \sigma E\)), \(E = \frac{j}{\sigma} = \frac{I}{\sigma A(x)}\), which varies with area.</li>
  <li>Drift Velocity \(v_d\): Given by \(v_d = \frac{I}{n e A(x)}\), which also varies inversely with area.</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Among all the given physical quantities, only the electric current remains constant throughout the length of the conductor.</p>

<p><strong>चरण 1: सैद्धांतिक नियम</strong><br/>
स्थायी अवस्था में आवेश संरक्षण के नियम के अनुसार, चालक के किसी भी अनुप्रस्थ काट से प्रति सेकंड प्रवाहित होने वाले आवेश की मात्रा नियत रहती है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: विभिन्न भौतिक राशियों का विश्लेषण</strong><br/>
चालक के बदलते अनुप्रस्थ काट क्षेत्रफल \(A\) के लिए:
<ul>
  <li>विद्युत धारा \(I\): स्थायी अवस्था में प्रत्येक अनुप्रस्थ काट पर धारा का मान नियत रहता है।</li>
  <li>धारा घनत्व \(j = \frac{I}{A}\): क्षेत्रफल बदलने पर परिवर्तित होता है।</li>
  <li>विद्युत क्षेत्र \(E = \frac{j}{\sigma}\): अनुप्रस्थ काट के साथ परिवर्तित होता है।</li>
  <li>अपवाह वेग \(v_d = \frac{I}{neA}\): क्षेत्रफल के व्युत्क्रमानुपाती होने से बदलता है।</li>
</ul></p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
अतः चालक के अनुदिश केवल विद्युत धारा (current) ही नियत रहती है।</p>
