<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2015_MCQ_Q146 - Chromosome number and DNA content after S phase</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>A somatic cell that has just completed the S-phase of its cell cycle, as compared to gamete of the same species has</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक कायिक कोशिका जिसने अपने कोशिका चक्र की S-प्रावस्था अभी-अभी पूरी की है, उसी जाति के युग्मक की तुलना में रखती है</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>Let the chromosome number and DNA content of a haploid gamete of a diploid species be represented as \(n\) and \(C\), respectively.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Cellular Model and DNA Replication</strong><br/>A normal somatic cell is diploid with chromosome number \(2n\) and DNA content \(2C\) in the \(G_1\) phase.<br/>During the synthesis (S) phase, DNA replication doubles the total quantity of DNA to \(4C\), while the total number of chromosomes remains unchanged at \(2n\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Comparison and Ratio Determination</strong><br/>Comparing the post-S-phase somatic cell (\(2n\) chromosomes, \(4C\) DNA) with the gamete (\(n\) chromosomes, \(C\) DNA):<br/>- Ratio of chromosomes: \(\frac{2n}{n} = 2\) (twice the number of chromosomes).<br/>- Ratio of DNA: \(\frac{4C}{C} = 4\) (four times the amount of DNA).</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The cell has twice the number of chromosomes and four times the amount of DNA compared to the gamete.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए मान एवं शर्तें</strong><br/>मान लीजिए किसी द्विगुणित जाति के एक अगुणित युग्मक में गुणसूत्रों की संख्या \(n\) तथा DNA की मात्रा \(C\) है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: कोशिका चक्र एवं DNA प्रतिकृतियन</strong><br/>एक सामान्य कायिक कोशिका द्विगुणित होती है, जिसमें \(G_1\) प्रावस्था में गुणसूत्र संख्या \(2n\) तथा DNA की मात्रा \(2C\) होती है।<br/>S-प्रावस्था में DNA प्रतिकृतियन के कारण DNA की मात्रा दोगुनी होकर \(4C\) हो जाती है, किंतु गुणसूत्र संख्या \(2n\) ही बनी रहती है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: तुलना एवं अनुपात निर्धारण</strong><br/>S-प्रावस्था पूरी कर चुकी कायिक कोशिका (\(2n, 4C\)) की तुलना युग्मक (\(n, C\)) से करने पर:<br/>- गुणसूत्र संख्या: \(\frac{2n}{n} = 2\) (दोगुनी गुणसूत्र संख्या)।<br/>- DNA मात्रा: \(\frac{4C}{C} = 4\) (चार गुनी DNA मात्रा)।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>कायिक कोशिका में उसी जाति के युग्मक की तुलना में गुणसूत्रों की संख्या दोगुनी तथा DNA की मात्रा चार गुनी होती है।</p>]]></text>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Brunner's glands (submucosal glands) are compound branched tubular glands found specifically in the submucosa of the duodenum (small intestine), not in the stomach. Pepsinogen is secreted by chief (peptic) cells located in the mucosa of the stomach.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Valid Statements</strong><br/>- Goblet cells are mucus-secreting cells present throughout the intestinal mucosa.<br/>- Oxyntic (parietal) cells reside within the gastric glands of the stomach mucosa and secrete hydrochloric acid (\(\text{HCl}\)) and intrinsic factor.<br/>- Pancreatic acini are exocrine secretory clusters that secrete digestive enzymes, including procarboxypeptidase.</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Gastric juice secreted by the gastric mucosa of human infants contains specific enzymes adapted for digesting milk:<br/>- Pepsinogen: The zymogen precursor to pepsin, which hydrolyzes proteins into peptones and proteoses.<br/>- Rennin (prorennin): A proteolytic enzyme found specifically in infants that curdles milk protein (casein) in the presence of calcium ions, slowing its passage to ensure adequate protein digestion.<br/>- Gastric lipase: Secreted in small quantities to begin hydrolyzing emulsified milk fats.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of Non-Gastric Enzymes</strong><br/>Maltase is an intestinal disaccharidase; nucleases and amylases are secreted by the salivary glands and pancreas, not present in gastric secretions.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The gastric juice of infants contains pepsinogen, lipase, and rennin.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>शिशुओं के जठर रस में दुग्ध पाचन के लिए विशिष्ट पाचक एंजाइम होते हैं:<br/>- पेप्सिनोजन: निष्क्रिय प्रोएंजाइम जो पेप्सिन में परिवर्तित होकर प्रोटीन का पाचन करता है।<br/>- रेनिन: यह विशेष रूप से शिशुओं में पाया जाने वाला प्रोटियोलिटिक एंजाइम है जो दूध की प्रोटीन (केसीन) को जमाने (दही बनाने) में सहायता करता है।<br/>- जठर लाइपेज: दुग्ध वसा के आंशिक जल-अपघटन के लिए अल्प मात्रा में स्रावित होता है।</p>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2015_MCQ_Q158 - Hormonal and chemical regulation of diuresis</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Which of the following does not favour the formation of large quantities of dilute urine ?</p>
<hr/>
<p>निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा बड़ी मात्रा में तनु मूत्र बनने का समर्थन नहीं करता है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The excretion of large quantities of dilute urine (diuresis) is promoted by agents that suppress antidiuretic mechanisms or inhibit tubular water and solute reabsorption. Conversely, agents that stimulate water reabsorption conserve fluid and yield concentrated urine.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Options</strong><br/>- Alcohol: Inhibits the release of vasopressin (ADH) from the posterior pituitary, blocking aquaporin-mediated water reabsorption in the collecting ducts and producing copious dilute urine.<br/>- Caffeine: Reduces renal tubular sodium and water reabsorption, acting as a diuretic.<br/>- Atrial-natriuretic factor (ANF): Secreted by atrial myocytes to lower blood pressure, it antagonizes the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone axis and promotes natriuresis and diuresis.<br/>- Renin: An enzyme secreted by juxtaglomerular cells in response to reduced renal perfusion. Renin converts angiotensinogen to angiotensin I, leading to angiotensin II generation, which stimulates aldosterone and ADH release. This stimulates intense \(Na^+\) and water reabsorption in distal tubules, opposing diuresis and concentrating the urine.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Renin opposes water loss and does not favour the formation of large quantities of dilute urine.</p>
<hr/>

  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Freshly ejaculated mammalian spermatozoa are infertile and cannot penetrate an oocyte until they undergo final physiological maturation within the secretions of the female reproductive tract (uterus and fallopian tubes). This prerequisite maturation process is termed capacitation.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Biochemical Alterations in Sperm</strong><br/>During capacitation, which occurs over several hours prior to fertilization:<br/>- Decapacitation factors, surface seminal glycoproteins, and cholesterol are removed from the sperm plasma membrane.<br/>- The fluidity and permeability of the acrosomal membrane increase, permitting rapid influx of calcium ions.<br/>- Whiplash motility (hyperactivation) is initiated, enabling the sperm to traverse the cumulus cells and undergo the acrosome reaction upon contact with the zona pellucida.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Capacitation refers specifically to physiological changes occurring in the sperm before fertilisation.</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p>Which of these is not an important component of initiation of parturition in humans ?</p>
<hr/>
<p>मनुष्यों में प्रसव (पार्चुरिशन) के आरंभ होने में निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा एक महत्वपूर्ण घटक नहीं है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Parturition is induced by a complex neuroendocrine mechanism initiated by signals from the fully mature fetus and the placenta (the fetal ejection reflex).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of Parturition Stimulants</strong><br/>- Increased Estrogen/Progesterone Ratio: Near term, a relative rise in maternal estrogen compared to progesterone enhances myometrial excitability, increases gap junctions, and upregulates oxytocin receptors in the uterine wall.<br/>- Release of Oxytocin: Oxytocin released from the maternal posterior pituitary directly triggers vigorous uterine contractions.<br/>- Synthesis of Prostaglandins: Uterine tissue and fetal membranes locally produce prostaglandins, which work synergistically with oxytocin to induce intense uterine contractions and cervical softening.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Role of Prolactin</strong><br/>Prolactin is an anterior pituitary hormone that stimulates milk production (lactogenesis) within the mammary alveoli after childbirth. It does not initiate or stimulate myometrial labor contractions during the onset of parturition.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The release of prolactin is not an important component of the initiation of parturition.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>मानव में प्रसव का आरंभ एक जटिल तंत्रिका-अंतःस्रावी प्रक्रिया (गर्भ-उत्क्षेपण प्रतिवर्त) द्वारा होता है, जो पूर्ण विकसित गर्भ और अपरा से शुरू होती है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: प्रसव आरंभ करने वाले कारक</strong><br/>- एस्ट्रोजन और प्रोजेस्टेरोन अनुपात में वृद्धि: यह गर्भाशय पेशी में संकुचनशीलता तथा ऑक्सीटोसिन ग्राही की संख्या बढ़ाती है।<br/>- ऑक्सीटोसिन का स्राव: पश्च पीयूष ग्रंथि से स्रावित ऑक्सीटोसिन गर्भाशय में तीव्र संकुचन उत्पन्न करता है।<br/>- प्रोस्टाग्लैंडिंस का संश्लेषण: यह ऑक्सीटोसिन के साथ मिलकर संकुचन को तीव्र करता है।</p>
