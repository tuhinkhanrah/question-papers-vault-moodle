  <name>
    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2013_Q47 - Maximum electrons for quantum number set</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>What is the maximum numbers of electrons that can be associated with the following set of quantum numbers?</p>
<p>\[ n = 3, l = 1 \text{ and } m = -1 \]</p>
<hr/>
<p>क्वांटम संख्याओं के निम्नलिखित समुच्चय से संबंधित इलेक्ट्रॉनों की अधिकतम संख्या क्या हो सकती है?</p>
<p>\[ n = 3, l = 1 \text{ तथा } m = -1 \]</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Principal quantum number: \(n = 3\).<br/>
Azimuthal (orbital angular momentum) quantum number: \(l = 1\).<br/>
Magnetic quantum number: \(m = -1\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Physical Concepts</strong><br/>
According to the quantum mechanical model of the atom and the Pauli exclusion principle, a unique combination of \(n\), \(l\), and \(m\) specifies one particular atomic orbital in three-dimensional space.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
1. The quantum numbers \(n = 3\) and \(l = 1\) designate the \(3p\) subshell.<br/>
2. A specific value of the magnetic quantum number \(m\) (here \(m = -1\)) defines a single orbital within that subshell (e.g., \(3p_x\) or \(3p_y\)).<br/>
3. By the Pauli exclusion principle, an individual orbital can hold a maximum of 2 electrons with opposite spin orientations (\(s = +\frac{1}{2}\) and \(s = -\frac{1}{2}\)).</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Because all three spatial quantum numbers \((n, l, m)\) are fixed, they uniquely identify exactly one orbital. The maximum electron capacity of any single orbital is 2 electrons.</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
If only \(n = 3\) were given, the maximum capacity would be \(2n^2 = 18\); if \(n = 3, l = 1\) were given, the capacity would be \(2(2l+1) = 6\). Specifying \(m\) restricts the state to a single orbital of capacity 2.<br/>
The maximum number of electrons is 2.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ (Given Data and Conditions)</strong><br/>
मुख्य क्वांटम संख्या: \(n = 3\)।<br/>
द्विगंशी क्वांटम संख्या: \(l = 1\)।<br/>
चुंबकीय क्वांटम संख्या: \(m = -1\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और भौतिक सिद्धांत (Assumptions and Physical Concepts)</strong><br/>
तीन क्वांटम संख्याओं \((n, l, m)\) का एक निश्चित समुच्चय अंतरिक्ष में एक विशिष्ट कक्षक (orbital) को निर्दिष्ट करता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत (Relevant Formulae and Principles)</strong><br/>
\(n = 3, l = 1\) से \(3p\) उपकोश प्राप्त होता है। \(m = -1\) इस उपकोश के केवल एक विशिष्ट कक्षक को निरूपित करता है। पॉली के अपवर्जन नियम के अनुसार किसी भी एक कक्षक में विपरीत चक्रण वाले अधिकतम 2 इलेक्ट्रॉन रह सकते हैं।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल (Step-by-Step Working)</strong><br/>
चूँकि दिए गए तीनों मान एक कक्षक का निर्धारण करते हैं, अतः इसमें अधिकतम 2 इलेक्ट्रॉन ही समाहित हो सकते हैं।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर (Check and Final Answer)</strong><br/>
एक कक्षक की अधिकतम क्षमता 2 इलेक्ट्रॉन होती है।<br/>
अतः संबंधित इलेक्ट्रॉनों की अधिकतम संख्या 2 है।</p>
    ]]></text>

  <name>
    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2013_Q48 - Activation energy from rate doubling with temperature</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>What is the activation energy for a reaction if its rate doubles when the temperature is raised from \(20^{\circ}\text{C}\) to \(35^{\circ}\text{C}\)? (\(R = 8.314\text{ J mol}^{-1}\text{ K}^{-1}\))</p>
<hr/>
<p>किसी अभिक्रिया के लिए सक्रियण ऊर्जा क्या होगी यदि ताप \(20^{\circ}\text{C}\) से \(35^{\circ}\text{C}\) तक बढ़ाने पर उसकी दर दोगुनी हो जाती है? (\(R = 8.314\text{ J mol}^{-1}\text{ K}^{-1}\))</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Initial temperature: \(T_1 = 20^{\circ}\text{C} = 20 + 273.15 = 293.15\text{ K} \approx 293\text{ K}\).<br/>
Final temperature: \(T_2 = 35^{\circ}\text{C} = 35 + 273.15 = 308.15\text{ K} \approx 308\text{ K}\).<br/>
Ratio of rate constants: \(\frac{k_2}{k_1} = 2\).<br/>
Universal gas constant: \(R = 8.314\text{ J mol}^{-1}\text{ K}^{-1}\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The temperature dependence of the rate constant obeys the Arrhenius equation with temperature-independent activation energy \(E_a\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The integrated Arrhenius equation in logarithmic form is:
\[ \log_{10}\left(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right) = \frac{E_a}{2.303 R}\left(\frac{T_2 - T_1}{T_1 T_2}\right) \]
Solving for \(E_a\):
\[ E_a = \frac{2.303 R \cdot T_1 T_2 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right)}{T_2 - T_1} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Substitute the values into the equation:
\[ \Delta T = 308 - 293 = 15\text{ K} \]
\[ T_1 T_2 = 293 \times 308 = 90244\text{ K}^2 \]
\[ \log_{10}(2) \approx 0.3010 \]
\[ E_a = \frac{2.303 \times 8.314 \times 90244 \times 0.3010}{15} \]
\[ E_a = \frac{19.147 \times 90244 \times 0.3010}{15} \]
\[ E_a \approx \frac{520117}{15} \approx 34674\text{ J mol}^{-1} \approx 34.7\text{ kJ mol}^{-1} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Typical chemical reactions with a temperature coefficient of \(\approx 2\) around room temperature exhibit an activation energy between \(30\) and \(50\text{ kJ/mol}\).<br/>
The activation energy is \(34.7\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ (Given Data and Conditions)</strong><br/>
\(T_1 = 20 + 273 = 293\text{ K}\), \(T_2 = 35 + 273 = 308\text{ K}\)।<br/>
दर स्थिरांक का अनुपात: \(\frac{k_2}{k_1} = 2\)।<br/>
गैस नियतांक: \(R = 8.314\text{ J mol}^{-1}\text{ K}^{-1}\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी (Assumptions and Sign Conventions)</strong><br/>
अभिक्रिया आरहेनियस समीकरण का पालन करती है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत (Relevant Formulae and Principles)</strong><br/>
आरहेनियस समीकरण:
\[ \log_{10}\left(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right) = \frac{E_a}{2.303 R}\left(\frac{T_2 - T_1}{T_1 T_2}\right) \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल (Step-by-Step Working)</strong><br/>
मान प्रतिस्थापित करने पर:
\[ 0.3010 = \frac{E_a}{2.303 \times 8.314}\left(\frac{15}{293 \times 308}\right) \]
\[ E_a = \frac{0.3010 \times 19.147 \times 90244}{15} \approx 34674\text{ J mol}^{-1} = 34.7\text{ kJ mol}^{-1} \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर (Check and Final Answer)</strong><br/>
गणना की पुष्टि होती है।<br/>
अतः सक्रियण ऊर्जा \(34.7\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}\) है।</p>
    ]]></text>

  <name>
    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2013_Q49 - Oxidation potential of hydrogen electrode at pH 10</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>A hydrogen gas electrode is made by dipping platinum wire in a solution of HCl of \(\text{pH} = 10\) and by passing hydrogen gas around the platinum wire at 1 atm pressure. The oxidation potential of electrode would be</p>
<hr/>
<p>\(\text{pH} = 10\) के HCl विलयन में प्लैटिनम तार को डुबोकर तथा उसके चारों ओर 1 atm दाब पर हाइड्रोजन गैस प्रवाहित करके एक हाइड्रोजन गैस इलेक्ट्रोड बनाया जाता है। इलेक्ट्रोड का ऑक्सीकरण विभव होगा:</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
\(\text{pH}\) of HCl solution \(= 10\).<br/>
Pressure of \(\text{H}_2\) gas: \(P_{\text{H}_2} = 1\text{ atm}\).<br/>
Standard hydrogen electrode potential: \(E^\circ = 0.00\text{ V}\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The electrode reaction is considered in the oxidation direction:
\[ \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2(g) \longrightarrow \text{H}^+(aq) + e^- \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The Nernst equation for the oxidation half-cell reaction at \(298\text{ K}\) is:
\[ E_{\text{ox}} = E^\circ_{\text{ox}} - \frac{0.0591}{n}\log_{10}\left(\frac{[\text{H}^+]}{P_{\text{H}_2}^{1/2}}\right) \]
Since \(n = 1\), \(E^\circ_{\text{ox}} = 0\text{ V}\), and \(P_{\text{H}_2} = 1\text{ atm}\):
\[ E_{\text{ox}} = -0.0591 \log_{10}[\text{H}^+] \]
By definition of \(\text{pH}\):
\[ \text{pH} = -\log_{10}[\text{H}^+] \implies \log_{10}[\text{H}^+] = -\text{pH} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Substitute the relationship into the Nernst equation:
\[ E_{\text{ox}} = -0.0591 \times (-\text{pH}) = 0.0591 \times \text{pH} \]
Given \(\text{pH} = 10\):
\[ E_{\text{ox}} = 0.0591 \times 10 = 0.591\text{ V} \approx 0.59\text{ V} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The reduction potential of hydrogen electrode is \(E_{\text{red}} = -0.0591 \times \text{pH} = -0.59\text{ V}\). The oxidation potential is the negative of reduction potential: \(E_{\text{ox}} = -E_{\text{red}} = +0.59\text{ V}\).<br/>
The oxidation potential of the electrode is \(0.59\text{ V}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ (Given Data and Conditions)</strong><br/>
विलयन का \(\text{pH} = 10\)।<br/>
हाइड्रोजन गैस का दाब: \(P_{\text{H}_2} = 1\text{ atm}\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी (Assumptions and Sign Conventions)</strong><br/>
ऑक्सीकरण अर्ध-सेल अभिक्रिया:
\[ \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2(g) \longrightarrow \text{H}^+(aq) + e^- \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत (Relevant Formulae and Principles)</strong><br/>
ऑक्सीकरण विभव के लिए नेर्नस्ट समीकरण:
\[ E_{\text{ox}} = E^\circ_{\text{ox}} - \frac{0.0591}{1}\log_{10}[\text{H}^+] \]
चूँकि \(\text{pH} = -\log_{10}[\text{H}^+]\), अतः:
\[ E_{\text{ox}} = +0.0591 \times \text{pH} \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल (Step-by-Step Working)</strong><br/>
\(\text{pH} = 10\) का मान रखने पर:
\[ E_{\text{ox}} = 0.0591 \times 10 \approx 0.59\text{ V} \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर (Check and Final Answer)</strong><br/>
अपचयन विभव \(-0.59\text{ V}\) है, अतः ऑक्सीकरण विभव \(+0.59\text{ V}\) प्राप्त होता है।<br/>
अतः इलेक्ट्रोड का ऑक्सीकरण विभव \(0.59\text{ V}\) होगा।</p>
    ]]></text>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name>
    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2013_Q50 - Thermodynamic condition for equal activation energies</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>A reaction having equal energies of activation for forward and reverse reactions has</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक अभिक्रिया, जिसमें अग्रिम (forward) तथा प्रतीप (reverse) अभिक्रियाओं की सक्रियण ऊर्जाएं समान हैं, के लिए होता है:</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Activation energy of the forward reaction: \(E_{a(f)}\).<br/>
Activation energy of the backward (reverse) reaction: \(E_{a(b)}\).<br/>
Condition: \(E_{a(f)} = E_{a(b)}\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Physical Concepts</strong><br/>
The enthalpy change of a reaction \(\Delta H\) is the difference between the potential energy of the products and the reactants along the reaction coordinate.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Relationship between activation energies and enthalpy of reaction:
\[ \Delta H = E_{a(f)} - E_{a(b)} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Substitute the given equality \(E_{a(f)} = E_{a(b)}\) into the relationship:
\[ \Delta H = E_{a(f)} - E_{a(f)} = 0 \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
An equality of forward and reverse activation energies directly implies thermoneutrality (\(\Delta H = 0\)). It does not impose any necessary requirement on the entropy change \(\Delta S\) or free energy change \(\Delta G\).<br/>
The reaction has \(\Delta H = 0\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ (Given Data and Conditions)</strong><br/>
अग्रिम अभिक्रिया की सक्रियण ऊर्जा: \(E_{a(f)}\)।<br/>
प्रतीप अभिक्रिया की सक्रियण ऊर्जा: \(E_{a(b)}\)।<br/>
दी गई शर्त: \(E_{a(f)} = E_{a(b)}\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और भौतिक सिद्धांत (Assumptions and Physical Concepts)</strong><br/>
अभिक्रिया की एन्थैल्पी परिवर्तन \(\Delta H\) अग्रिम तथा प्रतीप सक्रियण ऊर्जाओं के अंतर के बराबर होता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत (Relevant Formulae and Principles)</strong><br/>
सूत्र:
\[ \Delta H = E_{a(f)} - E_{a(b)} \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल (Step-by-Step Working)</strong><br/>
समान मान रखने पर:
\[ \Delta H = 0 \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर (Check and Final Answer)</strong><br/>
यह शर्त केवल \(\Delta H = 0\) को सुनिश्चित करती है, \(\Delta S\) या \(\Delta G\) का शून्य होना आवश्यक नहीं है।<br/>
अतः सही संबंध \(\Delta H = 0\) है।</p>

\[ m_{\text{pure}} = 0.50\text{ mol} \times 63.0\text{ g mol}^{-1} = 31.5\text{ g} \]
3. Determine the required mass of \(70\%\) concentrated acid solution:
\[ m_{\text{solution}} = \frac{31.5\text{ g}}{70} \times 100 = 45.0\text{ g} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
\(45.0\text{ g}\) of \(70\%\) solution contains \(45.0 \times 0.70 = 31.5\text{ g}\) of \(\text{HNO}_3\), which corresponds to \(\frac{31.5}{63} = 0.5\text{ mol}\), exactly yielding \(2.0\text{ M}\) in \(250\text{ mL}\).<br/>
The mass of concentrated nitric acid solution required is \(45.0\text{ g conc. HNO}_3\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ (Given Data and Conditions)</strong><br/>
आयतन: \(V = 250\text{ mL} = 0.25\text{ L}\)।<br/>
मोलरता: \(M = 2.0\text{ M}\)।<br/>
अम्ल की सांद्रता: \(70\%\ \text{HNO}_3\) (द्रव्यमान अनुसार)।<br/>
\(\text{HNO}_3\) का मोलर द्रव्यमान \(= 63\text{ g mol}^{-1}\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी (Assumptions and Sign Conventions)</strong><br/>
\(100\text{ g}\) सांद्र अम्ल में \(70\text{ g}\) शुद्ध \(\text{HNO}_3\) है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत (Relevant Formulae and Principles)</strong><br/>
मोल: \(n = M \times V\)।<br/>
शुद्ध अम्ल का द्रव्यमान: \(m_{\text{शुद्ध}} = n \times 63\)।<br/>
सांद्र विलयन का द्रव्यमान: \(m_{\text{विलयन}} = \frac{m_{\text{शुद्ध}}}{70} \times 100\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल (Step-by-Step Working)</strong><br/>
आवश्यक मोल:
\[ n = 2.0 \times 0.25 = 0.50\text{ mol} \]<br/>

\[ m_{\text{शुद्ध}} = 0.50 \times 63 = 31.5\text{ g} \]<br/>
सांद्र विलयन का द्रव्यमान:
\[ m_{\text{विलयन}} = \frac{31.5}{70} \times 100 = 45.0\text{ g} \]</p>

अतः \(45.0\text{ g}\) सांद्र \(\text{HNO}_3\) की आवश्यकता होगी।</p>

    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2013_Q55 - Carbon atoms per unit cell of diamond</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>The number of carbon atoms per unit cell of diamond unit cell is</p>
<hr/>
<p>हीरे के एकक कोष्ठिका (diamond unit cell) में प्रति एकक कोष्ठिका कार्बन परमाणुओं की संख्या होती है:</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Crystal structure: Diamond cubic lattice composed exclusively of carbon atoms.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Physical Concepts</strong><br/>
The diamond crystal structure consists of a face-centered cubic (\(\text{fcc}\)) lattice of carbon atoms with an additional four carbon atoms occupying half of the internal tetrahedral interstitial sites.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Contribution of lattice positions in a cubic unit cell:
- Corner atom: \(\frac{1}{8}\) contribution per corner \(\times 8\) corners \(= 1\).<br/>
- Face-center atom: \(\frac{1}{2}\) contribution per face \(\times 6\) faces \(= 3\).<br/>
- Tetrahedral void atom: fully within the unit cell, contributing \(1\) atom each.<br/>
Total effective number of atoms:
\[ Z = Z_{\text{fcc}} + Z_{\text{tetrahedral}} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
1. Number of carbon atoms forming the \(\text{fcc}\) lattice:
\[ 8 \times \frac{1}{8} + 6 \times \frac{1}{2} = 1 + 3 = 4 \]
2. Carbon atoms occupying half of the 8 tetrahedral voids:
\[ \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 1 = 4 \]
3. Total number of carbon atoms per unit cell:
\[ Z = 4 + 4 = 8 \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The packing efficiency of diamond is approximately \(34\%\), which directly accounts for \(Z = 8\) atoms in a cubic unit cell with edge length \(a = \frac{8r}{\sqrt{3}}\).<br/>
The number of carbon atoms per unit cell is 8.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ (Given Data and Conditions)</strong><br/>
हीरे की क्रिस्टल संरचना (Diamond cubic structure)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और भौतिक सिद्धांत (Assumptions and Physical Concepts)</strong><br/>
हीरे में कार्बन परमाणु \(\text{fcc}\) जालक बनाते हैं तथा आधे चतुष्फलकीय रिक्तियों (tetrahedral voids) में उपस्थित रहते हैं।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत (Relevant Formulae and Principles)</strong><br/>
प्रति एकक कोष्ठिका परमाणुओं की संख्या:
\[ Z = Z_{\text{fcc}} + Z_{\text{चतुष्फलकीय}} \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल (Step-by-Step Working)</strong><br/>
\(\text{fcc}\) जालक बिंदुओं से योगदान:
\[ 8 \times \frac{1}{8} + 6 \times \frac{1}{2} = 1 + 3 = 4 \]<br/>
चतुष्फलकीय रिक्तियों से योगदान (कुल 8 में से आधी भरी हैं):
\[ 4 \times 1 = 4 \]<br/>
कुल कार्बन परमाणु:
\[ Z = 4 + 4 = 8 \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर (Check and Final Answer)</strong><br/>
गणना संरचनात्मक विश्लेषण से पूर्णतः मेल खाती है।<br/>
अतः हीरे की एकक कोष्ठिका में कार्बन परमाणुओं की संख्या 8 होती है।</p>

<p>Maximum deviation from ideal gas is expected from</p>
<hr/>
<p>आदर्श गैस व्यवहार से अधिकतम विचलन किस गैस से अपेक्षित है?</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Gases to compare: \(\text{H}_2(g)\), \(\text{N}_2(g)\), \(\text{CH}_4(g)\), and \(\text{NH}_3(g)\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Physical Concepts</strong><br/>
Real gases deviate from ideal behavior primarily due to two non-ideal factors: intermolecular attractive forces and finite molecular volume. Greater intermolecular attraction leads to more pronounced deviation from ideal gas laws and higher liquefaction tendency.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The van der Waals parameter \(a\) quantifies the strength of intermolecular attractive forces. Polar molecules with strong dipole-dipole interactions or hydrogen bonding have significantly higher \(a\) values than non-polar molecules that only experience weak London dispersion forces.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
- \(\text{H}_2\) and \(\text{N}_2\) are non-polar homonuclear diatomic molecules with very weak dispersion forces.<br/>
- \(\text{CH}_4\) is non-polar with tetrahedral symmetry and small dispersion forces.<br/>
- \(\text{NH}_3\) is a polar pyramidal molecule capable of forming extensive intermolecular hydrogen bonds, leading to the strongest attractive intermolecular forces and the highest critical temperature among the four gases.<br/>
Consequently, \(\text{NH}_3\) shows the largest value of van der Waals constant \(a\) and exhibits the maximum deviation from ideality.</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Because \(\text{NH}_3\) is easily liquefiable owing to strong intermolecular hydrogen bonding, it departs most strongly from ideal gas assumptions.<br/>
Maximum deviation from ideal gas is expected from \(\text{NH}_3(g)\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ (Given Data and Conditions)</strong><br/>
तुलना के लिए गैसें: \(\text{H}_2\), \(\text{N}_2\), \(\text{CH}_4\) तथा \(\text{NH}_3\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और भौतिक सिद्धांत (Assumptions and Physical Concepts)</strong><br/>
गैसों का आदर्श व्यवहार से विचलन अंतराण्विक आकर्षण बलों की प्रबलता पर निर्भर करता है। आकर्षण बल जितना अधिक होगा, विचलन उतना ही अधिक होगा।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत (Relevant Formulae and Principles)</strong><br/>
वान्डरवाल्स स्थिरांक \(a\) अंतराण्विक आकर्षण बलों के परिमाण को दर्शाता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल (Step-by-Step Working)</strong><br/>
\(\text{H}_2\), \(\text{N}_2\) तथा \(\text{CH}_4\) अध्रुवीय गैसें हैं जिनमें केवल दुर्बल परिक्षेपण बल होते हैं। इसके विपरीत, \(\text{NH}_3\) एक ध्रुवीय अणु है जिसमें प्रबल हाइड्रोजन आबंधन उपस्थित होता है, जिससे इसका वान्डरवाल्स नियतांक \(a\) उच्चतम होता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर (Check and Final Answer)</strong><br/>
प्रबल आकर्षण बलों के कारण \(\text{NH}_3\) आदर्श गैस व्यवहार से अधिकतम विचलन प्रदर्शित करती है।<br/>
अतः अधिकतम विचलन \(\text{NH}_3(g)\) से अपेक्षित है।</p>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name>
    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2013_Q57 - Molar mass of fcc metal from density</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>A metal has a fcc lattice. The edge length of the unit cell is \(404\text{ pm}\). The density of the metal is \(2.72\text{ g cm}^{-3}\). The molar mass of the metal is</p>
<p>(\(\text{N}_\text{A}\) Avogadro's constant \(= 6.02 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}\))</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक धातु का जालक फलक केंद्रित घनीय (\(\text{fcc}\)) है। एकक कोष्ठिका के कोर की लंबाई \(404\text{ pm}\) है। धातु का घनत्व \(2.72\text{ g cm}^{-3}\) है। धातु का मोलर द्रव्यमान है:</p>
<p>(\(\text{N}_\text{A}\) आवोगाद्रो नियतांक \(= 6.02 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}\))</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Lattice type: Face-centered cubic (\(\text{fcc}\)), so \(Z = 4\).<br/>
Edge length: \(a = 404\text{ pm} = 404 \times 10^{-10}\text{ cm} = 4.04 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}\).<br/>
Density: \(d = 2.72\text{ g cm}^{-3}\).<br/>
Avogadro's constant: \(N_A = 6.02 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The crystal is an ideal, defect-free cubic unit cell.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Density of a cubic unit cell:
\[ d = \frac{Z \cdot M}{a^3 \cdot N_A} \]
Rearranging for molar mass \(M\):
\[ M = \frac{d \cdot a^3 \cdot N_A}{Z} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
1. Compute the volume of the unit cell \(a^3\):
\[ a^3 = (4.04 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm})^3 = 65.94 \times 10^{-24}\text{ cm}^3 = 6.594 \times 10^{-23}\text{ cm}^3 \]
2. Substitute the values into the molar mass expression:
\[ M = \frac{2.72 \times (6.594 \times 10^{-23}) \times (6.02 \times 10^{23})}{4} \]
\[ M = \frac{2.72 \times 39.70}{4} = \frac{107.98}{4} \approx 27.0\text{ g mol}^{-1} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The calculated density and molar mass correspond precisely to metallic Aluminum (\(M = 27\text{ g/mol}\), \(\text{fcc}\) structure, \(d \approx 2.7\text{ g/cm}^3\)).<br/>
The molar mass of the metal is \(27\text{ g mol}^{-1}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ (Given Data and Conditions)</strong><br/>
\(\text{fcc}\) जालक के लिए: \(Z = 4\)।<br/>
कोर लंबाई: \(a = 404\text{ pm} = 4.04 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}\)।<br/>
घनत्व: \(d = 2.72\text{ g cm}^{-3}\)।<br/>
आवोगाद्रो संख्या: \(N_A = 6.02 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी (Assumptions and Sign Conventions)</strong><br/>
क्रिस्टल एक आदर्श फलक-केंद्रित घनीय जालक है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत (Relevant Formulae and Principles)</strong><br/>
घनत्व का सूत्र:
\[ d = \frac{Z \times M}{a^3 \times N_A} \implies M = \frac{d \times a^3 \times N_A}{Z} \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल (Step-by-Step Working)</strong><br/>
एकक कोष्ठिका का आयतन:
\[ a^3 = (4.04 \times 10^{-8})^3 \approx 6.594 \times 10^{-23}\text{ cm}^3 \]<br/>
मोलर द्रव्यमान:
\[ M = \frac{2.72 \times 6.594 \times 10^{-23} \times 6.02 \times 10^{23}}{4} \approx \frac{107.98}{4} \approx 27\text{ g mol}^{-1} \]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर (Check and Final Answer)</strong><br/>
यह मान ऐलुमिनियम धातु (\(M = 27\text{ g mol}^{-1}\)) के अनुरूप है।<br/>
अतः धातु का मोलर द्रव्यमान \(27\text{ g mol}^{-1}\) है।</p>

    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Candidate molecular pairs:<br/>
- \(\text{H}_2\text{O}\) and alcohol<br/>
- \(\text{Cl}_2\) and \(\text{CCl}_4\)<br/>
- \(\text{HCl}\) and \(\text{He}\) atoms<br/>
- \(\text{SiF}_4\) and \(\text{He}\) atoms</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Physical Concepts</strong><br/>
Dipole-induced dipole interactions (Debye forces) arise when a molecule with a permanent dipole moment polarizes the electron cloud of an adjacent non-polar particle (atom or molecule), thereby inducing a temporary dipole in it.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
- Polar molecule + Polar molecule \(\rightarrow\) Dipole-dipole interactions / Hydrogen bonding.<br/>
- Non-polar particle + Non-polar particle \(\rightarrow\) Dispersion (London) forces.<br/>
- Polar molecule + Non-polar particle \(\rightarrow\) Dipole-induced dipole interaction.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Analyzing each pair:<br/>
- \(\text{H}_2\text{O}\) and alcohol: Both are polar molecules; interaction is primarily hydrogen bonding and dipole-dipole.<br/>
- \(\text{Cl}_2\) and \(\text{CCl}_4\): Both species are non-polar (\(\mu = 0\)); interaction is London dispersion forces.<br/>
- \(\text{HCl}\) and \(\text{He}\): \(\text{HCl}\) is a polar molecule having a permanent dipole (\(\mu \neq 0\)), whereas \(\text{He}\) is a non-polar monoatomic noble gas. The permanent dipole of \(\text{HCl}\) induces a dipole in the \(\text{He}\) atom, producing dipole-induced dipole interaction.<br/>
- \(\text{SiF}_4\) and \(\text{He}\): \(\text{SiF}_4\) has tetrahedral symmetry (\(\mu = 0\)) and is non-polar, so only dispersion forces exist.</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The combination of polar \(\text{HCl}\) with non-polar \(\text{He}\) uniquely exemplifies Debye interaction.<br/>
Dipole-induced dipole interactions are present in \(\text{HCl}\) and \(\text{He}\) atoms.</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर (Check and Final Answer)</strong><br/>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Physical Concepts</strong><br/>
The acid strength of oxoacids is governed by the stability of the conjugate base formed after deprotonation. Delocalization of negative charge over terminal oxygen atoms strongly stabilizes the anion.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
For oxoacids of central atoms with similar or high electronegativity:
1. Higher oxidation state of the central atom increases acidity.<br/>
2. In the conjugate base of perchloric acid, \(\text{ClO}_4^-\), chlorine is in its maximum \(+7\) oxidation state. The single negative charge is symmetrically dispersed over four equivalent oxygen atoms via resonance:
\[ \text{ClO}_4^- \longleftrightarrow \text{four equivalent resonance contributors} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
- In \(\text{H}_2\text{SO}_3\), \(\text{S}\) is in \(+4\) oxidation state (weak acid).<br/>
- In \(\text{HClO}_3\), \(\text{Cl}\) is in \(+5\) oxidation state.<br/>
- In \(\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4\), \(\text{S}\) is in \(+6\) oxidation state.<br/>
- In \(\text{HClO}_4\), \(\text{Cl}\) is in \(+7\) oxidation state. The resulting perchlorate ion \(\text{ClO}_4^-\) exhibits exceptionally high resonance stabilization, minimizing basicity of the anion and making \(\text{HClO}_4\) the strongest acid (\(pK_a \approx -10\)).</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Perchloric acid (\(\text{HClO}_4\)) is widely recognized as the strongest mineral oxoacid among those listed.<br/>
The strongest acid is \(\text{HClO}_4\).</p>

    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2013_Q62 - Paramagnetic diatomic species identification</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>Which of the following is paramagnetic?</p>
<hr/>
<p>निम्नलिखित में से कौन अनुचुंबकीय (paramagnetic) है?</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Species to evaluate: \(\text{CO}\), \(\text{O}_2^-\), \(\text{CN}^-\), and \(\text{NO}^+\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Physical Concepts</strong><br/>
According to Molecular Orbital Theory (MOT), a species containing one or more unpaired electrons is paramagnetic, whereas a species with all electrons paired is diamagnetic.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Count the total number of electrons in each diatomic species:
- Species with an odd number of electrons must possess at least one unpaired electron and are inherently paramagnetic.<br/>
- Species with 14 electrons have a closed-shell electronic configuration \(\sigma_{1s}^2 \sigma^*_{1s} \sigma_{2s}^2 \sigma^*_{2s} \pi_{2p_x}^2 \pi_{2p_y}^2 \sigma_{2p_z}^2\) and are diamagnetic.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
1. \(\text{CO}\): Total electrons \(= 6 (\text{C}) + 8 (\text{O}) = 14\). All electrons are paired \(\implies\) Diamagnetic.<br/>
2. \(\text{O}_2^-\) (superoxide ion): Total electrons \(= 8 + 8 + 1 = 17\). Since 17 is an odd number, there must be an unpaired electron in the antibonding \(\pi^*\) molecular orbital (\(\pi^*_{2p_x}^2 \pi^*_{2p_y}^1\)) \(\implies\) Paramagnetic.<br/>
3. \(\text{CN}^-\): Total electrons \(= 6 (\text{C}) + 7 (\text{N}) + 1 = 14\). Isoelectronic with \(\text{N}_2\), all electrons paired \(\implies\) Diamagnetic.<br/>
4. \(\text{NO}^+\): Total electrons \(= 7 (\text{N}) + 8 (\text{O}) - 1 = 14\). Isoelectronic with \(\text{N}_2\), all electrons paired \(\implies\) Diamagnetic.</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Only the superoxide ion \(\text{O}_2^-\) contains an odd number of electrons and an unpaired electron.<br/>
The paramagnetic species is \(\text{O}_2^-\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ (Given Data and Conditions)</strong><br/>
दी गई स्पीशीज: \(\text{CO}\), \(\text{O}_2^-\), \(\text{CN}^-\) तथा \(\text{NO}^+\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और भौतिक सिद्धांत (Assumptions and Physical Concepts)</strong><br/>
आण्विक कक्षक सिद्धांत (MOT) के अनुसार, अयुग्मित इलेक्ट्रॉन युक्त स्पीशीज अनुचुंबकीय होती है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत (Relevant Formulae and Principles)</strong><br/>
इलेक्ट्रॉनों की कुल संख्या:<br/>
- \(\text{CO} = 6 + 8 = 14\) (प्रतिचुंबकीय)<br/>
- \(\text{O}_2^- = 8 + 8 + 1 = 17\) (विषम संख्या, अनुचुंबकीय)<br/>
- \(\text{CN}^- = 6 + 7 + 1 = 14\) (प्रतिचुंबकीय)<br/>
- \(\text{NO}^+ = 7 + 8 - 1 = 14\) (प्रतिचुंबकीय)</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल (Step-by-Step Working)</strong><br/>
सुपरऑक्साइड आयन (\(\text{O}_2^-\)) के \(\pi^*_{2p}\) आण्विक कक्षक में एक अयुग्मित इलेक्ट्रॉन उपस्थित होता है, जिसके कारण यह अनुचुंबकीय गुण प्रदर्शित करता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच एवं अंतिम उत्तर (Check and Final Answer)</strong><br/>
केवल \(\text{O}_2^-\) में अयुग्मित इलेक्ट्रॉन है।<br/>
अतः अनुचुंबकीय स्पीशीज \(\text{O}_2^-\) है।</p>

Chemical group under consideration: Silicates minerals.</p>

Silicates represent a vast class of minerals whose crystal frameworks are constructed from fundamental tetrahedral building blocks sharing vertices.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
In all silicates, silicon is \(sp^3\)-hybridized and bonded to four oxygen atoms in a tetrahedral arrangement, carrying an overall charge of \(-4\):
\[ [\text{SiO}_4]^{4-} \]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>

The basic structural unit of silicates is \(\text{SiO}_4^{4-}\).</p>

    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2013_Q65 - Reaction not preparing benzaldehyde</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>Reaction by which benzaldehyde cannot be prepared?</p>
<hr/>
<p>वह अभिक्रिया, जिसके द्वारा बेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड नहीं बनाया जा सकता है:</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Four organic reaction pathways to evaluate for the preparation of benzaldehyde (\(\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CHO}\)):<br/>
1. Toluene with \(\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2\) in \(\text{CS}_2\) followed by \(\text{H}_3\text{O}^+\).<br/>
2. Benzoyl chloride with \(\text{H}_2\) in the presence of \(\text{Pd-BaSO}_4\).<br/>
3. Benzene with \(\text{CO} + \text{HCl}\) in the presence of anhydrous \(\text{AlCl}_3\).<br/>
4. Benzoic acid with \(\text{Zn/Hg}\) and concentrated \(\text{HCl}\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Physical Concepts</strong><br/>
Evaluate standard synthetic methods (named reactions) for aromatic aldehydes.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
1. Etard reaction: Oxidation of toluene (\(\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_3\)) by chromyl chloride (\(\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2\)) yields a chromium complex that hydrolyzes to benzaldehyde.<br/>
2. Rosenmund reduction: Catalytic hydrogenation of benzoyl chloride (\(\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{COCl}\)) over poisoned palladium (\(\text{Pd-BaSO}_4\)) produces benzaldehyde.<br/>
3. Gattermann-Koch reaction: Formylation of benzene (\(\text{C}_6\text{H}_6\)) with \(\text{CO}\) and \(\text{HCl}\) in the presence of anhydrous \(\text{AlCl}_3\) gives benzaldehyde.<br/>
4. Clemmensen reduction: The reagent \(\text{Zn/Hg}\) in concentrated \(\text{HCl}\) specifically reduces the carbonyl group (\(>\!\text{C}=\text{O}\)) of aldehydes and ketones to a methylene group (\(-\text{CH}_2-\)). It does not reduce carboxylic acids (\(-\text{COOH}\)) to aldehydes.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Treating benzoic acid (\(\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{COOH}\)) with \(\text{Zn/Hg}\) and concentrated \(\text{HCl}\) does not reduce the carboxyl group to an aldehyde group. Carboxylic acids are inert to Clemmensen reduction conditions. Hence, this reaction cannot form benzaldehyde.</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Reactions 1, 2, and 3 are standard textbook preparations of benzaldehyde, whereas Clemmensen reduction does not reduce carboxylic acids.<br/>
Benzaldehyde cannot be prepared by treating benzoic acid with \(\text{Zn/Hg}\) and concentrated \(\text{HCl}\).</p>
