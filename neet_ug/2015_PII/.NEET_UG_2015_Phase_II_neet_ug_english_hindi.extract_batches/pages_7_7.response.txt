    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle and Formulae</strong><br/>The number of molecules \(N\) in a sample is given by:<br/>\[N = n \times N_A = \frac{m}{M} \times N_A\]<br/>where \(n\) is the number of moles, \(m\) is the mass in grams, \(M\) is the molar mass (\(18\text{ g mol}^{-1}\) for \(\mathrm{H_2O}\)), and \(N_A \approx 6.022 \times 10^{23}\text{ molecules mol}^{-1}\) is Avogadro's number.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Step-by-Step Calculation for Each Case</strong><br/>- In 18 molecules of water: \(N = 18\).<br/>- In \(1.8\text{ g}\) of water: \(n = \frac{1.8}{18} = 0.1\text{ mol} \implies N = 0.1 \times N_A \approx 6.022 \times 10^{22}\) molecules.<br/>- In \(18\text{ g}\) of water: \(n = \frac{18}{18} = 1.0\text{ mol} \implies N = 1.0 \times N_A \approx 6.022 \times 10^{23}\) molecules.<br/>- In 18 moles of water: \(n = 18\text{ mol} \implies N = 18 \times N_A \approx 1.084 \times 10^{25}\) molecules.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Comparison and Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Comparing all values:<br/>\[18 < 6.022 \times 10^{22} < 6.022 \times 10^{23} < 1.084 \times 10^{25}\]<br/>The number of water molecules is greatest in 18 moles of water.</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत एवं सूत्र</strong><br/>अणुओं की संख्या: \(N = n \times N_A\)<br/>जहाँ जल का मोलर द्रव्यमान \(M = 18\text{ g mol}^{-1}\) है तथा \(N_A \approx 6.022 \times 10^{23}\)।</p><p><strong>चरण 2: प्रत्येक स्थिति की गणना</strong><br/>- जल के 18 अणुओं में: \(N = 18\)<br/>- \(1.8\text{ g}\) जल में: \(n = \frac{1.8}{18} = 0.1\text{ मोल} \implies N = 0.1 \times N_A \approx 6.022 \times 10^{22}\)<br/>- \(18\text{ g}\) जल में: \(n = \frac{18}{18} = 1\text{ मोल} \implies N = N_A \approx 6.022 \times 10^{23}\)<br/>- 18 मोल जल में: \(N = 18 \times N_A \approx 1.084 \times 10^{25}\)</p><p><strong>चरण 3: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>जल के अणुओं की संख्या 18 मोल जल में अधिकतम है।</p>]]></text>
