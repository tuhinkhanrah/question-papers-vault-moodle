<question type="multichoice">
  <name>
    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2017_MCQ_Q90 - Relationship between emf of Daniell cells</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>In the electrochemical cell:</p>
<p>\[\text{Zn} \mid \text{ZnSO}_4\,(0.01\text{ M}) \parallel \text{CuSO}_4\,(1.0\text{ M}) \mid \text{Cu}\]</p>
<p>the emf of this Daniell cell is \(\text{E}_1\). When the concentration of \(\text{ZnSO}_4\) is changed to \(1.0\text{ M}\) and that of \(\text{CuSO}_4\) is changed to \(0.01\text{ M}\), the emf changes to \(\text{E}_2\). From the following, which one is the relationship between \(\text{E}_1\) and \(\text{E}_2\)?<br/>(Given, \(\frac{\text{RT}}{\text{F}} = 0.059\))</p>
<hr/>
<p>विद्युत रासायनिक सेल:</p>
<p>\[\text{Zn} \mid \text{ZnSO}_4\,(0.01\text{ M}) \parallel \text{CuSO}_4\,(1.0\text{ M}) \mid \text{Cu}\]</p>
<p>में इस डेनियल सेल का विद्युत वाहक बल (emf) \(\text{E}_1\) है। जब \(\text{ZnSO}_4\) की सांद्रता को परिवर्तित करके \(1.0\text{ M}\) तथा \(\text{CuSO}_4\) की सांद्रता को \(0.01\text{ M}\) कर दिया जाता है, तो विद्युत वाहक बल बदलकर \(\text{E}_2\) हो जाता है। निम्नलिखित में से \(\text{E}_1\) और \(\text{E}_2\) के बीच कौन-सा संबंध सही है?<br/>(दिया गया है, \(\frac{\text{RT}}{\text{F}} = 0.059\))</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
For the first cell configuration:
\[[\text{Zn}^{2+}]_1 = 0.01\text{ M} = 10^{-2}\text{ M}, \quad [\text{Cu}^{2+}]_1 = 1.0\text{ M}, \quad E_{\text{cell}} = \text{E}_1\]
For the second cell configuration:
\[[\text{Zn}^{2+}]_2 = 1.0\text{ M}, \quad [\text{Cu}^{2+}]_2 = 0.01\text{ M} = 10^{-2}\text{ M}, \quad E_{\text{cell}} = \text{E}_2\]
Given conversion factor: \(\frac{2.303\,\text{RT}}{\text{F}} = 0.059\text{ V}\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Cell Reaction</strong><br/>
The overall redox reaction occurring in the Daniell cell is:
\[\text{Zn}(s) + \text{Cu}^{2+}(aq) \rightleftharpoons \text{Zn}^{2+}(aq) + \text{Cu}(s)\]
The number of moles of electrons transferred in the balanced equation is \(n = 2\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Applying the Nernst equation at \(298\text{ K}\):
\[E_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{2.303\,\text{RT}}{n\text{F}} \log_{10} Q = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2} \log_{10} \left(\frac{[\text{Zn}^{2+}]}{[\text{Cu}^{2+}]}\right)\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
For the initial potential \(\text{E}_1\):
\[Q_1 = \frac{[\text{Zn}^{2+}]_1}{[\text{Cu}^{2+}]_1} = \frac{0.01}{1.0} = 10^{-2}\]
\[\text{E}_1 = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2} \log_{10}(10^{-2}) = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2}(-2) = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} + 0.059\text{ V}\]
For the modified potential \(\text{E}_2\):
\[Q_2 = \frac{[\text{Zn}^{2+}]_2}{[\text{Cu}^{2+}]_2} = \frac{1.0}{0.01} = 10^2\]
\[\text{E}_2 = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2} \log_{10}(10^2) = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2}(2) = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - 0.059\text{ V}\]
Subtracting the two values:
\[\text{E}_1 - \text{E}_2 = (E^\circ_{\text{cell}} + 0.059\text{ V}) - (E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - 0.059\text{ V}) = 0.118\text{ V} > 0\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Since \(\text{E}_1\) exceeds \(\text{E}_2\) by \(0.118\text{ V}\), the correct physical relationship is \(\text{E}_1 > \text{E}_2\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए मान और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>
प्रथम सेल व्यवस्था के लिए:
\[[\text{Zn}^{2+}]_1 = 0.01\text{ M} = 10^{-2}\text{ M}, \quad [\text{Cu}^{2+}]_1 = 1.0\text{ M}, \quad E_{\text{cell}} = \text{E}_1\]
द्वितीय सेल व्यवस्था के लिए:
\[[\text{Zn}^{2+}]_2 = 1.0\text{ M}, \quad [\text{Cu}^{2+}]_2 = 0.01\text{ M} = 10^{-2}\text{ M}, \quad E_{\text{cell}} = \text{E}_2\]
दिया गया गुणांक: \(\frac{2.303\,\text{RT}}{\text{F}} = 0.059\text{ V}\)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: पूर्वधारणाएँ और सेल अभिक्रिया</strong><br/>
डेनियल सेल में होने वाली समग्र रेडॉक्स अभिक्रिया है:
\[\text{Zn}(s) + \text{Cu}^{2+}(aq) \rightleftharpoons \text{Zn}^{2+}(aq) + \text{Cu}(s)\]
संतुलित अभिक्रिया में स्थानांतरित इलेक्ट्रॉनों की संख्या \(n = 2\) है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
\(298\text{ K}\) पर नर्नस्ट समीकरण के अनुसार:
\[E_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{2.303\,\text{RT}}{n\text{F}} \log_{10} Q = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2} \log_{10} \left(\frac{[\text{Zn}^{2+}]}{[\text{Cu}^{2+}]}\right)\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध गणना</strong><br/>
प्रारंभिक विभव \(\text{E}_1\) के लिए:
\[Q_1 = \frac{[\text{Zn}^{2+}]_1}{[\text{Cu}^{2+}]_1} = \frac{0.01}{1.0} = 10^{-2}\]
\[\text{E}_1 = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2} \log_{10}(10^{-2}) = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2}(-2) = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} + 0.059\text{ V}\]
परिवर्तित विभव \(\text{E}_2\) के लिए:
\[Q_2 = \frac{[\text{Zn}^{2+}]_2}{[\text{Cu}^{2+}]_2} = \frac{1.0}{0.01} = 10^2\]
\[\text{E}_2 = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2} \log_{10}(10^2) = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{2}(2) = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - 0.059\text{ V}\]
दोनों मानों का अंतर:
\[\text{E}_1 - \text{E}_2 = (E^\circ_{\text{cell}} + 0.059\text{ V}) - (E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - 0.059\text{ V}) = 0.118\text{ V} > 0\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: सत्यापन एवं अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
चूँकि \(\text{E}_1\) का मान \(\text{E}_2\) से \(0.118\text{ V}\) अधिक है, अतः सही संबंध \(\text{E}_1 > \text{E}_2\) है।</p>]]></text>

  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>The final proof for DNA as the genetic material came from the experiments of:</p>
<hr/>
<p>डीएनए (DNA) के आनुवंशिक पदार्थ होने का अंतिम प्रमाण किसके प्रयोगों से प्राप्त हुआ?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
The establishment of DNA as the genetic material proceeded through several key historical milestones, from transformation to biochemical identification and ultimately unequivocal viral replication proof.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Historical Milestones</strong><br/>
- Frederick Griffith (1928) discovered the "transforming principle" in <i>Streptococcus pneumoniae</i> but could not determine its biochemical nature.<br/>
- Oswald Avery, Colin MacLeod, and Maclyn McCarty (1944) discovered that DNA was the transforming substance through enzymatic digestion assays, but many biologists remained skeptical that proteins might carry hereditary information.<br/>
- Har Gobind Khorana contributed fundamentally to deciphering the genetic code and synthetic nucleotide sequences.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Hershey and Chase Experiment</strong><br/>
Alfred Hershey and Martha Chase (1952) worked with bacteriophage T2 and <i>Escherichia coli</i>. They differentially labeled bacteriophage DNA with radioactive phosphorus (\(^{32}\text{P}\)) and protein coats with radioactive sulfur (\(^{35}\text{S}\)). Upon infection, blending, and centrifugation, only \(^{32}\text{P}\) entered the bacterial host cells, conclusively demonstrating that viral DNA, not protein, directs progeny formation.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The final, unequivocal proof establishing DNA as the genetic material was provided by the experiments of Hershey and Chase.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
डीएनए के आनुवंशिक पदार्थ के रूप में स्थापित होने की यात्रा कई ऐतिहासिक चरणों से गुजरी, जिसमें रूपांतरण की खोज, जैव रासायनिक पहचान और अंततः विषाणुजनित निर्विवाद प्रमाण शामिल हैं।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: ऐतिहासिक प्रयोगों का मूल्यांकन</strong><br/>
- फ्रेडरिक ग्रिफिथ (1928) ने <i>स्ट्रेप्टोकोकस न्यूमोनी</i> में "रूपांतरणकारी सिद्धांत" की खोज की, परंतु वे इसकी रासायनिक प्रकृति नहीं बता सके।<br/>
- एवरी, मैकलियोड तथा मैकार्थी (1944) ने एंजाइमी विश्लेषण द्वारा रूपांतरणकारी कारक को डीएनए सिद्ध किया, फिर भी कई वैज्ञानिक प्रोटीन को आनुवंशिक पदार्थ मानते रहे।<br/>
- हरगोविंद खुराना ने आनुवंशिक कूट के रासायनिक संश्लेषण और कूट वाचन में महत्वपूर्ण योगदान दिया।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: हर्षे और चेस का प्रयोग</strong><br/>
अल्फ्रेड हर्षे और मार्था चेस (1952) ने जीवाणुभोजी T2 और <i>ई. कोलाई</i> पर कार्य किया। उन्होंने डीएनए को रेडियोधर्मी फास्फोरस (\(^{32}\text{P}\)) और प्रोटीन आवरण को रेडियोधर्मी सल्फर (\(^{35}\text{S}\)) से चिह्नित किया। संक्रमण, संमिश्रण (ब्लेंडिंग) और अपकेंद्रीकरण के पश्चात केवल \(^{32}\text{P}\) ही जीवाणु कोशिकाओं के भीतर प्रवेश कर सका, जिससे यह सिद्ध हुआ कि डीएनए ही आनुवंशिक पदार्थ है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
डीएनए के आनुवंशिक पदार्थ होने का अंतिम और निर्विवाद प्रमाण हर्षे और चेस के प्रयोगों से प्राप्त हुआ।</p>]]></text>

    <text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2017_MCQ_Q92 - Spliceosomes absence in prokaryotic bacterial cells</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Spliceosomes are not found in cells of:</p>
<hr/>
<p>स्प्लैशियोसोम (Spliceosomes) किसकी कोशिकाओं में नहीं पाए जाते हैं?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
Spliceosomes are complex molecular assemblies composed of small nuclear RNAs (snRNAs) and ribonucleoproteins (snRNPs) that catalyze the removal of non-coding intervening sequences (introns) and the ligation of coding sequences (exons) during precursor messenger RNA (pre-mRNA) splicing.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Comparison of Eukaryotic and Prokaryotic Gene Expression</strong><br/>
- Eukaryotes (including plants, animals, and fungi) possess split-gene organizations wherein monocistronic pre-mRNAs contain both exons and introns, necessitating post-transcriptional splicing by spliceosomes within the nucleus.<br/>
- Bacteria are prokaryotes whose genomes lack split genes and introns in protein-coding genes. Furthermore, transcription and translation are coupled in bacteria, which eliminates the requirement for pre-mRNA splicing machinery.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Because prokaryotic genes lack introns, spliceosomes are not found in the cells of bacteria.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
स्प्लैशियोसोम छोटे नाभिकीय आरएनए (snRNAs) तथा राइबोन्यूक्लियोप्रोटीनों (snRNPs) से मिलकर बने जटिल आणविक तंत्र होते हैं, जो प्राथमिक अनुलेख (pre-mRNA) से गैर-कोडिंग अनुक्रमों (इंट्रॉन) को हटाने तथा कोडिंग अनुक्रमों (एक्सॉन) को जोड़ने (समबंधन या स्प्लिसिंग) का कार्य करते हैं।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: यूकेरियोट्स एवं प्रोकेरियोट्स की तुलना</strong><br/>
- यूकेरियोटिक जीवों (जैसे पादपों, जंतुओं तथा कवकों) में विलगित जीन (स्प्लिट जीन) पाए जाते हैं, जिनमें इंट्रॉन और एक्सॉन उपस्थित होते हैं। इसलिए इनमें केंद्रक के भीतर स्प्लैशियोसोम द्वारा स्प्लिसिंग अनिवार्य होती है।<br/>
- जीवाणु प्रोकेरियोटिक जीव होते हैं जिनके प्रोटीन-कोडिंग जीनों में इंट्रॉन नहीं पाए जाते हैं। इसके अतिरिक्त, इनमें अनुलेखन और अनुवाद समकालिक होते हैं, जिसके कारण स्प्लिसिंग तंत्र अनुपस्थित होता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
जीवाणुओं में विलगित जीन एवं इंट्रॉन न होने के कारण स्प्लैशियोसोम जीवाणु (बैक्टीरिया) की कोशिकाओं में नहीं पाए जाते हैं।</p>]]></text>

    <text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2017_MCQ_Q93 - Pivot joint between atlas and axis vertebrae</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>The pivot joint between atlas and axis is a type of:</p>
<hr/>
<p>एटलस एवं एक्सिस के बीच का धुराग्र संधि (पिवट जोड़) किसका एक प्रकार है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
Joints in the human skeletal system are classified into three major structural categories based on degree of mobility: fibrous joints (immovable), cartilaginous joints (slightly movable), and synovial joints (freely movable, characterized by a fluid-filled synovial cavity).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Subtypes of Synovial Joints</strong><br/>
Synovial joints provide extensive articulation and mobility. Major types include:<br/>
- Ball and socket joint (e.g., shoulder and hip joints)<br/>
- Hinge joint (e.g., knee and elbow joints)<br/>
- Pivot joint (allowing rotational movement around a single central axis)<br/>
- Gliding joint (between carpals)<br/>
- Saddle joint (between carpal and metacarpal of thumb)</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Articulation between Atlas and Axis</strong><br/>
The first cervical vertebra (atlas) articulates with the second cervical vertebra (axis) via the odontoid process (dens) of the axis fitting into a ring formed by the anterior arch of the atlas and transverse ligament. Active transcription and replication require open, relaxed, and decondensed chromatin (euchromatin) where DNA is exposed and accessible to RNA and DNA polymerases.   <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Animal Breeding</strong><br/>
Pure lines represent populations of organisms that breed true for specific phenotypic traits over generations due to underlying genetic homozygosity.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Role and Consequences of Inbreeding</strong><br/>
Inbreeding is defined as the mating of closely related individuals within the same breed for 4 to 6 generations. Close inbreeding systematically increases homozygosity at gene loci, exposes deleterious recessive alleles that can subsequently be eliminated by rigorous artificial selection, and fixes desirable superior alleles, thereby yielding stable homozygous pure lines.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Evaluation of Cross-Breeding Alternatives</strong><br/>
- Mating unrelated individuals of the same breed (outcrossing) increases heterozygosity and introduces genetic variation.<br/>
- Mating individuals of different breeds (cross-breeding) and different species (interspecific hybridization) produce heterozygous hybrids showing hybrid vigor rather than homozygous pure lines.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Homozygous pure lines in cattle are obtained by the mating of related individuals of the same breed.</p>

Endocrine glands synthesize and secrete chemical messengers (hormones) directly into the blood. While most endocrine organs persist throughout adult life, certain structures develop and function only transiently during specific reproductive cycles.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Structures</strong><br/>
- <strong>Corpus allatum and corpus cardiacum:</strong> Neurohemal and endocrine structures present in arthropods (insects), where they secrete juvenile hormone and regulate development; they do not exist in the human body.<br/>
- <strong>Pineal gland:</strong> A permanent neuroendocrine gland situated on the dorsal aspect of the human diencephalon that secretes melatonin to regulate circadian rhythms.<br/>
- <strong>Corpus luteum:</strong> Formed inside the ovary from the remnants of the Graafian follicle following ovulation under the influence of luteinizing hormone (LH). It functions as a temporary endocrine gland by synthesizing and secreting substantial amounts of progesterone (as well as estrogen and relaxin) to maintain the uterine endometrium for pregnancy. In the absence of fertilization, it regresses into a fibrous scar (corpus albicans) within approximately 10 to 14 days.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The temporary endocrine gland present in the human body is the corpus luteum.</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Hemodynamic Regulation</strong><br/>
Blood pressure and circulating fluid volume are regulated coordinately by neuroendocrine feedback loops involving the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS), antidiuretic hormone (ADH), and atrial natriuretic factor (ANF).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Hormonal Response to Low Blood Pressure/Volume</strong><br/>
When arterial pressure or blood volume drops:<br/>
- Juxtaglomerular cells detect decreased glomerular filtration and renal perfusion, triggering the secretion of the enzyme <strong>renin</strong>.<br/>
- Renin converts angiotensinogen to angiotensin I, which is cleaved to angiotensin II. Angiotensin II triggers the adrenal cortex to release <strong>aldosterone</strong>, enhancing renal \(\text{Na}^+\) and water reabsorption.<br/>
- Hypothalamic osmoreceptors and arterial baroreceptors signal the posterior pituitary to secrete <strong>ADH (vasopressin)</strong>, promoting water reabsorption from late distal tubules and collecting ducts and causing systemic vasoconstriction.<br/>
Together, renin, aldosterone, and ADH restore blood volume and blood pressure.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Mechanism of Atrial Natriuretic Factor (ANF)</strong><br/>
Atrial Natriuretic Factor (ANF) is an antagonistic peptide hormone synthesized and released by the atrial cardiomyocytes of the heart specifically in response to excessive atrial stretch resulting from an <i>increase</i> in blood volume and high venous return. ANF stimulates natriuresis, diuresis, and systemic vasodilation, thereby acting to reduce blood pressure. Consequently, a decrease in blood pressure/volume inhibits rather than promotes ANF release.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
A decrease in blood pressure or blood volume will not cause the release of atrial natriuretic factor.</p>

<p><strong>चरण 1: रक्तदाब नियमन का संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>

<p><strong>Step 2: Fruit Wall versus Seed Tissues</strong><br/>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
Sexually transmitted infections (STIs) are infectious diseases transmitted through sexual contact. They are caused by distinct bacterial, viral, and protozoan pathogens.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Etiological Matching</strong><br/>
- <strong>Gonorrhea:</strong> A bacterial venereal disease affecting mucous membranes of the urogenital tract, caused by the Gram-negative diplococcus <i>Neisseria gonorrhoeae</i>. Thus, (A) matches with (ii).<br/>
- <strong>Syphilis:</strong> A chronic bacterial infection causing chancres and systemic complications, caused by the spirochete bacterium <i>Treponema pallidum</i>. Thus, (B) matches with (iii).<br/>
- <strong>Genital Warts:</strong> Benign epithelial lesions occurring on external genitalia and perianal regions, caused by Human Papilloma virus (HPV). Thus, (C) matches with (iv).<br/>
- <strong>AIDS:</strong> Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome is a progressive viral disease characterized by depletion of helper T lymphocytes (\(\text{CD4}^+\)), caused by Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV). Thus, (D) matches with (i).</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The correct correspondence between diseases and their causative agents is: (A)-(ii), (B)-(iii), (C)-(iv), (D)-(i).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
यौन संचारित संक्रमण (STIs) वे संक्रामक रोग हैं जो यौन संपर्क के माध्यम से फैलते हैं। ये विशिष्ट जीवाणुओं, विषाणुओं अथवा प्रोटोजोआ द्वारा जनित होते हैं।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: रोग कारकों का मिलान</strong><br/>
- <strong>सुजाक (गोनोरिया):</strong> यह जननमूत्र पथ की श्लेष्मा झिल्ली को प्रभावित करने वाला एक जीवाणुजनित रोग है, जो <i>Neisseria gonorrhoeae</i> द्वारा होता है। अतः (A) का सही मिलान (ii) है।<br/>
- <strong>उपदंश (सिफलिस):</strong> यह एक दीर्घकालिक जीवाणुजनित रोग है जो <i>Treponema pallidum</i> नामक स्पाइरोकीट जीवाणु द्वारा होता है। अतः (B) का सही मिलान (iii) है।<br/>
- <strong>जननांग मस्से (जेनिटल वार्ट्स):</strong> यह जननांगों पर होने वाला विषाणुजनित रोग है, जिसका कारक ह्यूमन पैपिलोमा वायरस (HPV) है। अतः (C) का सही मिलान (iv) है।<br/>
- <strong>एड्स (AIDS):</strong> उपार्जित प्रतिरक्षा न्यूनता संलक्षण (एड्स) एक रेट्रोविषाणु, ह्यूमन इम्यूनोडेफिशिएंसी वायरस (HIV) द्वारा होता है। अतः (D) का सही मिलान (i) है।</p>
