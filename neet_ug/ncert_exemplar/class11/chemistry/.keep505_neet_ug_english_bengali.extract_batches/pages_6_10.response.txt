  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Assertion (A):</strong> At constant temperature, pV vs V plot for real gases is not a straight line.</p>
<p><strong>Reason (R):</strong> At high pressure all gases have Z > 1 but at intermediate pressure most gases have Z < 1.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>বিবৃতি (A):</strong> স্থির তাপমাত্রায় বাস্তব গ্যাসের ক্ষেত্রে V-এর সাপেক্ষে pV-এর লেখচিত্রটি সরলরেখা হয় না।</p>
<p><strong>কারণ (R):</strong> উচ্চ চাপে সমস্ত গ্যাসের ক্ষেত্রে Z > 1 হয় কিন্তু মাঝারি চাপে অধিকাংশ গ্যাসের ক্ষেত্রে Z < 1 হয়।</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>For an ideal gas, Boyle's law holds at constant temperature, meaning \( pV = \text{constant} \), which produces a horizontal straight line when plotted against volume or pressure. For real gases, deviations from ideal behavior occur due to finite molecular volume and intermolecular attractions.</p>
<p><strong>Evaluation</strong><br/>The compressibility factor is defined as \( Z = \frac{pV}{nRT} \). At intermediate pressures, attractive forces dominate between molecules, pulling them closer together so that \( pV < nRT \) and \( Z < 1 \) (negative deviation). At high pressures, repulsive forces and the actual volume of the molecules dominate, causing the gas to resist compression so that \( pV > nRT \) and \( Z > 1 \) (positive deviation).</p>
<p><strong>Application</strong><br/>Because the value of \( Z \) (and therefore the product \( pV \)) varies across different pressure and volume regimes—first dipping below ideal behavior and then rising above it—the plot of \( pV \) versus \( V \) is a curve rather than a straight line. The assertion is correct, and the reason correctly explains why the plot deviates from linearity.</p>
<p><strong>Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Both statements are correct, and the behavior of the compressibility factor across pressure regimes provides the correct explanation for the non-linear plot.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধারণাগত নীতি</strong><br/>আদর্শ গ্যাসের ক্ষেত্রে স্থির তাপমাত্রায় বয়েলের সূত্র অনুযায়ী \( pV = \text{ধ্রুবক} \), ফলে আয়তন বা চাপের সাপেক্ষে \( pV \)-এর লেখচিত্র একটি অনুভূমিক সরলরেখা হয়। বাস্তব গ্যাসের ক্ষেত্রে অণুগুলির নিজস্ব আয়তন ও আন্তঃআণবিক আকর্ষণ বলের উপস্থিতির কারণে আদর্শ আচরণ থেকে বিচ্যুতি ঘটে।</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The boiling point of a liquid is defined thermodynamically as the temperature at which its saturated vapour pressure equals the prevailing external atmospheric pressure.</p>
<p><strong>Evaluation</strong><br/>Atmospheric pressure is caused by the weight of the air column above a given altitude. As altitude increases above sea level, the height and density of the overlying air column decrease, which causes the atmospheric pressure to decrease rather than increase.</p>
<p><strong>Application</strong><br/>The definition of boiling point stated in the assertion is scientifically accurate. However, the statement claiming that atmospheric pressure is high at high altitudes is physically false, because atmospheric pressure decreases with increasing elevation.</p>
<p><strong>Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The assertion is correct, but the reason is incorrect.</p>
