  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The stratospheric ozone layer is maintained by a dynamic photochemical steady state known as the Chapman cycle, and it acts as an essential planetary filter against solar ultraviolet radiation.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>In the upper stratosphere, ultraviolet solar radiation dissociates molecular oxygen into atomic oxygen, which subsequently combines with intact oxygen molecules to continuously form ozone (\(\mathrm{O}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{UV}} 2\mathrm{O}\) followed by \(\mathrm{O} + \mathrm{O}_2 \rightarrow \mathrm{O}_3\)).
