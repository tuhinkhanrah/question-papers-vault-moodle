  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>True value of mass = \(3.0\ \mathrm{g}\).<br/>Student A's readings: \(3.01\ \mathrm{g}\) and \(2.99\ \mathrm{g}\).<br/>Student B's readings: \(3.05\ \mathrm{g}\) and \(2.95\ \mathrm{g}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Definitions</strong><br/>Accuracy indicates how close a measured average value is to the true value.<br/>Precision indicates how close repeated individual measurements are to one another.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>Average reading = \(\frac{\text{Reading } 1 + \text{Reading } 2}{2}\).<br/>Spread or difference between readings = \(|\text{Reading } 1 - \text{Reading } 2|\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>For Student A:<br/>Average = \(\frac{3.01 + 2.99}{2} = 3.00\ \mathrm{g}\). Since this average is identical to the true value of \(3.0\ \mathrm{g}\), the measurement is accurate.<br/>Difference = \(3.01 - 2.99 = 0.02\ \mathrm{g}\). The two readings are very close to each other, so the measurement is also precise.<br/>For Student B:<br/>Average = \(\frac{3.05 + 2.95}{2} = 3.00\ \mathrm{g}\). While the average is accurate, the difference between readings is \(3.05 - 2.95 = 0.10\ \mathrm{g}\), which is five times larger than that of Student A, indicating low precision.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>Student A's results satisfy both accuracy and precision.<br/>Final Conceptual Conclusion: Results of student A are both precise and accurate.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: প্রদত্ত তথ্য ও শর্তাবলি</strong><br/>ভরের প্রকৃত মান = \(3.0\ \mathrm{g}\)।<br/>শিক্ষার্থী A-এর পাঠ: \(3.01\ \mathrm{g}\) এবং \(2.99\ \mathrm{g}\)।<br/>শিক্ষার্থী B-এর পাঠ: \(3.05\ \mathrm{g}\) এবং \(2.95\ \mathrm{g}\)।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ২: নীতি ও সংজ্ঞাসমূহ</strong><br/>যথার্থতা (Accuracy) বলতে বোঝায় পরিমাপের গড় মান প্রকৃত মানের কতটা নিকটবর্তী।<br/>সুনির্দিষ্টতা (Precision) বলতে বোঝায় একাধিক পরিমাপ পরস্পরের কতটা কাছাকাছি।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ৩: প্রয়োজনীয় সূত্র ও মূলনীতি</strong><br/>গড় পাঠ = \(\frac{\text{পাঠ } 1 + \text{পাঠ } 2}{2}\)।<br/>পাঠদ্বয়ের ব্যবধান = \(|\text{পাঠ } 1 - \text{পাঠ } 2|\)।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ৪: ধাপে ধাপে গণনা</strong><br/>শিক্ষার্থী A-এর জন্য:<br/>গড় পাঠ = \(\frac{3.01 + 2.99}{2} = 3.00\ \mathrm{g}\)। এই মান প্রকৃত মানের সমান হওয়ায় ফলাফল যথার্থ।<br/>পাঠদ্বয়ের ব্যবধান = \(3.01 - 2.99 = 0.02\ \mathrm{g}\)। পাঠগুলি পরস্পরের অত্যন্ত কাছাকাছি হওয়ায় ফলাফল সুনির্দিষ্টও বটে।<br/>শিক্ষার্থী B-এর জন্য:<br/>গড় পাঠ \(3.00\ \mathrm{g}\) হলেও ব্যবধান \(0.10\ \mathrm{g}\), ফলে তা সুনির্দিষ্ট নয়।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ৫: চূড়ান্ত সিদ্ধান্ত</strong><br/>শিক্ষার্থী A-এর ফলাফল যথার্থ এবং সুনির্দিষ্ট উভয়ই।<br/>চূড়ান্ত ধারণাগত উত্তর: Results of student A are both precise and accurate.</p>]]></text>

  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>A measured temperature on Fahrenheit scale is 200 °F. What will this reading be on Celsius scale?</p>
<hr/>
<p>ফারেনহাইট স্কেলে পরিমাপকৃত একটি তাপমাত্রা \(200\ ^\circ\mathrm{F}\)। সেলসিয়াস স্কেলে এই পাঠ কত হবে?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>Temperature on Fahrenheit scale, \(T_F = 200\ ^\circ\mathrm{F}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>Standard relationship between Fahrenheit and Celsius scales is used where \(32\ ^\circ\mathrm{F}\) corresponds to \(0\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\) and \(212\ ^\circ\mathrm{F}\) corresponds to \(100\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>\(^\circ\mathrm{C} = \frac{5}{9}(^\circ\mathrm{F} - 32)\)</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>Substitute the given Fahrenheit temperature into the equation:<br/>\(^\circ\mathrm{C} = \frac{5}{9}(200 - 32)\)<br/>\(^\circ\mathrm{C} = \frac{5}{9} \times 168 = \frac{840}{9} \approx 93.33\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\)</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>The temperature must lie between \(0\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\) and \(100\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\) since \(200\ ^\circ\mathrm{F}\) is close to the boiling point of water (\(212\ ^\circ\mathrm{F}\)). \(93.3\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\) is physically sound and accurate.<br/>Final Conceptual Conclusion: The reading on the Celsius scale is \(93.3\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: প্রদত্ত তথ্য ও শর্তাবলি</strong><br/>ফারেনহাইট স্কেলে তাপমাত্রা, \(T_F = 200\ ^\circ\mathrm{F}\)।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ২: রূপান্তর নীতি</strong><br/>সেলসিয়াস এবং ফারেনহাইট স্কেলের পারস্পরিক সম্পর্ক ব্যবহার করা হয় যেখানে \(32\ ^\circ\mathrm{F}\) মানটি \(0\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\) এবং \(212\ ^\circ\mathrm{F}\) মানটি \(100\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\)-এর সমতুল্য।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ৩: প্রয়োজনীয় সূত্র ও মূলনীতি</strong><br/>\(^\circ\mathrm{C} = \frac{5}{9}(^\circ\mathrm{F} - 32)\)</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ৪: ধাপে ধাপে গণনা</strong><br/>ফারেনহাইট তাপমাত্রার মান বসিয়ে পাই:<br/>\(^\circ\mathrm{C} = \frac{5}{9}(200 - 32)\)<br/>\(^\circ\mathrm{C} = \frac{5}{9} \times 168 = \frac{840}{9} \approx 93.33\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\)</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ৫: চূড়ান্ত সিদ্ধান্ত</strong><br/>প্রাপ্ত মান \(93.3\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\) পানির স্ফুটনাঙ্কের নিকটবর্তী তাপমাত্রার সাথে সামঞ্জস্যপূর্ণ।<br/>চূড়ান্ত ধারণাগত উত্তর: সেলসিয়াস স্কেলে পাঠ হবে \(93.3\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\)।</p>]]></text>

    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2026_SOMEBASICCONCEPTSOFCHEMISTRY_MCQ_Q03 - Molarity of sodium chloride solution calculation</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>What will be the molarity of a solution, which contains 5.85 g of NaCl(s) per 500 mL?</p>
<hr/>
<p>একটি দ্রবণের প্রতি \(500\ \mathrm{mL}\)-এ \(5.85\ \mathrm{g}\) \(\mathrm{NaCl(s)}\) উপস্থিত থাকলে দ্রবণটির মোলারিটি কত হবে?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>Mass of solute (\(\mathrm{NaCl}\)), \(w = 5.85\ \mathrm{g}\).<br/>Volume of solution, \(V = 500\ \mathrm{mL} = 0.500\ \mathrm{L}\).<br/>Molar mass of \(\mathrm{NaCl} = 23.0 + 35.5 = 58.5\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Model</strong><br/>The solute dissolves completely without volume expansion beyond the specified solution volume.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>Number of moles of solute, \(n = \frac{w}{M_{\text{molar}}}\)<br/>Molarity, \(M = \frac{n}{V\ (\text{in L})} = \frac{w \times 1000}{M_{\text{molar}} \times V\ (\text{in mL})}\)</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>Moles of \(\mathrm{NaCl} = \frac{5.85\ \mathrm{g}}{58.5\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}} = 0.1\ \mathrm{mol}\).<br/>Molarity = \(\frac{0.1\ \mathrm{mol}}{0.5\ \mathrm{L}} = 0.2\ \mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>Units are \(\mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\). The calculation is verified and yields \(0.2\ \mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\).<br/>Final Conceptual Conclusion: The molarity of the solution is \(0.2\ \mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: প্রদত্ত তথ্য ও শর্তাবলি</strong><br/>দ্রাবের ভর (\(\mathrm{NaCl}\)), \(w = 5.85\ \mathrm{g}\)।<br/>দ্রবণের আয়তন, \(V = 500\ \mathrm{mL} = 0.500\ \mathrm{L}\)।<br/>\(\mathrm{NaCl}\)-এর মোলার ভর = \(23.0 + 35.5 = 58.5\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}\)।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ২: নীতি ও অনুমিতি</strong><br/>সম্পূর্ণ দ্রাব দ্রবীভূত হয়ে দ্রবণ গঠন করে এবং নির্দিষ্ট আয়তন বজায় রাখে।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ৩: প্রয়োজনীয় সূত্র ও মূলনীতি</strong><br/>দ্রাবের মোল সংখ্যা, \(n = \frac{w}{M_{\text{molar}}}\)<br/>মোলারিটি, \(M = \frac{n}{V\ (\text{L এককে})}\)</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ৪: ধাপে ধাপে গণনা</strong><br/>\(\mathrm{NaCl}\)-এর মোল সংখ্যা = \(\frac{5.85}{58.5} = 0.1\ \mathrm{mol}\)।<br/>দ্রবণের মোলারিটি = \(\frac{0.1\ \mathrm{mol}}{0.5\ \mathrm{L}} = 0.2\ \mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\)।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ৫: চূড়ান্ত সিদ্ধান্ত</strong><br/>গণনাকৃত মোলারিটি হল \(0.2\ \mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\)।<br/>চূড়ান্ত ধারণাগত উত্তর: দ্রবণের মোলারিটি \(0.2\ \mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\)।</p>]]></text>

    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2026_SOMEBASICCONCEPTSOFCHEMISTRY_MCQ_Q04 - Molarity of solution obtained after dilution</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>If 500 mL of a 5M solution is diluted to 1500 mL, what will be the molarity of the solution obtained?</p>
<hr/>
<p>একটি \(5\ \mathrm{M}\) দ্রবণের \(500\ \mathrm{mL}\)-কে লঘু করে \(1500\ \mathrm{mL}\) করা হলে উৎপন্ন দ্রবণের মোলারিটি কত হবে?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>Initial molarity, \(M_1 = 5\ \mathrm{M}\).<br/>Initial volume, \(V_1 = 500\ \mathrm{mL}\).<br/>Final volume after dilution, \(V_2 = 1500\ \mathrm{mL}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Principles</strong><br/>On dilution, the total amount (moles) of solute remains unchanged.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>Molarity equation for dilution: \(M_1 V_1 = M_2 V_2\)</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>Substitute the values into the dilution formula:<br/>\(5\ \mathrm{M} \times 500\ \mathrm{mL} = M_2 \times 1500\ \mathrm{mL}\)<br/>\(M_2 = \frac{5 \times 500}{1500} = \frac{2500}{1500} = \frac{5}{3} \approx 1.666\ \mathrm{M} \approx 1.66\ \mathrm{M}\)</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>Because the volume increases by a factor of 3 (from \(500\ \mathrm{mL}\) to \(1500\ \mathrm{mL}\)), the molarity must decrease by a factor of 3: \(\frac{5}{3} \approx 1.66\ \mathrm{M}\).<br/>Final Conceptual Conclusion: The molarity of the obtained solution is \(1.66\ \mathrm{M}\).</p>
<hr/>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>Molar mass of \(\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6} = (6 \times 12) + (12 \times 1) + (6 \times 16) = 72 + 12 + 96 = 180\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}\).<br/>\(\text{Molarity } (M) = \frac{\text{Concentration in }\mathrm{g\ L^{-1}}}{\text{Molar mass in }\mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}}\)</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Principles</strong><br/>Molecular formula is an integral multiple of the empirical formula: \(\text{Molecular formula} = (\text{Empirical formula})_n\), where \(n = \frac{\text{Molecular mass}}{\text{Empirical formula mass}}\).</p>

  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>If the density of a solution is \(3.12\mathrm{g}\mathrm{mL}^{-1}\), the mass of \(1.5\mathrm{mL}\) solution in significant figures is ______.</p>
<hr/>
<p>একটি দ্রবণের ঘনত্ব \(3.12\mathrm{g}\mathrm{mL}^{-1}\) হলে তাৎপর্যপূর্ণ অঙ্ক সংখ্যা অনুসারে \(1.5\mathrm{mL}\) দ্রবণের ভর হবে ______।</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>Density of solution, \(d = 3.12\ \mathrm{g\ mL^{-1}}\) (contains 3 significant figures).<br/>Volume of solution, \(V = 1.5\ \mathrm{mL}\) (contains 2 significant figures).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Rules of Significant Figures</strong><br/>In multiplication or division, the final result cannot have more significant figures than the measurement with the fewest significant figures.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>\(\text{Mass} = \text{Density} \times \text{Volume}\)</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>Calculate numerical mass:<br/>\(\text{Mass} = 3.12\ \mathrm{g\ mL^{-1}} \times 1.5\ \mathrm{mL} = 4.68\ \mathrm{g}\).<br/>Apply significant figure rules:<br/>Since \(1.5\ \mathrm{mL}\) has only 2 significant figures, the product must be rounded to 2 significant figures.<br/>Rounding \(4.68\ \mathrm{g}\) to two significant figures gives \(4.7\ \mathrm{g}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>The digit after 6 is 8 (> 5), so 6 rounds up to 7, giving \(4.7\ \mathrm{g}\) with two significant figures.<br/>Final Conceptual Conclusion: The mass in appropriate significant figures is \(4.7\ \mathrm{g}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: প্রদত্ত তথ্য ও শর্তাবলি</strong><br/>দ্রবণের ঘনত্ব, \(d = 3.12\ \mathrm{g\ mL^{-1}}\) (৩টি তাৎপর্যপূর্ণ অঙ্ক সংখ্যা রয়েছে)।<br/>দ্রবণের আয়তন, \(V = 1.5\ \mathrm{mL}\) (২টি তাৎপর্যপূর্ণ অঙ্ক সংখ্যা রয়েছে)।</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of Mass Balance</strong><br/>Reactants side:<br/>Mass of iron = \(4 \times 55.85\ \mathrm{u} \approx 223.4\ \mathrm{u}\).<br/>Mass of oxygen = \(3 \times 32\ \mathrm{u} = 96\ \mathrm{u}\).<br/>Total mass of reactants = \(223.4 + 96 = 319.4\ \mathrm{u}\).<br/>Products side:<br/>Mass of \(2 \mathrm{Fe_2O_3} = 2 \times [(2 \times 55.85) + (3 \times 16)] = 2 \times [111.7 + 48] = 2 \times 159.7 = 319.4\ \mathrm{u}\).<br/>Total mass of reactants equals the total mass of the products.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Principles</strong><br/>According to the Law of Conservation of Mass (formulated by Antoine Lavoisier), matter can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. Therefore, the total mass of reactants must equal the total mass of products in any balanced chemical reaction.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Evaluation of Stoichiometric Constraints</strong><br/>Taking any one reactant in excess does not increase the amount of product if the other reactant is limiting. The limiting reactant determines the maximum amount of product formed.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Final Answer</strong><br/>The equality of total mass of iron and oxygen in the reactants to that in the product demonstrates the law of conservation of mass.<br/>Final Conceptual Conclusion: Total mass of iron and oxygen in reactants = total mass of iron and oxygen in product therefore it follows law of conservation of mass.</p>

    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2026_SOMEBASICCONCEPTSOFCHEMISTRY_MCQ_Q14 - Identifying unbalanced equation violating law of conservation of mass</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Which of the following reactions is not correct according to the law of conservation of mass.</p>
<hr/>
<p>ভরের সংরক্ষণ সূত্র অনুসারে নিম্নলিখিত কোন বিক্রিয়াটি সঠিক নয়?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Governing Principle</strong><br/>The law of conservation of mass states that mass cannot be created or destroyed during a chemical reaction. Therefore, the total number of atoms of each element on the reactant side must equal the total number of atoms of that element on the product side (the chemical equation must be stoichiometrically balanced).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Each Chemical Equation</strong><br/>1. \(2\mathrm{Mg(s)} + \mathrm{O}_2(\mathrm{g}) \longrightarrow 2\mathrm{MgO(s)}\):<br/>Reactants: \(2\ \mathrm{Mg}, 2\ \mathrm{O}\). Products: \(2\ \mathrm{Mg}, 2\ \mathrm{O}\). Balanced.<br/>2. \(\mathrm{C_3H_8(g) + O_2(g) \longrightarrow CO_2(g) + H_2O(g)}\):<br/>Reactants: \(3\ \mathrm{C}, 8\ \mathrm{H}, 2\ \mathrm{O}\). Products: \(1\ \mathrm{C}, 2\ \mathrm{H}, 3\ \mathrm{O}\). The equation is completely unbalanced; atoms and masses on both sides do not match.<br/>3. \(\mathrm{P}_4(\mathrm{s}) + 5\mathrm{O}_2(\mathrm{g}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{P}_4\mathrm{O}_{10}(\mathrm{s})\):<br/>Reactants: \(4\ \mathrm{P}, 10\ \mathrm{O}\). Products: \(4\ \mathrm{P}, 10\ \mathrm{O}\). Balanced.<br/>4. \(\mathrm{CH}_4(\mathrm{g}) + 2\mathrm{O}_2(\mathrm{g}) \longrightarrow \mathrm{CO}_2(\mathrm{g}) + 2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}(\mathrm{g})\):<br/>Reactants: \(1\ \mathrm{C}, 4\ \mathrm{H}, 4\ \mathrm{O}\). Products: \(1\ \mathrm{C}, 4\ \mathrm{H}, 4\ \mathrm{O}\). Balanced.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The reaction \(\mathrm{C_3H_8(g) + O_2(g) \longrightarrow CO_2(g) + H_2O(g)}\) is unbalanced and therefore incorrect according to the law of conservation of mass.<br/>Final Conceptual Conclusion: \(\mathrm{C_3H_8(g) + O_2(g) \longrightarrow CO_2(g) + H_2O(g)}\) is not correct according to the law of conservation of mass.</p>
<hr/>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Definitions of Concentration Terms</strong><br/>1. Molality (\(m\)): Number of moles of solute dissolved per kilogram of solvent. Unit: \(\mathrm{mol\ kg^{-1}}\) (not unitless).<br/>2. Molarity (\(M\)): Number of moles of solute dissolved per litre of solution. Unit: \(\mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\) or \(\mathrm{mol\ dm^{-3}}\) (not unitless).<br/>3. Mole fraction (\(\chi\)): Ratio of the number of moles of one component to the total number of moles of all components present in the solution. Being a ratio of two identical physical quantities (moles / moles), it is dimensionless and unitless.<br/>4. Mass percent: Ratio of the mass of a component to the total mass of the mixture multiplied by 100. Being a ratio of two masses (mass / mass), it is dimensionless and unitless.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis and Identification</strong><br/>Mole fraction and mass percent represent pure numerical ratios without physical dimensions or units.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Mole fraction and mass percent are unitless quantities.<br/>Final Conceptual Conclusion: Mole fraction and Mass percent are unitless.</p>
<hr/>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Dalton's Postulate</strong><br/>The postulate "Compounds are formed when atoms of different elements combine in a fixed ratio" explains the laws governing the composition of compounds by mass:</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Laws Directly Related to the Statement</strong><br/>1. Law of Definite Proportions: States that a given chemical compound always contains its constituent elements in a fixed ratio by mass. This directly corresponds to atoms combining in fixed numerical ratios.<br/>2. Law of Multiple Proportions: States that when elements form more than one compound, the masses of one element combining with a fixed mass of the other are in simple whole-number ratios. This also directly originates from whole atoms combining in different fixed integral ratios.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Laws Not Related to the Statement</strong><br/>1. Law of Conservation of Mass: Originates from a completely different postulate of Dalton's atomic theory, namely: "Atoms can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction." It is not derived from the postulate of fixed atomic ratios in compounds.<br/>2. Avogadro Law: Relates the macroscopic volume of gases at constant temperature and pressure to the number of molecules. Dalton's atomic theory did not consider polyatomic elemental molecules and could not account for Avogadro's law.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The laws not related to the given statement are the Law of conservation of mass and Avogadro law.<br/>Final Conceptual Conclusion: Law of conservation of mass and Avogadro law are not related to this statement.</p>
