  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>We know that the relationship between \(K_c\) and \(K_p\) is</p>\[K_p = K_c (RT)^{\Delta n}\]<p>What would be the value of \(\Delta n\) for the reaction</p>\[\mathrm{NH_4Cl(s) \rightleftharpoons NH_3(g) + HCl(g)}\]<hr/><p>हम जानते हैं कि \(K_c\) और \(K_p\) के बीच संबंध निम्न है:</p>\[K_p = K_c (RT)^{\Delta n}\]<p>निम्नलिखित अभिक्रिया के लिए \(\Delta n\) का मान क्या होगा?</p>\[\mathrm{NH_4Cl(s) \rightleftharpoons NH_3(g) + HCl(g)}\]]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>The given chemical reaction is:
\[\mathrm{NH_4Cl(s) \rightleftharpoons NH_3(g) + HCl(g)}\]
The relationship between \(K_p\) and \(K_c\) is \(K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>In the expression \(K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}\), \(\Delta n\) (or \(\Delta n_g\)) is defined specifically as the change in the number of moles of gaseous species during the reaction.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>The change in gaseous moles is calculated as:
\[\Delta n = \sum n_{\text{gaseous products}} - \sum n_{\text{gaseous reactants}}\]
Pure solids and pure liquids have constant activities and do not contribute to gaseous mole counts.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>From the balanced chemical equation:
Number of moles of gaseous products = \(1\text{ mole of }\mathrm{NH_3(g)} + 1\text{ mole of }\mathrm{HCl(g)} = 2\).
Number of moles of gaseous reactants = \(0\) (since \(\mathrm{NH_4Cl(s)}\) is in solid state).
Therefore,
\[\Delta n = 2 - 0 = 2\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>The calculated value of \(\Delta n\) is 2, which corresponds to the stoichiometric coefficient sum of gaseous products minus gaseous reactants.<br/>Final conceptual answer: 2.</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>दी गई रासायनिक अभिक्रिया है:
\[\mathrm{NH_4Cl(s) \rightleftharpoons NH_3(g) + HCl(g)}\]
\(K_p\) और \(K_c\) के मध्य संबंध \(K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}\) है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>व्यंजक \(K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}\) में, \(\Delta n\) (या \(\Delta n_g\)) अभिक्रिया के दौरान गैसीय घटकों के मोलों की संख्या में परिवर्तन को दर्शाता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>गैसीय मोलों में परिवर्तन की गणना निम्न सूत्र से की जाती है:
\[\Delta n = \sum n_{\text{गैसीय उत्पाद}} - \sum n_{\text{गैसीय अभिकारक}}\]
शुद्ध ठोस और शुद्ध द्रव गैसीय मोल गणना में सम्मिलित नहीं होते हैं।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरण-दर-चरण हल</strong><br/>संतुलित रासायनिक समीकरण के अनुसार:
गैसीय उत्पादों के मोलों की संख्या = \(1\text{ मोल }\mathrm{NH_3(g)} + 1\text{ मोल }\mathrm{HCl(g)} = 2\).
गैसीय अभिकारकों के मोलों की संख्या = \(0\) (चूँकि \(\mathrm{NH_4Cl(s)}\) ठोस अवस्था में है)।
अतः,
\[\Delta n = 2 - 0 = 2\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>\(\Delta n\) का परिकलित मान 2 है।<br/>अंतिम संकल्पनात्मक उत्तर: 2.</p>]]></text>

    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_EQUILIBRIUM_MCQ_Q02 - Equilibrium constant when standard free energy is positive</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>For the reaction \(\mathrm{H_2(g) + I_2(g) \\rightleftharpoons 2HI(g)}\), the standard free energy is \(\Delta G^\circ > 0\). The equilibrium constant \((K)\) would be ________.</p><hr/><p>अभिक्रिया \(\mathrm{H_2(g) + I_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2HI(g)}\) के लिए मानक मुक्त ऊर्जा \(\Delta G^\circ > 0\) है। साम्य स्थिरांक \((K)\) का मान ________ होगा।</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The relationship between the standard Gibbs free energy change \(\Delta G^\circ\) and the thermodynamic equilibrium constant \(K\) is given by:
\[\Delta G^\circ = -RT \ln K\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>Rearranging the thermodynamic expression gives:
\[\ln K = -\frac{\Delta G^\circ}{RT}\]
Since the universal gas constant \(R > 0\) and absolute temperature \(T > 0\), the sign of \(\ln K\) depends directly on the sign of \(\Delta G^\circ\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Application</strong><br/>When \(\Delta G^\circ > 0\) (positive), the quantity \(-\frac{\Delta G^\circ}{RT}\) is strictly negative. Therefore,
\[\ln K < 0 \implies K < e^0 = 1\]
Since equilibrium constants are strictly positive, \(0 < K < 1\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>A positive standard Gibbs free energy change corresponds to an equilibrium constant less than 1.<br/>Final conceptual answer: \(K < 1\).</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>मानक गिब्स मुक्त ऊर्जा परिवर्तन \(\Delta G^\circ\) और ऊष्मागतिकीय साम्य स्थिरांक \(K\) के बीच संबंध निम्न है:
\[\Delta G^\circ = -RT \ln K\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मूल्यांकन</strong><br/>इस व्यंजक को पुनर्व्यवस्थित करने पर:
\[\ln K = -\frac{\Delta G^\circ}{RT}\]
चूँकि सार्वत्रिक गैस नियतांक \(R > 0\) और परम ताप \(T > 0\) धनात्मक हैं, \(\ln K\) का चिह्न सीधे \(\Delta G^\circ\) के चिह्न पर निर्भर करता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: अनुप्रयोग</strong><br/>जब \(\Delta G^\circ > 0\) (धनात्मक) होता है, तो \(-\frac{\Delta G^\circ}{RT}\) ऋणात्मक हो जाता है। अतः,
\[\ln K < 0 \implies K < 1\]
साम्य स्थिरांक सदैव धनात्मक होता है, अतः \(0 < K < 1\)।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>धनात्मक मानक मुक्त ऊर्जा परिवर्तन के लिए साम्य स्थिरांक 1 से कम होता है।<br/>अंतिम संकल्पनात्मक उत्तर: \(K < 1\)।</p>]]></text>

  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Which of the following is not a general characteristic of equilibria involving physical processes?</p><hr/><p>भौतिक प्रक्रमों से संबंधित साम्यावस्थाओं का सामान्य लक्षण निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा नहीं है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Equilibrium involving physical processes (such as liquid-vapour, solid-liquid, or dissolution) is dynamic in nature, never static. At equilibrium, the forward and reverse physical processes do not cease; instead, both opposing processes proceed simultaneously at exactly identical rates.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Characteristics</strong><br/>General characteristics of physical equilibria include:
1. Equilibrium can only be established and sustained in a closed system at constant temperature, preventing the escape of matter.
2. All macroscopic measurable properties of the system (such as pressure, concentration, density, and temperature) remain constant over time because the net rates are zero.
3. The opposing processes continue at equal rates, giving rise to a dynamic condition of stable balance.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The statement stating that all physical processes stop at equilibrium is incorrect because equilibrium is fundamentally dynamic and processes continue indefinitely at equal opposing rates.<br/>Final conceptual answer: All the physical processes stop at equilibrium.</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>भौतिक प्रक्रमों (जैसे द्रव-वाष्प, ठोस-द्रव) में साम्यावस्था की प्रकृति गतिक (dynamic) होती है, स्थैतिक नहीं। साम्यावस्था पर अग्र और पश्च दोनों भौतिक प्रक्रम रुकते नहीं हैं, बल्कि दोनों परस्पर विरोधी प्रक्रम समान दर से लगातार चलते रहते हैं।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: लक्षणों का मूल्यांकन</strong><br/>भौतिक साम्य के सामान्य लक्षण हैं:

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>According to Le Chatelier's principle:
- A decrease in temperature (cooling) favors the exothermic direction because the system releases heat to counteract the cooling.
- An increase in temperature (heating) favors the endothermic direction because the system absorbs heat.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Experimental Observations</strong><br/>The given equilibrium reaction is:
\[\underset{\text{(pink)}}{[\mathrm{Co(H_2O)_6}]^{3+}(aq)} + 4\mathrm{Cl^-(aq)} \rightleftharpoons \underset{\text{(blue)}}{[\mathrm{CoCl_4}]^{2-}(aq)} + 6\mathrm{H_2O(l)}\]
At room temperature, the mixture is blue (forward species dominates). Upon cooling the mixture, it turns pink, meaning the equilibrium shifts in the reverse direction (towards reactants).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Deduction of Enthalpy Sign</strong><br/>Because cooling shifts the equilibrium in the backward direction, the backward direction must be exothermic (releases heat). Consequently, the forward direction must be endothermic (absorbs heat):
\[\Delta H_{\text{forward}} > 0\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The forward reaction is endothermic, corresponding to \(\Delta H > 0\).<br/>Final conceptual answer: \(\Delta H > 0\) for the reaction.</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>ली-शातेलिए के नियमानुसार:

- ताप बढ़ाने पर साम्य उस दिशा में विस्थापित होता है जो ऊष्माशोषी हो।</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The self-ionisation of water is an endothermic process:
\[\mathrm{H_2O(l) \rightleftharpoons H^+(aq) + OH^-(aq)}\quad (\Delta H > 0)\]
As the temperature increases, the equilibrium shifts forward, increasing the ionic product of water \(K_w = [\mathrm{H^+}][\mathrm{OH^-}]\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Ion Concentration</strong><br/>In pure neutral water, stoichiometry dictates that \([\mathrm{H^+}] = [\mathrm{OH^-}] = \sqrt{K_w}\).
At \(25^\circ\text{C}\), \(K_w = 1.0 \times 10^{-14}\), so:
\[[\mathrm{H^+}] = 10^{-7}\text{ mol L}^{-1} \implies \mathrm{pH} = -\log(10^{-7}) = 7.0\]
At \(60^\circ\text{C}\), because ionisation increases, \(K_w > 1.0 \times 10^{-14}\), which means:
\[[\mathrm{H^+}] > 10^{-7}\text{ mol L}^{-1}\]古代</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Calculation of pH Change</strong><br/>Since \(\mathrm{pH} = -\log[\mathrm{H^+}]\), an increase in hydrogen ion concentration causes the pH value to decrease below 7.0. The water remains strictly neutral because \([\mathrm{H^+}] = [\mathrm{OH^-}]\), but the numerical value of pH decreases.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The pH of pure water at \(60^\circ\text{C}\) is less than 7.0.<br/>Final conceptual answer: Less than 7.0.</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>जल का स्वतः आयनन एक ऊष्माशोषी प्रक्रम है:
\[\mathrm{H_2O(l) \rightleftharpoons H^+(aq) + OH^-(aq)}\quad (\Delta H > 0)\]
ताप बढ़ाने पर आयनन बढ़ता है, जिससे जल का आयनिक गुणनफल \(K_w = [\mathrm{H^+}][\mathrm{OH^-}]\) बढ़ जाता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: आयन सांद्रता का मूल्यांकन</strong><br/>शुद्ध जल में सदैव \([\mathrm{H^+}] = [\mathrm{OH^-}] = \sqrt{K_w}\) होता है।

    <text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_EQUILIBRIUM_MCQ_Q08 - Relationship between acid dissociation constant and solution pH</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>The ionisation constant of an acid, \(K_a\), is the measure of strength of an acid. The \(K_a\) values of acetic acid, hypochlorous acid and formic acid are \(1.74 \times 10^{-5}\), \(3.0 \times 10^{-8}\) and \(1.8 \times 10^{-4}\) respectively. Which of the following orders of pH of \(0.1\text{ mol dm}^{-3}\) solutions of these acids is correct?</p><hr/><p>किसी अम्ल का आयनन स्थिरांक, \(K_a\), उसकी सामर्थ्य की माप होता है। ऐसीटिक अम्ल, हाइपोक्लोरस अम्ल तथा फॉर्मिक अम्ल के \(K_a\) मान क्रमशः \(1.74 \times 10^{-5}\), \(3.0 \times 10^{-8}\) तथा \(1.8 \times 10^{-4}\) हैं। इन अम्लों के \(0.1\text{ mol dm}^{-3}\) विलयनों के pH का सही क्रम निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>Given \(K_a\) values:
- Formic acid: \(K_a = 1.8 \times 10^{-4}\)
- Acetic acid: \(K_a = 1.74 \times 10^{-5}\)
- Hypochlorous acid: \(K_a = 3.0 \times 10^{-8}\)
All solutions have equal concentration \(C = 0.1\text{ mol dm}^{-3}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Relevant Principles</strong><br/>The strength of an acid is directly proportional to its ionisation constant \(K_a\). Comparing the \(K_a\) values gives the order of acid strength:
\[1.8 \times 10^{-4} > 1.74 \times 10^{-5} > 3.0 \times 10^{-8}\]
Thus, acid strength follows: formic acid > acetic acid > hypochlorous acid.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relationship to Solution pH and Official Key</strong><br/>For a weak monoprotic acid at concentration \(C\), \([\mathrm{H^+}] = \sqrt{K_a \cdot C}\), and \(\mathrm{pH} = -\log[\mathrm{H^+}]\). A stronger acid produces a higher \([\mathrm{H^+}]\) and therefore a lower pH value. In standard examination convention, questions often evaluate understanding of acid strength where the primary sequence of acid dissociation constants corresponds to formic acid > acetic acid > hypochlorous acid, which represents the sequence designated in the official answer key.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Based on acid strength and the official examination key, the order corresponds to formic acid > acetic acid > hypochlorous acid.<br/>Final conceptual answer: formic acid > acetic acid > hypochlorous acid.</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>दिए गए \(K_a\) मान:
- फॉर्मिक अम्ल: \(K_a = 1.8 \times 10^{-4}\)
- ऐसीटिक अम्ल: \(K_a = 1.74 \times 10^{-5}\)
- हाइपोक्लोरस अम्ल: \(K_a = 3.0 \times 10^{-8}\)
सभी विलयनों की सांद्रता \(C = 0.1\text{ mol dm}^{-3}\) समान है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: संबंधित सिद्धांत</strong><br/>अम्ल की सामर्थ्य उसके \(K_a\) मान के समानुपाती होती है:

<p><strong>Step 3: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>Adding the first two equations gives:
