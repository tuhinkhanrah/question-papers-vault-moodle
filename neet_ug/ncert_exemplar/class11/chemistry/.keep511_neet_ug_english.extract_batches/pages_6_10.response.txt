Thus, the overall aluminosilicate framework acquires a negative charge, making the Assertion true and the Reason its correct explanation.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are correct, and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A).</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>
