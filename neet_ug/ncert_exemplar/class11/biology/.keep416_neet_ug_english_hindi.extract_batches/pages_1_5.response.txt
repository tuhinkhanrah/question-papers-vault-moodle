  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Intestinal villi are small finger-like foldings of the mucosa layer in the small intestine that are specialized for maximizing the absorption of digested nutrients.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>The epithelial cells lining the villi possess numerous microscopic projections called microvilli, giving a brush-border appearance. Together, villi and microvilli enormously increase the surface area for absorption. Each villus is supplied with a network of blood capillaries and a large lymph vessel called the lacteal, which absorbs fatty acids and glycerol.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Intestinal villi participate in the absorption of all major digested nutrients (monosaccharides, amino acids, fatty acids, glycerol, water, and electrolytes), not exclusively in the digestion of fats. Therefore, the statement that they only participate in digestion of fats is false.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Therefore, the hepato-pancreatic duct carries both bile and pancreatic juice into the duodenum.</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: अनुप्रयोग</strong><br/>चूँकि यह यकृत-पित्त प्रणाली और बहिःस्रावी अग्नाशय दोनों से स्राव प्राप्त करती है, यह पित्त और अग्नाशयी रस दोनों को एक साथ ग्रहणी में ले जाती है, जहाँ इसका द्वार ओडी की अवरोधिनी (sphincter of Oddi) द्वारा नियंत्रित होता है।</p>

  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>Which of the following is not a common disorder associated with digestive system?</p><hr/><p>निम्नलिखित में से कौन पाचन तंत्र से जुड़ा एक सामान्य विकार नहीं है?</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Common disorders of the human digestive system include jaundice, vomiting, diarrhoea, constipation, indigestion, and dysentery.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>Diarrhoea (abnormal frequency of bowel movement and increased liquidity of faecal discharge), jaundice (liver affected, skin and eyes turn yellow due to bile pigments), and dysentery (intestinal inflammation causing severe diarrhoea with blood/mucus) are all digestive system disorders. Tetanus, however, is a bacterial infection caused by <em>Clostridium tetani</em> that produces a neurotoxin affecting the nervous and muscular systems (causing sustained muscle spasms).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Thus, tetanus is not a disorder associated with the digestive system.</p>

<p><strong>चरण 1: अवधारणात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>मानव पाचन तंत्र के सामान्य विकारों में पीलिया, वमन, अतिसार (डायरिया), कब्ज, अपच और पेचिश शामिल हैं।</p>

  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>A gland not associated with the alimentary canal is</p><hr/><p>आहार नाल से संबंधित न होने वाली ग्रंथि है</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The digestive glands associated with the alimentary canal include the salivary glands, the liver, and the pancreas.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>Salivary glands secrete saliva into the buccal cavity; the liver secretes bile; and the pancreas secretes pancreatic juice into the duodenum. In contrast, the adrenal glands are endocrine glands situated at the anterior part of each kidney and secrete hormones such as adrenaline, cortisol, and aldosterone, having no direct digestive role in the alimentary canal.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Therefore, the adrenal gland is not associated with the alimentary canal.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: अवधारणात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>आहार नाल से संबंधित पाचन ग्रंथियों में लार ग्रंथियाँ, यकृत और अग्नाशय शामिल हैं।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>अतः अधिवृक्क (adrenal) ग्रंथि आहार नाल से संबंधित नहीं है।</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>
