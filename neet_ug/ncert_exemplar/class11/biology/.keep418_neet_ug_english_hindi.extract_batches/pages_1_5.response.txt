  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>Phagocytosis is the cellular process of engulfing and digesting cellular debris, foreign particles, and invading microbes. In circulating human blood, neutrophils and monocytes are the primary phagocytic leukocytes. When monocytes migrate from the bloodstream into surrounding tissues, they enlarge and differentiate into macrophages, which possess pronounced phagocytic capability.<br/>Basophils are non-phagocytic granulocytes that synthesize and store pharmacologically active mediators, notably histamine, serotonin, and heparin. Their primary physiological role is the mediation of inflammatory reactions and allergic responses rather than particle engulfment.</p><p><strong>Evaluation:</strong><br/>Monocytes, neutrophils, and macrophages all actively engage in phagocytosis to eliminate pathogens.     <text><![CDATA[<p>Which among the followings is correct during each cardiac cycle?</p><hr/><p>प्रत्येक हृदय चक्र के दौरान निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा सही है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>A cardiac cycle comprises the complete mechanical sequence of diastole and systole within all four chambers of the heart. During ventricular systole, each ventricle contracts and ejects blood into its respective outflow vessel: the right ventricle ejects blood into the pulmonary trunk, while the left ventricle ejects blood into the systemic aorta. The volume of blood ejected by each ventricle during each contraction is defined as the stroke volume, normally averaging about 70 mL in a resting adult.</p><p><strong>Evaluation:</strong><br/>Because the systemic and pulmonary circulatory circuits operate in series, the volume of blood pumped by the right ventricle must equal the volume of blood pumped by the left ventricle per beat over time. Any disparity would rapidly lead to catastrophic fluid accumulation either in the pulmonary vasculature or in systemic capillary beds.</p><p><strong>Final Answer:</strong><br/>The volume of blood pumped out by the Rt and Lt ventricles is same.</p><hr/><p><strong>संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत:</strong><br/>हृदय चक्र हृदय के चारों कक्षों में प्रकुंचन (सिस्टोल) और अनुशिथिलन (डायस्टोल) की एक पूर्ण शृंखला है। निलय प्रकुंचन के दौरान, दायाँ निलय फुफ्फुस धमनी में तथा बायाँ निलय महाधमनी में रक्त पंप करता है। प्रत्येक धड़कन में प्रत्येक निलय द्वारा बाहर निकाले गए रक्त के आयतन को स्ट्रोक आयतन कहते हैं, जो विश्राम अवस्था में लगभग 70 mL होता है।</p><p><strong>मूल्यांकन:</strong><br/>चूँकि दैहिक और फुफ्फुसीय परिसंचरण एक शृंखला में जुड़े होते हैं, अतः दोनों निलयों द्वारा प्रत्येक चक्र में पंप किए गए रक्त का आयतन बिल्कुल समान होना अनिवार्य है, अन्यथा किसी एक परिसंचरण में रक्त का असामान्य जमाव हो जाएगा।</p><p><strong>अंतिम उत्तर:</strong><br/>दाएं और बाएं निलय द्वारा पंप किए गए रक्त का आयतन समान होता है। (The volume of blood pumped out by the Rt and Lt ventricles is same.)</p>]]></text>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>Intrinsic nodal rhythmicity can be moderated by neural centers situated in the medulla oblongata via the sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions of the autonomic nervous system.<br/>Sympathetic nerves innervate the sinoatrial node, atrioventricular node, and ventricular myocardium. Postganglionic sympathetic fibers release norepinephrine, which enhances the rate of depolarisation at the pacemaker to accelerate heart rate, strengthens ventricular contractility, elevates stroke volume, and ultimately boosts total cardiac output.<br/>In contrast, parasympathetic innervation (via the vagus nerve) releases acetylcholine, which slows the rate of action potential initiation at the sinoatrial node, reduces conduction velocity, and decreases both heart rate and cardiac output.</p><p><strong>Evaluation:</strong><br/>The sympathetic division increases both chronotropic (rate) and inotropic (contractility/stroke volume) parameters, thereby stimulating both heart rate and stroke volume.   <generalfeedback format="html">
