  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Meiosis is a specialized form of cell division occurring in diploid germ cells during sexual reproduction. It comprises two successive nuclear and cytoplasmic divisions following a single cycle of DNA replication.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>During meiosis, reduction division reduces the chromosome number by half from diploid (\(2n\)) to haploid (\(n\)), resulting in the formation of haploid gametes. Furthermore, crossing over between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes during pachytene of prophase I and independent assortment during metaphase I introduce genetic variations into the daughter cells. Thus, meiosis leads to gamete formation, halving of chromosome number, and introduction of genetic variations.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Since meiosis results in gamete production, chromosome number reduction, and genetic variation, all of the listed phenomena are valid consequences in diploid organisms.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: ধারণাগত নীতি</strong><br/>মিয়োসিস হলো একটি বিশেষ ধরনের কোষ বিভাজন যা যৌন জননের সময় ডিপ্লয়েড জনন মাতৃকোষে সম্পন্ন হয়। এতে ডিএনএ প্রতিলিপিকরণের একটিমাত্র চক্রের পর পরপর দুটি নিউক্লিয়াস ও সাইটোপ্লাজম বিভাজন ঘটে।</p>

    ]]></text>

  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>During meiosis, exchange of genetic material occurs via crossing over during pachytene of prophase I, generating recombinant non-sister chromatids.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>In anaphase I, homologous chromosomes separate, but each chromosome still consists of two chromatids joined at their centromere that may differ genetically due to crossing over. In anaphase II, centromeres split and sister chromatids finally segregate to opposite poles as individual daughter chromosomes. It is this segregation of sister chromatids during anaphase II that determines the unique and final genetic constitution of each resulting haploid gamete.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The final genetic constitution of each gamete is determined when sister chromatids segregate during anaphase II.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: ধারণাগত নীতি</strong><br/>মিয়োসিসের প্রফেজ I-এর প্যাকাইটিন উপদশায় ক্রসিং ওভারের মাধ্যমে সমসংস্থ ক্রোমোজোমের নন-সিস্টার ক্রোমাটিডের মধ্যে খণ্ডাংশের বিনিময় ঘটে এবং পুনর্যোজিত ক্রোমাটিড সৃষ্টি হয়।</p>

    <text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_MCQ_Q05 - Characterisation of mitotic division in cells</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>Mitosis is characterised by</p>
<hr/>
<p>মাইটোসিসের বৈশিষ্ট্য হলো</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Mitosis is an equational division in which a parent cell divides to produce two daughter cells that have chromosome numbers and genetic content identical to each other and to the parent cell.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>Because duplicated sister chromatids segregate equally into two daughter nuclei, the chromosome number is strictly preserved. In contrast, reduction division and the pairing of homologous chromosomes (synapsis) are unique defining features of meiosis.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Mitosis is fundamentally characterised by equal (equational) division.</p>

<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The \(G_1\) phase corresponds to the interval between mitosis and the initiation of DNA replication in the cell cycle.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>During \(G_1\), the cell is metabolically active, undergoes continuous growth, and synthesizes proteins and RNAs needed for subsequent cellular processes. However, replication of the cell's nuclear DNA does not take place during \(G_1\); DNA replication is restricted to the subsequent S phase.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The valid statement is that DNA in the cell does not replicate during the \(G_1\) phase.</p>
