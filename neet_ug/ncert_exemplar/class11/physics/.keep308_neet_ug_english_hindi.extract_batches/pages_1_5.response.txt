  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>Satellites orbiting the earth have finite life and sometimes debris of satellites fall to the earth. This is because,</p>
<hr/>
<p>पृथ्वी की परिक्रमा करने वाले उपग्रहों का जीवनकाल सीमित होता है और कभी-कभी उपग्रहों का मलबा पृथ्वी पर गिर जाता है। ऐसा इसलिए होता है क्योंकि,</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Physical System and Atmospheric Conditions</strong><br/>Although the upper atmosphere is extremely tenuous, it possesses a non-zero density of air molecules. An orbiting satellite encounters atmospheric friction (viscous drag forces) along its orbital trajectory.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Energy Dissipation Mechanism</strong><br/>The total mechanical energy of a satellite of mass \(m\) orbiting Earth (mass \(M\)) at radius \(r\) is given by \(E = -\frac{G M m}{2r}\). Viscous drag does negative work on the satellite, continually dissipating its total mechanical energy as heat.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Orbital Altitude Reduction</strong><br/>As the total energy \(E\) becomes more negative, the orbital radius \(r\) must decrease.
