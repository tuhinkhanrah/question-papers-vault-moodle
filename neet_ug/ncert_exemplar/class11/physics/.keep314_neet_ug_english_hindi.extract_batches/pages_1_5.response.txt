  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2026_OSCILLATIONS_MCQ_Q14_01 - Nature and period of harmonic particle motion</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>The displacement of a particle is represented by the equation</p>
<p>\[y = 3 \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - 2\omega t \right)\]</p>
<p>The motion of the particle is</p>
<hr/>
<p>किसी कण के विस्थापन को निम्नलिखित समीकरण द्वारा निरूपित किया जाता है:</p>
<p>\[y = 3 \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - 2\omega t \right)\]</p>
<p>कण की गति है:</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
The given displacement of the particle as a function of time \(t\) is:
\[y(t) = 3 \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - 2\omega t \right)\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
Since the cosine function is an even function, \(\cos(-\theta) = \cos(\theta)\), the expression can be rewritten as:
\[y(t) = 3 \cos \left( 2\omega t - \frac{\pi}{4} \right)\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
A motion is simple harmonic if the acceleration is directly proportional to the displacement and directed opposite to it:
\[\frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -\omega'^2 y\]
For a general simple harmonic motion of the form \(y(t) = A \cos(\omega' t + \phi)\), the angular frequency is \(\omega'\), and the time period of oscillation is:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega'}\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Comparing \(y(t) = 3 \cos \left( 2\omega t - \frac{\pi}{4} \right)\) with the standard equation \(y(t) = A \cos(\omega' t + \phi)\):
Amplitude \(A = 3\)
Angular frequency \(\omega' = 2\omega\)
Initial phase \(\phi = -\frac{\pi}{4}\)
Differentiating with respect to time gives the velocity:
\[v(t) = \frac{dy}{dt} = -6\omega \sin \left( 2\omega t - \frac{\pi}{4} \right)\]
Differentiating again gives the acceleration:
\[a(t) = \frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -12\omega^2 \cos \left( 2\omega t - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = -(2\omega)^2 y = -4\omega^2 y\]
Because the acceleration satisfies \(a = -\omega'^2 y\) with \(\omega' = 2\omega\), the motion is simple harmonic motion.
The time period of oscillation is:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega'} = \frac{2\pi}{2\omega} = \frac{\pi}{\omega}\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The acceleration is directly proportional to the negative of displacement, confirming simple harmonic motion, and the period is \(\frac{\pi}{\omega}\).
Therefore, the motion of the particle is simple harmonic with period \(\pi/\omega\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आंकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>
समय \(t\) के फलन के रूप में कण का दिया गया विस्थापन समीकरण है:
\[y(t) = 3 \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - 2\omega t \right)\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी</strong><br/>
चूँकि कोज्या (cosine) एक सम फलन है, अर्थात् \(\cos(-\theta) = \cos(\theta)\), अतः विस्थापन को इस प्रकार लिखा जा सकता है:
\[y(t) = 3 \cos \left( 2\omega t - \frac{\pi}{4} \right)\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
कोई गति सरल आवर्त गति (SHM) होती है यदि त्वरण विस्थापन के समानुपाती और उसकी दिशा के विपरीत हो:
\[\frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -\omega'^2 y\]
मानक सरल आवर्त समीकरण \(y(t) = A \cos(\omega' t + \phi)\) के लिए कोणीय आवृत्ति \(\omega'\) होती है, तथा आवर्तकाल निम्न सूत्र से प्राप्त होता है:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega'}\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
दिए गए समीकरण की मानक समीकरण \(y(t) = A \cos(\omega' t + \phi)\) से तुलना करने पर:
आयाम \(A = 3\)
कोणीय आवृत्ति \(\omega' = 2\omega\)
प्रारंभिक कला \(\phi = -\frac{\pi}{4}\)
समय के सापेक्ष अवकलन करने पर वेग प्राप्त होता है:
\[v(t) = \frac{dy}{dt} = -6\omega \sin \left( 2\omega t - \frac{\pi}{4} \right)\]
पुनः अवकलन करने पर त्वरण प्राप्त होता है:
\[a(t) = \frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -12\omega^2 \cos \left( 2\omega t - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = -(2\omega)^2 y = -4\omega^2 y\]
चूँकि त्वरण विस्थापन के ऋणात्मक मान के समानुपाती है (\(a \propto -y\)), अतः यह गति सरल आवर्त गति है।
दोलन का आवर्तकाल है:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega'} = \frac{2\pi}{2\omega} = \frac{\pi}{\omega}\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जांच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
त्वरण विस्थापन के सीधे समानुपाती और विपरीत दिशा में है, जो सरल आवर्त गति की पुष्टि करता है, तथा इसका आवर्तकाल \(\frac{\pi}{\omega}\) है।
अतः कण की गति \(\pi/\omega\) आवर्तकाल के साथ सरल आवर्त गति है।</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2026_OSCILLATIONS_MCQ_Q14_02 - Periodicity of cubic sine displacement motion</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>The displacement of a particle is represented by the equation</p>
<p>\[y = \sin^3 \omega t\]</p>
<p>The motion is</p>
<hr/>
<p>किसी कण के विस्थापन को निम्नलिखित समीकरण द्वारा निरूपित किया जाता है:</p>
<p>\[y = \sin^3 \omega t\]</p>
<p>यह गति है:</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
The displacement equation of the particle is:
\[y(t) = \sin^3 \omega t\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
We use standard trigonometric identities to expand the cube of the sine function into linear harmonic terms.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Using the trigonometric identity:
\[\sin(3\theta) = 3\sin\theta - 4\sin^3\theta \implies \sin^3\theta = \frac{1}{4}(3\sin\theta - \sin 3\theta)\]
For motion to be simple harmonic, the restoring acceleration must satisfy \(\frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -\omega'^2 y\) for a single fixed frequency.
For a motion composed of harmonic terms with angular frequencies \(\omega\) and \(3\omega\), the period of the resultant motion is determined by the fundamental angular frequency \(\omega\):
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Substituting \(\theta = \omega t\):
\[y(t) = \frac{3}{4}\sin\omega t - \frac{1}{4}\sin 3\omega t\]
Finding the velocity:
\[v(t) = \frac{dy}{dt} = \frac{3}{4}\omega\cos\omega t - \frac{3}{4}\omega\cos 3\omega t\]
Finding the acceleration:
\[a(t) = \frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -\frac{3}{4}\omega^2\sin\omega t + \frac{9}{4}\omega^2\sin 3\omega t\]
Because \(a(t)\) cannot be written in the form \(-\omega'^2 y(t)\), the acceleration is not proportional to the displacement. Hence, the motion is not simple harmonic.
However, both terms \(\sin\omega t\) and \(\sin 3\omega t\) are periodic. The period of \(\sin\omega t\) is \(T_1 = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\) and the period of \(\sin 3\omega t\) is \(T_2 = \frac{2\pi}{3\omega}\).
The common period is the least common multiple of \(T_1\) and \(T_2\), which is:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\]
Since \(y(t + 2\pi/\omega) = y(t)\), the function repeats itself at regular intervals.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The motion repeats identically after every interval of \(2\pi/\omega\), but acceleration is not proportional to \(-y\).
Therefore, the motion is periodic but not simple harmonic.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आंकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>
कण का विस्थापन समीकरण दिया गया है:
\[y(t) = \sin^3 \omega t\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी</strong><br/>
त्रिकोणमितीय सर्वसमिका का उपयोग करके घन पद को एकल घात वाले आवर्ती पदों में व्यक्त करते हैं।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
त्रिकोणमितीय सर्वसमिका के अनुसार:
\[\sin(3\theta) = 3\sin\theta - 4\sin^3\theta \implies \sin^3\theta = \frac{1}{4}(3\sin\theta - \sin 3\theta)\]
सरल आवर्त गति के लिए त्वरण सदैव विस्थापन के ऋणात्मक मान के समानुपाती होना चाहिए:
\[\frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -\omega'^2 y\]
यदि गति \(\omega\) और \(3\omega\) आवृत्तियों के संयोजनों से बनी हो, तो परिणामी आवर्तकाल मूल आवृत्ति \(\omega\) द्वारा निर्धारित होता है:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
\(\theta = \omega t\) रखने पर:
\[y(t) = \frac{3}{4}\sin\omega t - \frac{1}{4}\sin 3\omega t\]
समय के सापेक्ष प्रथम अवकलन करने पर वेग:
\[v(t) = \frac{dy}{dt} = \frac{3}{4}\omega\cos\omega t - \frac{3}{4}\omega\cos 3\omega t\]
पुनः अवकलन करने पर त्वरण:
\[a(t) = \frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -\frac{3}{4}\omega^2\sin\omega t + \frac{9}{4}\omega^2\sin 3\omega t\]
चूँकि त्वरण \(a(t)\) को \(-\omega'^2 y(t)\) के रूप में नहीं लिखा जा सकता, अतः त्वरण विस्थापन के समानुपाती नहीं है। इसलिए यह गति सरल आवर्त गति नहीं है।
परन्तु \(\sin\omega t\) का आवर्तकाल \(\frac{2\pi}{\omega}\) है और \(\sin 3\omega t\) का आवर्तकाल \(\frac{2\pi}{3\omega}\) है।
दोनों का उभयनिष्ठ आवर्तकाल \(T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\) है। अतः विस्थापन एक निश्चित समय बाद स्वयं को दोहराता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जांच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
कण की गति प्रत्येक \(2\pi/\omega\) समय अंतराल के बाद दोहराई जाती है, परन्तु त्वरण \(-y\) के समानुपाती नहीं है।
अतः यह गति आवर्ती है परन्तु सरल आवर्त नहीं है।</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2026_OSCILLATIONS_MCQ_Q14_03 - Identification of simple harmonic motion relation</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>The relation between acceleration and displacement of four particles are given below:</p>
<p>(a) \(a_x = +2x\)<br/>
(b) \(a_x = +2x^2\)<br/>
(c) \(a_x = -2x^2\)<br/>
(d) \(a_x = -2x\)</p>
<p>Which one of the particles is executing simple harmonic motion?</p>
<hr/>
<p>चार कणों के त्वरण और विस्थापन के बीच संबंध नीचे दिए गए हैं:</p>
<p>(a) \(a_x = +2x\)<br/>
(b) \(a_x = +2x^2\)<br/>
(c) \(a_x = -2x^2\)<br/>
(d) \(a_x = -2x\)</p>
<p>इनमें से कौन-सा कण सरल आवर्त गति कर रहा है?</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Four relations between acceleration \(a_x\) and displacement \(x\) along the x-axis are provided:
\(a_x = +2x\)
\(a_x = +2x^2\)
\(a_x = -2x^2\)
\(a_x = -2x\)</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The motion is one-dimensional along the x-axis with the origin \(x = 0\) as the equilibrium position. A positive displacement means the particle is to the right of the mean position, and a negative displacement means it is to the left.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The defining condition for simple harmonic motion is that the acceleration must be directly proportional to the displacement from the equilibrium position and directed towards the equilibrium position:
\[a_x = -\omega^2 x\]
where \(\omega^2\) is a positive constant representing the square of the angular frequency.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Evaluating each functional relationship against the condition \(a_x = -\omega^2 x\):
For \(a_x = +2x\), the acceleration has the same sign as the displacement, which means the force is directed away from equilibrium (unstable repulsive force), so it is not simple harmonic motion.
For \(a_x = +2x^2\), the acceleration depends on the square of displacement rather than being linear, so it is not simple harmonic motion.
For \(a_x = -2x^2\), the acceleration is non-linear and does not change sign when \(x\) changes sign, so it is not simple harmonic motion.
For \(a_x = -2x\), the acceleration is linear in displacement and opposite in sign to displacement, matching \(a_x = -\omega^2 x\) with \(\omega = \sqrt{2}\text{ rad/s}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Only the relation \(a_x = -2x\) satisfies the defining differential equation of simple harmonic motion.
Therefore, the particle with acceleration relation \(a_x = -2x\) executes simple harmonic motion.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आंकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>
x-अक्ष के अनुदिश त्वरण \(a_x\) और विस्थापन \(x\) के बीच चार संबंध दिए गए हैं:
\(a_x = +2x\)
\(a_x = +2x^2\)
\(a_x = -2x^2\)
\(a_x = -2x\)</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी</strong><br/>
गति x-अक्ष के अनुदिश एकविमीय है जहाँ मूलबिंदु \(x = 0\) साम्यावस्था है। धनात्मक विस्थापन साम्य के दाईं ओर और ऋणात्मक विस्थापन बाईं ओर की स्थिति को दर्शाता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
सरल आवर्त गति की मूलभूत शर्त यह है कि त्वरण सदैव साम्यावस्था से विस्थापन के सीधे समानुपाती और साम्यावस्था की ओर निर्देशित (विपरीत दिशा में) होना चाहिए:
\[a_x = -\omega^2 x\]
जहाँ \(\omega^2\) एक धनात्मक नियतांक है जो कोणीय आवृत्ति के वर्ग को व्यक्त करता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
प्रत्येक संबंध की \(a_x = -\omega^2 x\) से तुलना करने पर:
\(a_x = +2x\) में त्वरण विस्थापन की ही दिशा में है (प्रतिकर्षी बल), अतः यह सरल आवर्त गति नहीं है।
\(a_x = +2x^2\) में त्वरण विस्थापन के वर्ग पर निर्भर करता है, अतः यह सरल आवर्त गति नहीं है।
\(a_x = -2x^2\) में त्वरण अरैखिक है और विस्थापन की दिशा बदलने पर चिन्ह नहीं बदलता, अतः यह सरल आवर्त गति नहीं है।
\(a_x = -2x\) में त्वरण विस्थापन के रैखिक रूप से समानुपाती है तथा दिशा विपरीत है, जो \(\omega = \sqrt{2}\text{ rad/s}\) के साथ \(a_x = -\omega^2 x\) के पूर्णतः अनुरूप है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जांच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
केवल संबंध \(a_x = -2x\) ही सरल आवर्त गति की परिभाषा को संतुष्ट करता है।
अतः \(a_x = -2x\) त्वरण वाला कण सरल आवर्त गति कर रहा है।</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
A liquid of density \(\rho\) is contained in a vertical U-tube of uniform cross-sectional area \(A\). Total length of the liquid column is \(L\), and the initial equilibrium height of the liquid in each arm is \(h = L/2\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The liquid is incompressible and non-viscous. When the liquid column is depressed by a displacement \(y\) in one arm, it rises by \(y\) in the other arm.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The difference in liquid levels between the two limbs is \(2y\).
The restoring force due to the excess weight of the liquid column of height \(2y\) is:
\[F = -(\text{mass of excess column}) \times g = -(A \cdot 2y \cdot \rho) g = -2A\rho g y\]
The total mass of the liquid column in the U-tube is:
\[m = A L \rho\]
Newton's second law gives:
\[m \frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -2A\rho g y \implies \frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -\frac{2A\rho g}{AL\rho} y = -\frac{2g}{L} y\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
The acceleration equation is:
\[a = -\frac{2g}{L} y = -\frac{g}{h} y\]
Because the restoring acceleration is directly proportional to displacement \(y\) and directed opposite to it, the motion is simple harmonic motion.
The angular frequency is \(\omega = \sqrt{\frac{2g}{L}} = \sqrt{\frac{g}{h}}\).
The time period of oscillation is:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{2g}} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{h}{g}}\]
Notice that the density \(\rho\) cancels out completely from the equation of motion.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The resulting differential equation is linear in \(y\), proving simple harmonic motion, and the time period expression contains only length and gravitational acceleration, being completely independent of liquid density.
Therefore, the motion is simple harmonic and the time period is independent of the density of the liquid.</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
The parametric equations describing the position coordinates of the particle are:
\[x(t) = a \cos \omega t\]
\[y(t) = a \sin \omega t\]
Both motions have the same amplitude \(a\) and angular frequency \(\omega\), and are mutually perpendicular.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
Cartesian coordinates \((x, y)\) define the plane of motion with the origin \((0, 0)\) as the center of oscillation.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The equation of the trajectory is obtained by eliminating the time parameter \(t\) between the expressions for \(x(t)\) and \(y(t)\) using the fundamental trigonometric identity:
\[\cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta = 1\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
From the given equations:
\[\frac{x}{a} = \cos \omega t\]
\[\frac{y}{a} = \sin \omega t\]
Squaring and adding both equations:
\[\left(\frac{x}{a}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{y}{a}\right)^2 = \cos^2 \omega t + \sin^2 \omega t\]
\[\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{a^2} = 1 \implies x^2 + y^2 = a^2\]
This is the standard equation of a circle centered at the origin with radius \(a\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The sum of squares of the coordinates is constant for all times, meaning the distance from the origin remains fixed at \(a\).
Therefore, the trajectory of motion of the particle will be a circle.</p>

\[\left(\frac{x}{a}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{y}{a}\right)^2 = \cos^2 \omega t + \sin^2 \omega t\]

<p>\[y = a \sin \omega t + b \cos \omega t\]</p>
<hr/>
<p>किसी कण का विस्थापन समय के साथ निम्नलिखित संबंध के अनुसार परिवर्तित होता है:</p>
<p>\[y = a \sin \omega t + b \cos \omega t\]</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
The displacement of the particle is given by the linear combination of two harmonic functions:
\[y(t) = a \sin \omega t + b \cos \omega t\]
where \(a\) and \(b\) are constants and \(\omega\) is the angular frequency.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
We express the constants \(a\) and \(b\) in terms of an amplitude \(A\) and a phase angle \(\phi\) such that:
\(a = A \cos \phi\) and \(b = A \sin \phi\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
Using the addition identity:
\[\sin(\omega t + \phi) = \sin\omega t \cos\phi + \cos\omega t \sin\phi\]
The resultant amplitude \(A\) is given by:
\[A^2 = (A\cos\phi)^2 + (A\sin\phi)^2 = a^2 + b^2 \implies A = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\]
The phase angle satisfies \(\tan\phi = b/a\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Substitute \(a = A \cos \phi\) and \(b = A \sin \phi\) into the displacement relation:
\[y(t) = A \cos\phi \sin\omega t + A \sin\phi \cos\omega t = A \sin(\omega t + \phi)\]
where:
\[A = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\]
Differentiating \(y(t)\) twice with respect to time:
\[\frac{dy}{dt} = A \omega \cos(\omega t + \phi)\]
\[\frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -A \omega^2 \sin(\omega t + \phi) = -\omega^2 y\]
Since the acceleration is directly proportional to \(-y\), the resulting motion is simple harmonic motion with amplitude \(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The superposition of two simple harmonic motions of identical frequency along the same line produces simple harmonic motion whose amplitude equals the vector hypotenuse \(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\).
Therefore, the motion is S.H.M. with amplitude \(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\).</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
त्रिकोणमितीय योग सूत्र के अनुसार:
\[\sin(\omega t + \phi) = \sin\omega t \cos\phi + \cos\omega t \sin\phi\]
परिणामी आयाम \(A\) का मान निम्न प्रकार प्राप्त होता है:
\[A = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\]
तथा कला कोण \(\tan\phi = b/a\) होता है।</p>

\[y(t) = A \cos\phi \sin\omega t + A \sin\phi \cos\omega t = A \sin(\omega t + \phi)\]
जहाँ:
\[A = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\]
समय के सापेक्ष दो बार अवकलन करने पर:
\[\frac{d^2y}{dt^2} = -\omega^2 A \sin(\omega t + \phi) = -\omega^2 y\]
चूँकि त्वरण विस्थापन के ऋणात्मक मान के समानुपाती है, अतः गति सरल आवर्त गति है जिसका आयाम \(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\) है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जांच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
समान आवृत्ति की दो परस्पर लंबवत कलाओं वाली गतियों का संयोजन सरल आवर्त गति उत्पन्न करता है जिसका आयाम \(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\) होता है।

\(l_D > l_A\)

<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The horizontal support is elastic and couples the vibrations of the pendulums. The energy of pendulum A is transmitted through the elastic support to pendulums B, C, and D as a periodic driving force of frequency equal to the natural frequency of pendulum A.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The natural time period and natural frequency of a simple pendulum of length \(l\) are:
\[T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}, \quad \nu = \frac{1}{2\pi} \sqrt{\frac{g}{l}}\]
When a system is subjected to an external periodic driving force whose frequency equals its own natural frequency, the phenomenon of resonance occurs, leading to a maximum amplitude of vibration.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Because pendulum A and pendulum C have identical lengths (\(l_A = l_C\)), their natural frequencies of vibration are identical:
\[\nu_A = \nu_C\]
The periodic transverse disturbance from pendulum A acts as an external driving force on the remaining pendulums.
Pendulums B and D have natural frequencies different from that of pendulum A because their lengths differ (\(\nu_B > \nu_A\) and \(\nu_D < \nu_A\)), so they undergo forced oscillations with relatively small amplitudes.
In contrast, pendulum C is driven exactly at its natural resonant frequency (\(\nu_{\text{driver}} = \nu_C\)). As a result, resonance occurs in pendulum C, causing a substantial transfer of energy from pendulum A to pendulum C.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Due to matching lengths and frequencies, resonance occurs exclusively for pendulum C, making its vibration amplitude the largest among all driven pendulums.
Therefore, pendulum C will vibrate with maximum amplitude.</p>

\(l_A = l_C\)
\(l_B < l_A\)
\(l_D > l_A\)

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
\(l\) लंबाई वाले सरल लोलक का स्वाभाविक आवर्तकाल और आवृत्ति निम्न प्रकार व्यक्त की जाती है:
\[T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}, \quad \nu = \frac{1}{2\pi} \sqrt{\frac{g}{l}}\]
जब किसी निकाय पर उसकी स्वाभाविक आवृत्ति के बराबर आवृत्ति वाला बाह्य आवर्ती चालक बल लगाया जाता है, तो अनुनाद की स्थिति उत्पन्न होती है, जिससे दोलन का आयाम अधिकतम हो जाता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
चूँकि लोलक A और C की लंबाइयाँ समान हैं (\(l_A = l_C\)), अतः उनकी स्वाभाविक आवृत्तियाँ भी समान हैं:
\[\nu_A = \nu_C\]
लोलक A के कंपन अन्य लोलकों के लिए बाह्य चालक बल का कार्य करते हैं।

अतः लोलक C अधिकतम आयाम से कंपन करेगा।</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>The equation of motion of a particle is \(x = a \cos (\alpha t)^2\). The motion is</p>
<hr/>
<p>किसी कण की गति का समीकरण \(x = a \cos (\alpha t)^2\) है। यह गति है:</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
The position coordinate of the particle as a function of time is given by:
\[x(t) = a \cos (\alpha^2 t^2)\]
where \(a\) and \(\alpha\) are non-zero positive constants.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The motion occurs along the x-axis with the origin \(x = 0\) as the central point.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
1. An oscillatory motion is one where the particle moves to and fro bounded between two extreme limits about a central equilibrium position.
2. A periodic motion is one where the motion repeats itself identically after a fixed interval of time \(T\), such that \(x(t + T) = x(t)\) for all \(t\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Because the cosine function is bounded, \(-1 \le \cos(\alpha^2 t^2) \le +1\), the displacement of the particle is always confined to the range:
\[-a \le x(t) \le +a\]
As time \(t\) increases, \(x(t)\) continually passes back and forth through the origin \(x = 0\) between \(+a\) and \(-a\). Hence, the motion is oscillatory.
To test for periodicity, consider the times at which \(x(t) = a\), which occur when:
\[\alpha^2 t^2 = 2n\pi \implies t_n = \frac{\sqrt{2n\pi}}{\alpha}, \quad n = 0, 1, 2, 3, \dots\]
The time difference between successive maxima is:
\[\Delta t_n = t_{n+1} - t_n = \frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}{\alpha} \left(\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n}\right)\]
As \(n\) increases, \(\left(\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n}\right) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1} + \sqrt{n}}\) steadily decreases towards zero.
Because the time taken to complete each successive cycle is not constant and continuously decreases with time, there is no constant period \(T\) satisfying \(x(t + T) = x(t)\). Thus, the motion is not periodic.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The motion is to-and-fro between \(-a\) and \(+a\), so it is oscillatory, but the oscillation frequency continuously increases, making it non-periodic.
Therefore, the motion is oscillatory but not periodic.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आंकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>
समय के फलन के रूप में कण का स्थिति समीकरण दिया गया है:
\[x(t) = a \cos (\alpha^2 t^2)\]
जहाँ \(a\) और \(\alpha\) शून्येतर धनात्मक नियतांक हैं।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी</strong><br/>
कण x-अक्ष के अनुदिश गति करता है जहाँ मूलबिंदु \(x = 0\) केंद्रीय स्थिति है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
1. दोलनी गति वह गति है जिसमें कण एक निश्चित माध्य स्थिति के दोनों ओर निश्चित सीमाओं के मध्य आगे-पीछे गति करता है।
2. आवर्ती गति वह गति है जो एक निश्चित समय अंतराल \(T\) के बाद हूबहू दोहराई जाती है, अर्थात् सभी \(t\) के लिए \(x(t + T) = x(t)\) होता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
चूँकि कोज्या फलन का मान \(-1\) और \(+1\) के बीच सीमित रहता है, अतः कण का विस्थापन सदैव निम्नलिखित परास में परिबद्ध रहता है:
\[-a \le x(t) \le +a\]
कण \(+a\) और \(-a\) के बीच मूलबिंदु के परितः बार-बार इधर-उधर गति करता है, अतः यह गति दोलनी (oscillatory) है।
आवर्तकाल की जांच करने के लिए, अधिकतम मान प्राप्त होने के समय:
\[\alpha^2 t^2 = 2n\pi \implies t_n = \frac{\sqrt{2n\pi}}{\alpha}\]
उत्तरोत्तर उच्चिष्ठों के बीच का समय अंतराल:
\[\Delta t_n = t_{n+1} - t_n = \frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}{\alpha} (\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n})\]
जैसे-जैसे \(n\) बढ़ता है, यह समयांतराल लगातार घटता जाता है।
चूँकि कोई स्थिर आवर्तकाल \(T\) विद्यमान नहीं है, अतः गति आवर्ती नहीं है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जांच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
गति परिबद्ध सीमाओं के बीच दोलन करती है, परन्तु चक्रों का समय स्थिर नहीं रहता।
अतः यह गति दोलनी है परन्तु आवर्ती नहीं है।</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>A particle executing S.H.M. has a maximum speed of \(30\text{ cm/s}\) and a maximum acceleration of \(60\text{ cm/s}^2\). The period of oscillation is</p>
<hr/>
<p>सरल आवर्त गति कर रहे एक कण की अधिकतम चाल \(30\text{ cm/s}\) तथा अधिकतम त्वरण \(60\text{ cm/s}^2\) है। दोलन का आवर्तकाल है:</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Maximum speed of the particle: \(v_{\max} = 30\text{ cm/s}\)
Maximum acceleration of the particle: \(a_{\max} = 60\text{ cm/s}^2\)</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The motion is standard simple harmonic motion with amplitude \(A\) and angular frequency \(\omega\). Magnitude values of maximum velocity and acceleration are used.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
In simple harmonic motion:
Maximum speed:
\[v_{\max} = \omega A\]
Maximum acceleration:
\[a_{\max} = \omega^2 A\]
The ratio of maximum acceleration to maximum speed gives the angular frequency:
\[\omega = \frac{a_{\max}}{v_{\max}}\]
The time period of oscillation is related to angular frequency by:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Calculate the angular frequency \(\omega\):
\[\omega = \frac{60\text{ cm/s}^2}{30\text{ cm/s}} = 2\text{ rad/s}\]
Calculate the time period \(T\):
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = \frac{2\pi}{2} = \pi\text{ s}\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Checking dimensions:
\([\omega] = [\text{T}^{-1}]\) and \([T] = [\text{T}]\), which is correct.
Substituting back: \(A = v_{\max}/\omega = 30/2 = 15\text{ cm}\), then \(a_{\max} = \omega^2 A = 4 \times 15 = 60\text{ cm/s}^2\), verifying consistency.
Therefore, the period of oscillation is \(\pi\text{ s}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आंकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>
कण की अधिकतम चाल: \(v_{\max} = 30\text{ cm/s}\)
कण का अधिकतम त्वरण: \(a_{\max} = 60\text{ cm/s}^2\)</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी</strong><br/>
कण आयाम \(A\) तथा कोणीय आवृत्ति \(\omega\) के साथ सरल आवर्त गति कर रहा है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
सरल आवर्त गति में:
अधिकतम चाल:
\[v_{\max} = \omega A\]
अधिकतम त्वरण:
\[a_{\max} = \omega^2 A\]
अधिकतम त्वरण तथा अधिकतम चाल का अनुपात कोणीय आवृत्ति देता है:
\[\omega = \frac{a_{\max}}{v_{\max}}\]
दोलन का आवर्तकाल निम्न सूत्र से प्राप्त होता है:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
कोणीय आवृत्ति \(\omega\) की गणना:
\[\omega = \frac{60}{30} = 2\text{ rad/s}\]
आवर्तकाल \(T\) की गणना:
\[T = \frac{2\pi}{2} = \pi\text{ s}\]
</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जांच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
मानों की पुनः जांच: \(A = 15\text{ cm}\) तथा \(a_{\max} = 2^2 \times 15 = 60\text{ cm/s}^2\), जो दिए गए मानों से पूर्णतः मेल खाता है।
अतः दोलन का आवर्तकाल \(\pi\text{ s}\) है।</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>When a mass \(m\) is connected individually to two springs \(S_1\) and \(S_2\), the oscillation frequencies are \(\nu_1\) and \(\nu_2\). If the same mass is attached to the two springs as shown in the figure, the oscillation frequency would be</p>
<p><img src="@@PLUGINFILE@@/img-2.jpeg" alt="Mass attached between two springs connected to rigid walls" /></p>
<hr/>
<p>जब एक द्रव्यमान \(m\) को अलग-अलग दो कमानी \(S_1\) और \(S_2\) से जोड़ा जाता है, तो दोलन आवृत्तियाँ \(\nu_1\) और \(\nu_2\) होती हैं। यदि उसी द्रव्यमान को चित्र में दिखाए अनुसार दोनों कमानियों से जोड़ दिया जाए, तो दोलन आवृत्ति होगी:</p>
<p><img src="@@PLUGINFILE@@/img-2.jpeg" alt="दो कमानियों के बीच जुड़ा द्रव्यमान" /></p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Oscillation frequency of mass \(m\) with spring \(S_1\) alone: \(\nu_1\)
Oscillation frequency of mass \(m\) with spring \(S_2\) alone: \(\nu_2\)
In the combined system, the mass \(m\) is placed between the two springs, each attached to a fixed rigid wall.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
Let the spring constants of \(S_1\) and \(S_2\) be \(k_1\) and \(k_2\). The surface is frictionless. When mass \(m\) is displaced by a distance \(x\) to the right from its equilibrium position, spring \(S_1\) is stretched by \(x\) and spring \(S_2\) is compressed by \(x\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The frequency of oscillation for a mass-spring system is:
\[\nu = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} \implies k = 4\pi^2 m \nu^2\]
For the two individual springs:
\[k_1 = 4\pi^2 m \nu_1^2, \quad k_2 = 4\pi^2 m \nu_2^2\]
When displaced by \(x\), both springs exert restoring forces in the direction opposing displacement:
\[F_{\text{net}} = -k_1 x - k_2 x = -(k_1 + k_2) x\]
Thus, the two springs act in parallel, giving an effective spring constant:
\[k_{\text{eff}} = k_1 + k_2\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
The frequency \(\nu\) of the combined system is:
\[\nu = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k_{\text{eff}}}{m}} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k_1 + k_2}{m}}\]
Expressing \(k_1/m\) and \(k_2/m\) in terms of the individual frequencies:
\[\frac{k_1}{m} = 4\pi^2 \nu_1^2, \quad \frac{k_2}{m} = 4\pi^2 \nu_2^2\]
Substitute these into the expression for \(\nu\):
\[\nu = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{4\pi^2 \nu_1^2 + 4\pi^2 \nu_2^2} = \frac{1}{2\pi} (2\pi) \sqrt{\nu_1^2 + \nu_2^2} = \sqrt{\nu_1^2 + \nu_2^2}\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Since both springs assist in restoring the mass to equilibrium, the effective stiffness increases, which raises the frequency above both \(\nu_1\) and \(\nu_2\), satisfying \(\nu = \sqrt{\nu_1^2 + \nu_2^2}\).
Therefore, the oscillation frequency would be \(\sqrt{\nu_1^2 + \nu_2^2}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आंकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>
कमानी \(S_1\) के साथ द्रव्यमान \(m\) की दोलन आवृत्ति: \(\nu_1\)
कमानी \(S_2\) के साथ द्रव्यमान \(m\) की दोलन आवृत्ति: \(\nu_2\)
संयुक्त निकाय में द्रव्यमान \(m\) दोनों कमानियों के बीच स्थित है जो दृढ़ दीवारों से जुड़ी हैं।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी</strong><br/>
कमानियों के बल नियतांक क्रमशः \(k_1\) और \(k_2\) हैं। सतह घर्षणहीन है। साम्यावस्था से दाईं ओर \(x\) विस्थापन देने पर कमानी \(S_1\) में \(x\) का खिंचाव तथा कमानी \(S_2\) में \(x\) का संपीड़न उत्पन्न होता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
कमानी-द्रव्यमान निकाय की आवृत्ति निम्न सूत्र से दी जाती है:
\[\nu = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} \implies k = 4\pi^2 m \nu^2\]
प्रत्येक कमानी के लिए:
\[k_1 = 4\pi^2 m \nu_1^2, \quad k_2 = 4\pi^2 m \nu_2^2\]
विस्थापन \(x\) के लिए दोनों कमानियों द्वारा लगाया गया कुल प्रत्यानयन बल:
\[F_{\text{net}} = -k_1 x - k_2 x = -(k_1 + k_2) x\]
अतः यह संयोजन समांतर संयोजन के समतुल्य है जिसका प्रभावी बल नियतांक \(k_{\text{eff}} = k_1 + k_2\) है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
संयुक्त निकाय की दोलन आवृत्ति:
\[\nu = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k_{\text{eff}}}{m}} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k_1 + k_2}{m}}\]
\(k_1/m\) तथा \(k_2/m\) के मान प्रतिस्थापित करने पर:
\[\nu = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{4\pi^2 \nu_1^2 + 4\pi^2 \nu_2^2} = \sqrt{\nu_1^2 + \nu_2^2}\]</p>
<p><strong>चरण 5: जांच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
चूँकि दोनों कमानियाँ मिलकर प्रत्यानयन बल प्रदान करती हैं, अतः प्रभावी दृढ़ता बढ़ती है और परिणामी आवृत्ति दोनों व्यक्तिगत आवृत्तियों से अधिक \(\sqrt{\nu_1^2 + \nu_2^2}\) होती है।
अतः दोलन आवृत्ति \(\sqrt{\nu_1^2 + \nu_2^2}\) होगी।</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
The physical motion under consideration is the spinning of the Earth about its geographic polar axis.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The rotation of the Earth has a fixed period of approximately 24 hours (one sidereal day) and takes place continuously in one rotational direction (from west to east).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
1. Periodic motion is a motion that repeats itself identically after a definite interval of time.
2. Simple harmonic motion requires bounded to-and-fro motion along a path about a stable equilibrium point, driven by a linear restoring force proportional to the negative of displacement from that equilibrium position.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
The Earth completes one full revolution about its axis every 24 hours, meaning the state of motion repeats regularly after every constant time interval \(T\). Thus, it satisfies the precise definition of periodic motion.
However, the Earth's rotation is unidirectional rotational motion; it does not oscillate back and forth about an equilibrium orientation, nor is there a restoring force directed towards an equilibrium position proportional to angular displacement. Hence, it is not simple harmonic motion.
Consequently, this motion is periodic, and it is periodic but not simple harmonic motion.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The motion has a constant repetition time without any to-and-fro oscillatory displacement.
Therefore, the rotation of the Earth about its axis is a periodic motion, and it is periodic but not simple harmonic motion.</p>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2026_OSCILLATIONS_MCQ_Q14_13 - Motion of ball bearing inside bowl</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>Motion of a ball bearing inside a smooth curved bowl, when released from a point slightly above the lower point is</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक चिकने वक्रित कटोरे के अंदर, पेंदे (निम्नतम बिंदु) से थोड़ा ऊपर किसी बिंदु से छोड़े गए छर्रे (ball bearing) की गति होती है:</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
A small ball bearing of mass \(m\) is placed inside a spherical bowl of radius of curvature \(R\). The surface of the bowl is smooth (frictionless). The ball is released from a point slightly above the lowest bottom point.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The angular displacement \(\theta\) from the lowest equilibrium point is very small, so the small-angle approximation applies: \(\sin\theta \approx \theta\). The displacement along the curve is \(s = R\theta\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The restoring force acting tangentially on the ball bearing along the surface of the bowl is:
\[F = -mg \sin\theta\]
Using the small-angle approximation \(\sin\theta \approx \theta = \frac{s}{R}\):
\[F = -mg \left(\frac{s}{R}\right) = -\left(\frac{mg}{R}\right) s\]
Acceleration along the path is:
\[a = \frac{F}{m} = -\frac{g}{R} s\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Because the restoring acceleration \(a\) is directly proportional to displacement \(s\) and directed toward the lowest point (the equilibrium point), the motion satisfies \(a = -\omega^2 s\) with:
\[\omega = \sqrt{\frac{g}{R}}\]
This proves that the motion is simple harmonic motion.
Furthermore, every simple harmonic motion is fundamentally a periodic motion, repeating with time period:
\[T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{R}{g}}\]
Therefore, the motion is both simple harmonic motion and periodic motion.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>

Therefore, the motion of the ball bearing is simple harmonic motion, and it is also periodic motion.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
In simple harmonic motion:
1. Restoring force is directly proportional to displacement and opposite to it:
\[F = -k x\]
2. Displacement, velocity, and acceleration are sinusoidal functions of time with period \(T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\):
\[x(t) = A \sin(\omega t + \phi)\]

<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>

1. Force acting on the oscillator: The defining relation of simple harmonic motion is \(F = -kx\), which means the restoring force is directly proportional to the displacement from the mean position and directed opposite to it. This statement is true.

The force satisfies Hooke's restoring law, the position and velocity repeat periodically, while the acceleration varies sinusoidally with position.
Therefore, the true statements are: force acting is directly proportional to displacement from the mean position and opposite to it; motion is periodic; and the velocity is periodic.</p>

Velocity is \(v(t) = \frac{dx}{dt} = -A\omega \sin(\omega t)\).
Acceleration is \(a(t) = \frac{dv}{dt} = -A\omega^2 \cos(\omega t) = -\omega^2 x(t)\).
Restoring force is \(F(t) = m a(t) = -m\omega^2 x(t)\Close</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
1. Force is proportional to displacement: \(F = -m\omega^2 x\). When \(x = 0\), \(F = 0\).
2. Magnitude of acceleration is \(|a| = \omega^2 |x|\). It is maximum when \(|x| = A\).
3. Speed is \(|v| = \omega \sqrt{A^2 - x^2}\). It is maximum when \(x = 0\).
4. Kinetic energy \(K = \frac{1}{2}m v^2\) and potential energy \(U = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 x^2\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Evaluating each statement:
1. At \(t = \frac{3T}{4}\):
\(x(3T/4) = A \cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{T} \cdot \frac{3T}{4}\right) = A \cos\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}\right) = 0\).
Since displacement is zero, the restoring force \(F = -m\omega^2(0) = 0\). This statement is true.
2. At \(t = \frac{4T}{4} = T\):
\(x(T) = A \cos(2\pi) = A\).
The particle is at the extreme position. The acceleration magnitude is \(|a| = \omega^2 A\), which is the maximum possible value. This statement is true.
3. At \(t = \frac{T}{4}\):
\(x(T/4) = A \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 0\).
The particle is passing through the equilibrium position, so its speed \(|v| = A\omega\) is maximum. This statement is true.
4. At \(t = \frac{T}{2}\):
\(x(T/2) = A \cos(\pi) = -A\).
The particle is at an extreme position where velocity is zero, so kinetic energy is \(K = 0\) and potential energy is maximum: \(U = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 A^2\). Thus, P.E. is not equal to K.E. (they are equal at \(x = \pm A/\sqrt{2}\)). This statement is false.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
At the mean positions (quarter periods), force is zero and speed is maximum. At the extremes (half and full periods), acceleration magnitude is maximum.
Therefore, the true statements are: the force is zero at \(t = \frac{3T}{4}\); the acceleration is maximum at \(t = \frac{4T}{4}\); and the velocity is maximum at \(t = \frac{T}{4}\).</p>

<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आंकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>

\(t = 2T/4 = T/2\) पर \(x = -A\) (ऋणात्मक चरम स्थिति)

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
A body of mass \(m\) performs simple harmonic motion with amplitude \(A\) and angular frequency \(\omega\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
Let the displacement be \(x(t) = A \sin\omega t\).
Then velocity is \(v(t) = A\omega \cos\omega t\), and maximum velocity is \(v_{\max} = A\omega\).
The period of a complete cycle is \(T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
1. Total mechanical energy in SHM is conserved at all instants:
\[E = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 A^2\]
Since maximum kinetic energy occurs when potential energy is zero (at the mean position):
\[K_{\max} = \frac{1}{2}m v_{\max}^2 = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 A^2 = E\]
2. Instantaneous kinetic energy:
\[K(t) = \frac{1}{2}m v(t)^2 = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 A^2 \cos^2\omega t = K_{\max} \cos^2\omega t\]
3. The average value of \(\cos^2\omega t\) over a complete cycle is \(\frac{1}{2}\).
4. The mean of a sinusoidal quantity over a complete period is zero.
5. The root mean square (rms) value of \(v(t) = v_{\max}\cos\omega t\) is:
\[v_{\text{rms}} = \sqrt{\langle v^2 \rangle}\]</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Evaluating each statement:
1. Average total energy per cycle: Since total energy is constant at every instant, its average over a cycle is simply \(E = K_{\max}\). This statement is true.
2. Average kinetic energy per cycle:
\[\langle K \rangle = K_{\max} \langle \cos^2\omega t \rangle = K_{\max} \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{2} K_{\max}\]
Thus, average kinetic energy per cycle is half of maximum kinetic energy. This statement is true.
3. Mean velocity over a complete cycle:
\[\langle v \rangle = \frac{1}{T} \int_0^T v(t) dt = \frac{x(T) - x(0)}{T} = 0\]
Because velocity is symmetrically positive for half the cycle and negative for the other half, the mean velocity over a complete cycle is zero, not \(\frac{2}{\pi} v_{\max}\) (which is the average speed). This statement is false.
4. Root mean square velocity:
\[v_{\text{rms}} = \sqrt{\langle v^2 \rangle} = \sqrt{v_{\max}^2 \langle \cos^2\omega t \rangle} = \sqrt{\frac{v_{\max}^2}{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} v_{\max}\]
Thus, the root mean square velocity is \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\) times the maximum velocity. This statement is true.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Total energy is invariant and equals maximum kinetic energy; time-average kinetic energy is half of the peak; and the rms value of a sinusoidal wave is its peak divided by \(\sqrt{2}\).
Therefore, the true statements are: average total energy per cycle is equal to its maximum kinetic energy; average kinetic energy per cycle is equal to half of its maximum kinetic energy; and root mean square velocity is \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\) times of its maximum velocity.</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>

2. तात्क्षणिक गतिज ऊर्जा: \(K(t) = K_{\max} \cos^2\omega t\)।
3. पूर्ण चक्र पर \(\cos^2\omega t\) का औसत मान \(1/2\) होता है।

<p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>
कथनों का क्रमिक सत्यापन:
1. कुल ऊर्जा सदैव नियत रहती है, अतः इसका प्रति चक्र औसत मान अधिकतम गतिज ऊर्जा के बराबर होता है। यह कथन सत्य है।
2. प्रति चक्र औसत गतिज ऊर्जा:
\[\langle K \rangle = \frac{1}{2} K_{\max}\]
अतः औसत गतिज ऊर्जा अधिकतम गतिज ऊर्जा की आधी होती है। यह कथन सत्य है।
3. एक पूर्ण चक्र में विस्थापन का कुल परिवर्तन शून्य होता है, अतः माध्य वेग शून्य होता है, \(\frac{2}{\pi} v_{\max}\) नहीं। यह कथन असत्य है।
4. वर्ग माध्य मूल वेग:

  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>A particle is in linear simple harmonic motion between two points A and B, \(10\text{ cm}\) apart as shown in the figure. Take the direction from A to B as the +ve direction and choose the correct statements.</p>

<hr/>
<p>चित्र में दर्शाए अनुसार एक कण \(10\text{ cm}\) दूर स्थित दो बिंदुओं A और B के बीच सरल रेखीय सरल आवर्त गति में है। A से B की दिशा को धनात्मक (+ve) दिशा मानकर सही कथनों का चयन कीजिए:</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Total path length between extremes A and B: \(AB = 10\text{ cm}\).
Mean position is at O with \(AO = OB = 5\text{ cm}\), so amplitude is \(A = 5\text{ cm}\).
Reference direction: From A to B is defined as the positive (+ve) direction.
Looking at the line from left to right: point B is on the left, O is at the center, point C is between O and A, and point A is on the right.
Since \(BC = 8\text{ cm}\) and \(OB = 5\text{ cm}\), point C is at distance \(OC = BC - OB = 8 - 5 = 3\text{ cm}\) from O towards A.
Also, \(AC = OA - OC = 5 - 3 = 2\text{ cm}\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
Direction from A to B (right to left) is taken as POSITIVE (+ve).
Consequently, the direction from B to A (left to right) is NEGATIVE (-ve).
At equilibrium position O, \(x = 0\).
Displacement towards B is positive: \(x_B = +5\text{ cm}\).
Displacement towards A is negative: \(x_A = -5\text{ cm}\).
At point C, displacement from O is towards A, so \(x_C = -3\text{ cm}\).
Velocity \(v\) is positive when moving towards B, and negative when moving towards A.
Acceleration is directed towards the equilibrium position O:
\[a = -\omega^2 x\]
Restoring force has the same sign as acceleration: \(F = ma = -m\omega^2 x\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
1. If the particle is moving towards B, \(v > 0\) (positive). If moving towards A, \(v < 0\) (negative).
2. If displacement \(x < 0\) (particle is on the A side of O), acceleration and force are directed towards O (which is towards B, the +ve direction), so \(a > 0\) and \(F > 0\).
3. If displacement \(x > 0\) (particle is on the B side of O), acceleration and force are directed towards O (which is towards A, the -ve direction), so \(a < 0\) and \(F < 0\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Analyzing each statement:
1. When the particle is \(3\text{ cm}\) away from A going towards B:
The distance from A is \(3\text{ cm}\) towards O, so its position is \(2\text{ cm}\) from O on the A side: displacement \(x = -2\text{ cm}\).
Because it is moving towards B (the positive direction), its velocity is positive: \(v > 0\).
Because \(x = -2\text{ cm} < 0\), the restoring acceleration and force act towards O (towards B), which is the positive direction: \(a > 0\) and \(F > 0\).
Thus, velocity, acceleration, and force are all positive. This statement is true.
2. At point C going towards O:
Point C is on the A side, so moving from C towards O means moving towards B (the positive direction). Therefore, the velocity of the particle is positive, not negative. This statement is false.
3. When the particle is \(4\text{ cm}\) away from B going towards A:
The distance from B is \(4\text{ cm}\) towards O, so its position is \(1\text{ cm}\) from O on the B side: displacement \(x = +1\text{ cm}\).
Because it is moving towards A, its velocity is in the negative direction: \(v < 0\).
Because \(x = +1\text{ cm} > 0\), the restoring acceleration and force act towards O (towards A), which is the negative direction: \(a < 0\) and \(F < 0\).
Thus, velocity, acceleration, and force are all negative. This statement is true.
4. When the particle is at point B:
At point B, displacement is positive maximum: \(x = +5\text{ cm}\).
The restoring force and acceleration act towards the mean position O (which is towards A, the negative direction). Hence, both acceleration and force are negative. This statement is true.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
With the positive direction defined from A to B:
On the A side of O moving towards B, \(v > 0\), \(a > 0\), \(F > 0\).
On the B side of O moving towards A, \(v < 0\), \(a < 0\), \(F < 0\).
At point B, the particle experiences restoring acceleration and force directed towards O (negative direction).
Therefore, the correct statements are:
The sign of velocity, acceleration and force on the particle when it is \(3\text{ cm}\) away from A going towards B are positive;
The sign of velocity, acceleration and force on the particle when it is \(4\text{ cm}\) away from B going towards A are negative;
The sign of acceleration and force on the particle when it is at point B is negative.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आंकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>
A और B के बीच की कुल दूरी: \(AB = 10\text{ cm}\)।
साम्यावस्था O पर \(AO = OB = 5\text{ cm}\) है, अतः आयाम \(A = 5\text{ cm}\) है।
निर्देश दिशा: A से B की ओर की दिशा धनात्मक (+ve) ली गई है।
अतः B से A की ओर की दिशा ऋणात्मक (-ve) है।
बिंदु C, O से A की ओर \(3\text{ cm}\) की दूरी पर स्थित है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिन्ह परिपाटी</strong><br/>

B की ओर गति के लिए वेग धनात्मक (\(v > 0\)) तथा A की ओर गति के लिए वेग ऋणात्मक (\(v < 0\)) है।

3. जब कण B से \(4\text{ cm}\) दूर A की ओर जा रहा है: कण B की तरफ स्थित है (\(x > 0\)) तथा A की ओर गतिमान है। अतः वेग ऋणात्मक है, तथा त्वरण व बल O की ओर (A की दिशा में) होने से ऋणात्मक हैं। यह कथन सत्य है।
