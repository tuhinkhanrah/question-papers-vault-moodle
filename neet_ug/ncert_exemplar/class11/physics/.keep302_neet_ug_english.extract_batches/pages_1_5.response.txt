  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Fundamental Quantities and Dimensions</strong><br/>
Let energy \(E\) depend on momentum \(P\), area \(A\), and time \(T\) as:
\[
E \propto P^a A^b T^c
\]
The standard dimensions are:
- Energy: \([E] = [\text{M}\text{L}^2\text{T}^{-2}]\)
- Momentum: \([P] = [\text{M}\text{L}\text{T}^{-1}]\)
- Area: \([A] = [\text{L}^2]\)
- Time: \([T] = [\text{T}]\)
Alternatively, notice that:
\[
E = \frac{P^2}{2m} \quad \text{and} \quad P = F \cdot t = \frac{E}{v} \implies E = P \cdot v
\]
Since velocity has dimensions \([v] = [\text{L}\text{T}^{-1}]\) and area is \([A] = [\text{L}^2]\), the length dimension is \([\text{L}] = [A^{1/2}]\).
Therefore, velocity is \([v] = [A^{1/2} T^{-1}]\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Step-by-Step Derivation</strong><br/>
Expressing energy directly:
\[
[E] = [P][v] = [P][A^{1/2} T^{-1}] = [\text{P}^1 \text{A}^{1/2} \text{T}^{-1}]
\]
Let us verify by equating powers:
\[
[\text{M}\text{L}^2\text{T}^{-2}] = [\text{M}\text{L}\text{T}^{-1}]^a [\text{L}^2]^b [\text{T}]^c = \text{M}^a \text{L}^{a+2b} \text{T}^{-a+c}
\]
Comparing exponents:
- For \(\text{M}\): \(a = 1\)
- For \(\text{L}\): \(a + 2b = 2 \implies 1 + 2b = 2 \implies 2b = 1 \implies b = 1/2\)
- For \(\text{T}\): \(-a + c = -2 \implies -1 + c = -2 \implies c = -1\)
Hence, \([E] = [\text{P}^1 \text{A}^{1/2} \text{T}^{-1}]\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The dimensional formula for energy in terms of \(P\), \(A\), and \(T\) is \((\text{P}^1\text{A}^{1/2}\text{T}^{-1})\).</p>
