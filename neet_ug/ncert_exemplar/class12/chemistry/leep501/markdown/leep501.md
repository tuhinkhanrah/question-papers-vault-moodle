77. Let the formula of sample be

$$
\left(\mathrm {F e} ^ {2 +}\right) _ {x} \left(\mathrm {F e} ^ {3 +}\right) _ {y} \mathrm {O}.
$$

On looking at the given formula of the compound

$$
x + y = 0. 9 3 \quad \dots (1)
$$

Total positive charge on ferrous and ferric ions should balance the two units of negative charge on oxygen. Therefore,

$$
2 x + 3 y = 2 \quad \dots (2)
$$

$$
\Rightarrow x + \frac {3}{2} y = 1 \quad \dots (3)
$$

On subtracting equation (1) from equation (3) we have

$$
\begin{array}{l} \frac {3}{2} y - y = 1 - 0. 9 3 \\ \Rightarrow \frac {1}{2} y = 0. 0 7 \\ \Rightarrow y = 0. 1 4 \\ \end{array}
$$

On putting the value of $y$ in equation (1) we get,

$$
\begin{array}{l} x + 0. 1 4 = 0. 9 3 \\ \Rightarrow x = 0. 9 3 - 0. 1 4 \\ x = 0. 7 9 \\ \end{array}
$$

Fraction of $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ ions present in the sample $= \frac{0.79}{0.93} = 0.81$
