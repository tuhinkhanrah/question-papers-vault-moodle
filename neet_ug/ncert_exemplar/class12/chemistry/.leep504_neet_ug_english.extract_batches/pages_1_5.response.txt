At time \(t\), partial pressure of \(\mathrm{A}\) decreases due to decomposition, and the total pressure becomes \(p_t\), having increased by \(x\) units (\(p_t = p_{\mathrm{i}} + x\)).</p><p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Principles</strong><br/>For a first-order gas-phase reaction, the integrated rate equation in terms of partial pressures is: \[k = \frac{2.303}{t} \log\left(\frac{p_{\mathrm{i}}}{p_{\mathrm{A}}}\right)\] where \(p_{\mathrm{A}}\) is the partial pressure of reactant \(\mathrm{A}\) remaining at time \(t\).</p><p><strong>Step 3: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>Let \(x\) be the decrease in partial pressure of \(\mathrm{A}\) at time \(t\).
