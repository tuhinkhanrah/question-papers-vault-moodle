  <name><text>NCERTEXEMPLAR_CHEMISTRY_ALDEHYDESKETONESANDCARBOXYLICACIDS_AR_Q42 - Planarity and hybridisation of formaldehyde</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>In the following question a statement of assertion followed by a statement of reason is given. Choose the correct answer out of the given choices.</p><p><strong>Assertion :</strong> Formaldehyde is a planar molecule.</p><p><strong>Reason :</strong> It contains \(sp^2\) hybridised carbon atom.</p><hr/><p>निम्नलिखित प्रश्न में एक अभिकथन (Assertion) के बाद एक कारण (Reason) दिया गया है। दिए गए विकल्पों में से सही उत्तर चुनिए।</p><p><strong>अभिकथन :</strong> फॉर्मेल्डिहाइड एक समतलीय अणु है।</p><p><strong>कारण :</strong> इसमें \(sp^2\) संकरित कार्बन परमाणु होता है।</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Verification of Assertion</strong><br/>Formaldehyde (\(\mathrm{HCHO}\)) has all four atoms (\(\mathrm{H, H, C, O}\)) lying in the same plane, so it is indeed a planar molecule.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Verification of Reason and Link</strong><br/>The carbonyl carbon in formaldehyde forms three \(\sigma\) bonds (two with \(\mathrm{H}\) and one with \(\mathrm{O}\)) and one \(\pi\) bond with \(\mathrm{O}\), making it \(sp^2\) hybridised.
