  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>Common names of aldehydes and ketones are derived from historical or botanical origins, whereas their systematic IUPAC names are derived from the longest continuous carbon chain containing the principal carbonyl functional group.</p>
<p><strong>Evaluation:</strong><br/>1. Cinnamaldehyde has the structure \(\mathrm{C_6H_5-CH=CH-CHO}\), which consists of a three-carbon unsaturated aldehyde chain with a phenyl substituent at position 3, corresponding to the IUPAC name 3-Phenylprop-2-enal.<br/>
2. Acetophenone has the structure \(\mathrm{C_6H_5-CO-CH_3}\), which is a methyl phenyl ketone where the parent alkane chain is ethane with an oxo group at carbon 1 and a phenyl group at carbon 1, corresponding to 1-Phenylethanone.<br/>
3. Valeraldehyde is the common name for the straight five-carbon aliphatic aldehyde \(\mathrm{CH_3(CH_2)_3CHO}\), which is named Pentanal.<br/>
4. Acrolein is the common name for the simplest unsaturated aldehyde \(\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CHO}\), named Prop-2-enal.<br/>
5. Mesityl oxide is an \(\alpha,\beta\)-unsaturated ketone produced by condensation of acetone, having the structure \(\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C=CH-CO-CH_3}\), which is systematically numbered from the carbonyl end to give 4-Methylpent-3-en-2-one.</p>
<p><strong>Final Conclusion:</strong><br/>Therefore, the correct matches are: Cinnamaldehyde matches 3-Phenylprop-2-enal; Acetophenone matches 1-Phenylethanone; Valeraldehyde matches Pentanal; Acrolein matches Prop-2-enal; and Mesityl oxide matches 4-Methylpent-3-en-2-one.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>सैद्धांतिक सिद्धांत:</strong><br/>एल्डिहाइडों और कीटोनों के सामान्य नाम उनके ऐतिहासिक स्रोतों पर आधारित होते हैं, जबकि आईयूपीएसी (IUPAC) नाम मुख्य कार्बोनिल समूह युक्त सबसे लंबी कार्बन श्रृंखला पर आधारित होते हैं।</p>
<p><strong>मूल्यांकन:</strong><br/>1. सिन्नैमैल्डिहाइड (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5-CH=CH-CHO}\)) का आईयूपीएसी नाम 3-फेनिलप्रोप-2-ईनैल (3-Phenylprop-2-enal) है।<br/>
2. ऐसीटोफीनोन (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5-CO-CH_3}\)) का आईयूपीएसी नाम 1-फेनिलएथेनोन (1-Phenylethanone) है।<br/>
3. वैलेरैल्डिहाइड (\(\mathrm{CH_3(CH_2)_3CHO}\)) पाँच कार्बन वाला सरल एल्डिहाइड है, जिसका आईयूपीएसी नाम पेन्टेनैल (Pentanal) है।<br/>
4. ऐक्रोलीन (\(\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CHO}\)) सबसे सरल असंतृप्त एल्डिहाइड है, जिसका आईयूपीएसी नाम प्रोप-2-ईनैल (Prop-2-enal) है।<br/>
5. मेसिटिल ऑक्साइड (\(\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C=CH-CO-CH_3}\)) का आईयूपीएसी नाम 4-मेथिलपेन्ट-3-ईन-2-ओन (4-Methylpent-3-en-2-one) है।</p>
<p><strong>अंतिम निष्कर्ष:</strong><br/>अतः सही सुमेलन है: सिन्नैमैल्डिहाइड का 3-Phenylprop-2-enal; ऐसीटोफीनोन का 1-Phenylethanone; वैलेरैल्डिहाइड का Pentanal; ऐक्रोलीन का Prop-2-enal; तथा मेसिटिल ऑक्साइड का 4-Methylpent-3-en-2-one से सुमेलन होता है।</p>]]></text>

  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>Dicarboxylic acids possess two terminal carboxyl (\(-\mathrm{COOH}\)) groups. In IUPAC nomenclature, the longest continuous carbon chain containing both carboxyl carbons is identified, and the suffix "-dioic acid" is appended to the alkane name.</p>
<p><strong>Evaluation:</strong><br/>1. Phthalic acid is an aromatic ortho-dicarboxylic acid with two adjacent carboxyl groups on a benzene ring, systematically named Benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid.<br/>
2. Oxalic acid is the simplest dicarboxylic acid, \(\mathrm{HOOC-COOH}\), containing two carbon atoms, named Ethane-1,2-dioic acid.<br/>
3. Succinic acid contains a four-carbon chain, \(\mathrm{HOOC-(CH_2)_2-COOH}\), named Butane-1,4-dioic acid.<br/>
4. Adipic acid contains a six-carbon chain, \(\mathrm{HOOC-(CH_2)_4-COOH}\), named Hexane-1,6-dioic acid.<br/>
5. Glutaric acid contains a five-carbon chain, \(\mathrm{HOOC-(CH_2)_3-COOH}\), named Pentane-1,5-dioic acid.</p>
<p><strong>Final Conclusion:</strong><br/>Therefore, the correct matches are: Phthalic acid matches Benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid; Oxalic acid matches Ethane-1,2-dioic acid; Succinic acid matches Butane-1,4-dioic acid; Adipic acid matches Hexane-1,6-dioic acid; and Glutaric acid matches Pentane-1,5-dioic acid.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>सैद्धांतिक सिद्धांत:</strong><br/>डाइकार्बोक्सिलिक अम्लों में दो सिरे वाले कार्बोक्सिल (-\(\mathrm{COOH}\)) समूह होते हैं। आईयूपीएसी पद्धति में दोनों कार्बोक्सिल कार्बन परमाणुओं को सम्मिलित करते हुए सबसे लंबी श्रृंखला का चयन किया जाता है तथा ऐल्केन नाम के अंत में "-डाइओइक अम्ल" अनुलग्न लगाया जाता है।</p>
<p><strong>मूल्यांकन:</strong><br/>1. थैलिक अम्ल एक ऐरोमैटिक डाइकार्बोक्सिलिक अम्ल है जिसका आईयूपीएसी नाम बेन्जीन-1,2-डाइकार्बोक्सिलिक अम्ल (Benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid) है।<br/>
2. ऑक्सैलिक अम्ल (\(\mathrm{HOOC-COOH}\)) दो कार्बन वाला अम्ल है, जिसका नाम एथेन-1,2-डाइओइक अम्ल (Ethane-1,2-dioic acid) है।<br/>
3. सक्सिनिक अम्ल (\(\mathrm{HOOC-(CH_2)_2-COOH}\)) चार कार्बन वाला अम्ल है, जिसका नाम ब्यूटेन-1,4-डाइओइक अम्ल (Butane-1,4-dioic acid) है।<br/>
4. ऐडिपिक अम्ल (\(\mathrm{HOOC-(CH_2)_4-COOH}\)) छह कार्बन वाला अम्ल है, जिसका नाम हेक्सेन-1,6-डाइओइक अम्ल (Hexane-1,6-dioic acid) है।<br/>
5. ग्लूटैरिक अम्ल (\(\mathrm{HOOC-(CH_2)_3-COOH}\)) पाँच कार्बन वाला अम्ल है, जिसका नाम पेन्टेन-1,5-डाइओइक अम्ल (Pentane-1,5-dioic acid) है।</p>
<p><strong>अंतिम निष्कर्ष:</strong><br/>अतः सही सुमेलन है: थैलिक अम्ल का Benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid; ऑक्सैलिक अम्ल का Ethane-1,2-dioic acid; सक्सिनिक अम्ल का Butane-1,4-dioic acid; ऐडिपिक अम्ल का Hexane-1,6-dioic acid; तथा ग्लूटैरिक अम्ल का Pentane-1,5-dioic acid से सुमेलन होता है।</p>]]></text>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>Different synthetic transformations of carbonyl functional groups (reduction of ketones to alkanes, addition of Grignard reagents to aldehydes, reduction of ketones to secondary alcohols, and partial reduction of esters to aldehydes) demand specific selective reagents.</p>
<p><strong>Evaluation:</strong><br/>1. The complete reduction of the ketone carbonyl group in benzophenone to a methylene group yielding diphenylmethane (\(\mathrm{Ph_2C=O} \to \mathrm{Ph_2CH_2}\)) is carried out by Clemmensen reduction using amalgamated zinc in concentrated hydrochloric acid, \(\mathrm{Zn(Hg) / Conc.HCl}\).<br/>
2. The conversion of benzaldehyde into the secondary alcohol 1-phenylethanol (\(\mathrm{PhCHO} \to \mathrm{PhCH(OH)CH_3}\)) requires nucleophilic addition of a methyl carbanion, which is accomplished using the Grignard reagent \(\mathrm{CH_3MgBr}\) followed by hydrolysis.<br/>
3. The reduction of the cyclic ketone cyclohexanone to the secondary alcohol cyclohexanol is effected by reducing hydride reagents such as lithium aluminium hydride, \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\).<br/>
4. The selective reduction of an ester (phenyl benzoate) into an aldehyde (benzaldehyde) at low temperatures without further reducing the aldehyde to alcohol is achieved using diisobutylaluminium hydride, DIBAL-H.</p>
<p><strong>Final Conclusion:</strong><br/>Therefore, the correct matches are: Benzophenone \(\rightarrow\) Diphenylmethane matches \(\mathrm{Zn(Hg) / Conc.HCl}\); Benzaldehyde \(\rightarrow\) 1-Phenylethanol matches \(\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{MgBr}\); Cyclohexanone \(\rightarrow\) Cyclohexanol matches \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\); and Phenyl benzoate \(\rightarrow\) Benzaldehyde matches DIBAL-H.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>सैद्धांतिक सिद्धांत:</strong><br/>कार्बोनिल समूहों के विभिन्न रासायनिक रूपांतरणों (कीटोन का ऐल्केन में अपचयन, एल्डिहाइड पर ग्रीन्यार अभिकर्मक का योग, कीटोन का द्वितीयक ऐल्कोहॉल में अपचयन, तथा ऐस्टर का एल्डिहाइड में चयनात्मक अपचयन) के लिए विशिष्ट अभिकर्मकों की आवश्यकता होती है।</p>
<p><strong>मूल्यांकन:</strong><br/>1. बेन्ज़ोफीनोन के कार्बोनिल समूह को मेथिलीन समूह में अपचयित करके डाइफेनिलमेथेन बनाने के लिए क्लेमेंसन अपचयन अभिकर्मक, \(\mathrm{Zn(Hg) / Conc.HCl}\) का उपयोग किया जाता है।<br/>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_2018_ALDEHYDESKETONESANDCARBOXYLICACIDS_MATCHING_Q41 - Named organic reactions and characteristic equations</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Match the example given in Column I with the name of the reaction in Column II.</p>
<hr/>
<p>कॉलम I में दिए गए उदाहरणों को कॉलम II में दी गई अभिक्रियाओं के नाम से सुमेलित कीजिए।</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle:</strong><br/>Each named organic reaction in carbonyl and carboxylic acid chemistry exhibits a unique combination of starting materials, catalytic conditions, and characteristic products.</p>
<p><strong>Evaluation:</strong><br/>1. Hydrogenation of acetyl chloride over palladium catalyst supported on barium sulphate poisoned with sulphur or quinoline yields acetaldehyde:
\[\mathrm{CH_3COCl} + \mathrm{H_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Pd-BaSO_4}} \mathrm{CH_3CHO} + \mathrm{HCl}\]
This reaction is Rosenmund's reduction.<br/>
2. Reaction of benzaldehyde (an aldehyde lacking \(\alpha\)-hydrogens) with concentrated sodium hydroxide yields benzyl alcohol and sodium benzoate:
\[2\,\mathrm{C_6H_5CHO} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NaOH}} \mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2OH} + \mathrm{C_6H_5COONa}\]
This disproportionation is Cannizaro's reaction.<br/>
3. Reaction of benzene with acetyl chloride in the presence of anhydrous aluminium chloride yields acetophenone:
\[\mathrm{C_6H_6} + \mathrm{CH_3COCl} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{AlCl_3}} \mathrm{C_6H_5COCH_3} + \mathrm{HCl}\]
This electrophilic aromatic substitution is Friedel Crafts acylation.<br/>
4. Halogenation of a carboxylic acid containing \(\alpha\)-hydrogens with bromine in the presence of red phosphorus yields an \(\alpha\)-bromocarboxylic acid:
\[\mathrm{R-CH_2-COOH} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Br_2 / \text{Red P}}} \mathrm{R-CH(Br)-COOH}\]
This reaction is the Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (HVZ) reaction.<br/>
5. Reduction of acetonitrile with stannous chloride and hydrochloric acid to an aldimine hydrochloride followed by hydrolysis yields acetaldehyde:
\[\mathrm{CH_3CN} \xrightarrow{\text{(i) }\mathrm{SnCl_2/HCl}\text{, (ii) }\mathrm{H_3O^+}} \mathrm{CH_3CHO}\]
This reduction is Stephen's reaction.<br/>
6. Self-condensation of two molecules of acetaldehyde in the presence of dilute sodium hydroxide followed by dehydration yields but-2-enal:
\[2\,\mathrm{CH_3CHO} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NaOH}} \mathrm{CH_3-CH=CH-CHO} + \mathrm{H_2O}\]
This reaction is Aldol condensation.</p>
<p><strong>Final Conclusion:</strong><br/>Therefore, the correct matches are: Equation (i) matches Rosenmund's reduction; Equation (ii) matches Cannizaro's reaction; Equation (iii) matches Friedel Crafts acylation; Equation (iv) matches HVZ reaction; Equation (v) matches Stephen's reaction; and Equation (vi) matches Aldol condensation.</p>

<p><strong>Evaluation:</strong><br/>1. Both aromatic aldehydes (such as benzaldehyde) and formaldehyde (\(\mathrm{HCHO}\)) lack \(\alpha\)-hydrogen atoms; therefore, on treatment with concentrated alkali, both undergo redox disproportionation via the Cannizzaro reaction. Thus, the reason is a false statement.</p>

<p><strong>Evaluation:</strong><br/>1. The assertion claims that both aldehydes and ketones react with Tollen's reagent to form a silver mirror.
