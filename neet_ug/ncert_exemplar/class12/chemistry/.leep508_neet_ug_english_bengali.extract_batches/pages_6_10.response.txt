  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>In the actinoid series, the early elements exhibit a wide variety of oxidation states because the energy levels of \(5f\), \(6d\), and \(7s\) subshells are very close, allowing electrons from all these subshells to participate in chemical bonding.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Oxidation States</strong><br/>The maximum oxidation state increases from +3 in actinium up to +7 in neptunium and plutonium. Beyond plutonium, the oxidation states decrease.<br/>Neptunium (\(\mathrm{Np}\)) and plutonium (\(\mathrm{Pu}\)) exhibit oxidation states up to +7, occurring in species such as \([\mathrm{NpO}_5]^{3-}\) and \([\mathrm{PuO}_5]^{3-}\).<br/>Uranium (\(\mathrm{U}\)) exhibits oxidation states up to +6 (such as in uranyl ion, \(\mathrm{UO}_2^{2+}\)), while americium (\(\mathrm{Am}\)) shows oxidation states up to +6.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The actinoids that show oxidation states up to +7 are neptunium (\(\mathrm{Np}\)) and plutonium (\(\mathrm{Pu}\)).</p>

<p><strong>ধাপ ২: জারণ অবস্থার মূল্যায়ন</strong><br/>সর্বোচ্চ জারণ অবস্থা অ্যাক্টিনিয়ামের +3 থেকে বৃদ্ধি পেয়ে নেপচুনিয়াম ও প্লুটোনিয়ামে +7 পর্যন্ত পৌঁছায়। প্লুটোনিয়ামের পর সর্বোচ্চ জারণ অবস্থা হ্রাস পায়।<br/>নেপচুনিয়াম (\(\mathrm{Np}\)) এবং প্লুটোনিয়াম (\(\mathrm{Pu}\)) +7 পর্যন্ত জারণ অবস্থা প্রদর্শন করে, যেমন \([\mathrm{NpO}_5]^{3-}\) এবং \([\mathrm{PuO}_5]^{3-}\) স্পিসিসে।<br/>ইউরেনিয়াম (\(\mathrm{U}\)) সর্বোচ্চ +6 জারণ অবস্থা প্রদর্শন করে (যেমন ইউরেনাইল আয়ন, \(\mathrm{UO}_2^{2+}\)), অন্যদিকে অ্যামেরিসিয়াম (\(\mathrm{Am}\)) সর্বোচ্চ +6 পর্যন্ত জারণ অবস্থা দেখায়।</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Electronic Configurations</strong><br/>Evaluating the electronic configurations of the given actinoids:<br/>Uranium (\(\mathrm{U}\), \(Z = 92\)): \([\mathrm{Rn}]\,5f^3\,6d^1\,7s^2\), which contains exactly one electron in the \(6d\) subshell.<br/>Neptunium (\(\mathrm{Np}\), \(Z = 93\)): \([\mathrm{Rn}]\,5f^4\,6d^1\,7s^2\), which also contains exactly one electron in the \(6d\) subshell.<br/>Plutonium (\(\mathrm{Pu}\), \(Z = 94\)): \([\mathrm{Rn}]\,5f^6\,7s^2\), which has no electrons in the \(6d\) subshell (\(6d^0\)).<br/>Americium (\(\mathrm{Am}\), \(Z = 95\)): \([\mathrm{Rn}]\,5f^7\,7s^2\), which has no electrons in the \(6d\) subshell (\(6d^0\)).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The actinoids that possess one electron in the \(6d\) orbital are uranium (\(\mathrm{U}\)) and neptunium (\(\mathrm{Np}\)).</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Although +3 is the common and characteristic oxidation state for all lanthanoids, +2 oxidation states arise in certain elements due to the extra thermodynamic stability associated with empty (\(4f^0\)), half-filled (\(4f^7\)), or completely filled (\(4f^{14}\)) f-subshell configurations.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Electronic Configuration Analysis</strong><br/>Europium (\(\mathrm{Eu}\), \(Z = 63\)) has the ground-state electronic configuration \([\mathrm{Xe}]\,4f^7\,6s^2\). By losing two \(6s\) electrons, it forms \(\mathrm{Eu}^{2+}\) with a highly stable half-filled \(4f^7\) configuration.<br/>Ytterbium (\(\mathrm{Yb}\), \(Z = 70\)) has the ground-state electronic configuration \([\mathrm{Xe}]\,4f^{14}\,6s^2\). By losing two \(6s\) electrons, it forms \(\mathrm{Yb}^{2+}\) with an exceptionally stable completely filled \(4f^{14}\) configuration.<br/>Cerium (\(\mathrm{Ce}\), \(Z = 58\)) shows +3 and +4 oxidation states, while holmium (\(\mathrm{Ho}\), \(Z = 67\)) exhibits predominantly the +3 oxidation state.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Europium (\(\mathrm{Eu}\)) and ytterbium (\(\mathrm{Yb}\)) show the +2 oxidation state in addition to +3.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: ধারণাগত নীতি</strong><br/>যদিও সমস্ত ল্যান্থানয়েডের সাধারণ ও বৈশিষ্ট্যপূর্ণ জারণ অবস্থা হল +3, তবুও খালি (\(4f^0\)), অর্ধপূর্ণ (\(4f^7\)) বা সম্পূর্ণ পূর্ণ (\(4f^{14}\)) f-উপকক্ষের বিশেষ তাপগতীয় স্থায়িত্বের কারণে কিছু ল্যান্থানয়েড +2 জারণ অবস্থা প্রদর্শন করে।</p>
<p><strong>ধাপ ২: ইলেকট্রন বিন্যাস বিশ্লেষণ</strong><br/>ইউরোপিয়াম (\(\mathrm{Eu}\), \(Z = 63\))-এর ভূমিস্তরে ইলেকট্রন বিন্যাস \([\mathrm{Xe}]\,4f^7\,6s^2\)। দুটি \(6s\) ইলেকট্রন ত্যাগের মাধ্যমে এটি অত্যন্ত সুস্থিত অর্ধপূর্ণ \(4f^7\) বিন্যাসযুক্ত \(\mathrm{Eu}^{2+}\) ক্যাটায়ন গঠন করে।<br/>ইটারবিয়াম (\(\mathrm{Yb}\), \(Z = 70\))-এর ভূমিস্তরে ইলেকট্রন বিন্যাস \([\mathrm{Xe}]\,4f^{14}\,6s^2\)। দুটি \(6s\) ইলেকট্রন বর্জনের মাধ্যমে এটি অত্যন্ত সুস্থিত সম্পূর্ণ পূর্ণ \(4f^{14}\) বিন্যাসযুক্ত \(\mathrm{Yb}^{2+}\) ক্যাটায়ন তৈরি করে।<br/>সেরিয়াম (\(\mathrm{Ce}\), \(Z = 58\)) প্রধানত +3 এবং +4 জারণ অবস্থা দেখায়, অন্যদিকে হলমিয়াম (\(\mathrm{Ho}\), \(Z = 67\)) মূলত +3 জারণ অবস্থা প্রদর্শন করে।</p>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_MCQ_Q28 - Ions with higher spin only magnetic moment</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Which of the following ions show higher spin only magnetic moment value?</p><hr/><p>নিম্নলিখিত আয়নগুলির মধ্যে কোনগুলি তুলনামূলকভাবে উচ্চ ঘূর্ণন-ভিত্তিক চৌম্বক ভ্রামক মান প্রদর্শন করে?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Formula and Principle</strong><br/>The spin-only magnetic moment is given by \(\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\text{ B.M.}\), where \(n\) represents the number of unpaired d-electrons. A greater number of unpaired electrons yields a higher magnetic moment.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Calculation of Unpaired Electrons and Moments</strong><br/>\(\mathrm{Ti}^{3+}\) has atomic number \(Z = 22\). Its configuration is \(3d^1\), so \(n = 1\), giving \(\mu = \sqrt{1(3)} = 1.73\text{ B.M.}\)<br/>\(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) has atomic number \(Z = 25\). Its configuration is \(3d^5\), so \(n = 5\), giving \(\mu = \sqrt{5(7)} = 5.92\text{ B.M.}\)<br/>\(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) has atomic number \(Z = 26\). Its configuration is \(3d^6\), having \(n = 4\) unpaired electrons, giving \(\mu = \sqrt{4(6)} = 4.90\text{ B.M.}\)<br/>\(\mathrm{Co}^{3+}\) has atomic number \(Z = 27\). Its configuration is \(3d^6\), having \(n = 4\) unpaired electrons, giving \(\mu = \sqrt{4(6)} = 4.90\text{ B.M.}\)</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Comparison and Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The ions with the highest numbers of unpaired electrons and consequently the highest spin-only magnetic moments are manganese(II) with \(5.92\text{ B.M.}\) and iron(II) with \(4.90\text{ B.M.}\)</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>ধাপ ১: সূত্র ও নীতি</strong><br/>ঘূর্ণন-ভিত্তিক চৌম্বক ভ্রামক নির্ণয়ের সূত্রটি হল \(\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\text{ B.M.}\), যেখানে \(n\) হল অযুগ্ম d-ইলেকট্রনের সংখ্যা। অযুগ্ম ইলেকট্রনের সংখ্যা যত বেশি হয়, চৌম্বক ভ্রামকের মানও তত বৃদ্ধি পায়।</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Step-by-Step Association of Pairs</strong><br/>Osmium (\(\mathrm{Os}\)), a member of the platinum group (\(5d\) series), can involve all eight valence electrons (\(5d^6 6s^2\)) in bonding with oxygen, thereby exhibiting the exceptional +8 oxidation state in osmium tetroxide (\(\mathrm{OsO}_4\)).<br/>Manganese (\(\mathrm{Mn}\)), a \(3d\) block element with ground state configuration \(3d^5 4s^2\), can lose or share all seven valence electrons to reach the maximum oxidation state of +7 (as in permanganate, \(\mathrm{MnO}_4^-\)).<br/>Chromium (\(\mathrm{Cr}\)), with a half-filled electronic configuration of \(3d^5 4s^1\), possesses six unpaired electrons available for interatomic metallic bonding, resulting in the highest melting point among the first transition (\(3d\)) series elements.<br/>Iron (\(\mathrm{Fe}\)) serves as an unselected distractor.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The correct matching associations are: An element which can show +8 oxidation state pairs with Os; 3d block element that can show upto +7 oxidation state pairs with Mn; and 3d block element with highest melting point pairs with Cr.</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Evaluation of Assertion</strong><br/>Osmium exhibits oxidation states ranging from 0 up to +8, reaching +8 in compounds such as osmium tetroxide (\(\mathrm{OsO}_4\)) and osmium octafluoride (\(\mathrm{OsF}_8\)). Therefore, the assertion is correct.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Reason</strong><br/>Osmium has atomic number \(Z = 76\) and belongs to the third transition series (\(5d\) series) of the d-block with ground-state electronic configuration \([\mathrm{Xe}]\,4f^{14}\,5d^6\,6s^2\). Therefore, the statement in the reason is factually correct.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Evaluation of Explanation</strong><br/>Belonging to the \(5d\) series does not automatically mean an element will exhibit a +8 oxidation state; for example, other \(5d\) elements such as tungsten (+6), gold (+3), and platinum (+6) do not show +8. Osmium exhibits a +8 oxidation state because it has eight valence electrons (\(5d^6 6s^2\)) and can share all eight electrons with highly electronegative oxygen atoms to form strong metal-oxygen multiple bonds. Hence, while both statements are true, being a \(5d\) element is not the explanation for displaying the +8 oxidation state.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Both (A) and (R) are correct but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A).</p>
