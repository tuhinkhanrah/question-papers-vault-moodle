  <name><text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_THE_P_BLOCK_ELEMENTS_MCQ_Q04 - Participation of elements in pi backbonding mechanisms</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>Which of the following elements can be involved in \(p\pi\text{–}d\pi\) bonding?</p><hr/><p>निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा तत्व \(p\pi\text{–}d\pi\) आबंधन में भाग ले सकता है?</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The formation of a \(p\pi\text{–}d\pi\) bond requires an element to have vacant \(d\)-orbitals of suitable energy in its valence shell to overlap with the filled or partially filled \(p\)-orbitals of an adjacent atom.</p><p><strong>Evaluation</strong><br/>Carbon, Nitrogen, and Boron are elements of the second period of the periodic table with principal quantum number \(n = 2\).
