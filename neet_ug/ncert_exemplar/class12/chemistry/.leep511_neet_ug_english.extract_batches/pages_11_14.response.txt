Sodium ethoxide is instead synthesized using active sodium metal. Therefore, the reason is an incorrect statement.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>The assertion is a correct statement, but the reason is an incorrect statement.</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>
