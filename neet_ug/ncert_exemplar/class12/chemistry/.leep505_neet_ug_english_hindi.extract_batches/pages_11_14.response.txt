<p><strong>Reason (R):</strong> Pore size of the filter paper becomes more than the size of colloidal particle.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>अभिकथन (A):</strong> कॉलोडियन विलयन से संसेचित एक साधारण फिल्टर पेपर कोलॉइडी कणों के प्रवाह को रोक देता है।</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Ultrafiltration</strong><br/>
Ordinary filter paper has relatively large capillary pore dimensions (greater than \(1000\text{ nm}\)), allowing both the dispersion medium and colloidal particles to pass through unhindered. When ordinary filter paper is impregnated with collodion (a \(4\%\) solution of nitrocellulose in an alcohol-ether mixture) and subsequently hardened by soaking in formaldehyde, the effective diameter of the pores is significantly reduced.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of the Assertion</strong><br/>
The reduced pore dimensions of the treated filter paper become smaller than the diameter of colloidal particles (which range between \(1\text{ nm}\) and \(1000\text{ nm}\)). As a result, the impregnated filter paper acts as an ultrafilter that stops the flow of colloidal particles while allowing solvent molecules and crystalloid ions to pass through freely. Thus, the assertion is scientifically correct.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Analysis of the Reason</strong><br/>
Impregnating filter paper with collodion decreases the pore size of the paper so that the pores become substantially smaller than colloidal particles, rather than larger. Therefore, the reason claiming that the pore size becomes larger than colloidal particles is false.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The assertion is correct, but the reason is incorrect.

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Colligative Properties</strong><br/>
Colligative properties depend exclusively upon the total number of solute particles present per unit volume of solution, regardless of their nature, identity, or chemical structure.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of the Assertion</strong><br/>
Colloidal solutions are heterogeneous dispersions containing individual dispersed phase entities; hence, they do exhibit colligative properties such as osmotic pressure, depression in freezing point, elevation in boiling point, and relative lowering of vapor pressure. Thus, the assertion is correct.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Analysis of the Reason and its Relationship</strong><br/>
Colloidal particles consist of large molecular aggregates or macromolecules whose sizes range between \(1\text{ nm}\) and \(1000\text{ nm}\), which is substantially larger than simple solute molecules in true solutions. Because colloidal particles are large in size, the total number of particles in a given mass of colloidal solute is vastly smaller than the number of particles in a true solution of equivalent concentration. Consequently, colloidal solutions exhibit colligative values that are extraordinarily small in magnitude compared to true solutions. While the statement regarding particle size is scientifically correct, having large particles accounts for the small magnitude of the properties rather than explaining the fundamental occurrence of colligative phenomena.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Both the assertion and the reason are correct, but the reason is not the correct explanation of the assertion.
</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Brownian Motion</strong><br/>
When colloidal sols are viewed under an ultramicroscope, the dispersed particles are observed to undergo continuous, rapid, zig-zag motion known as Brownian movement. This motion is caused by the ceaseless, unbalanced mechanical bombardment of the colloidal particles by thermal solvent molecules in the continuous dispersion medium.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of the Assertion</strong><br/>
Because Brownian motion is a universal characteristic feature of all true colloidal sols, stating that colloidal solutions do not show Brownian motion is scientifically false. Thus, the assertion is incorrect.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Analysis of the Reason</strong><br/>
Brownian motion imparts a continuous stirring and agitation effect that counteracts the downward gravitational pull acting on colloidal particles, thereby preventing sedimentation and providing kinetic stability to the sol. Thus, the reason is correct.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The assertion is incorrect, but the reason is correct.
</p>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_CHEMISTRY_SURFACE_CHEMISTRY_ASSERTION_REASON_Q76 - Coagulating power and Hardy Schulze rule</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Assertion (A):</strong> Coagulation power of \(\text{Al}^{3+}\) is more than \(\text{Na}^+\).</p>
<p><strong>Reason (R):</strong> Greater the valency of the flocculating ion added, greater is its power to cause precipitation (Hardy Schulze rule).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>अभिकथन (A):</strong> \(\text{Al}^{3+}\) की स्कंदन शक्ति \(\text{Na}^+\) से अधिक होती है।</p>
<p><strong>कारण (R):</strong> मिलाए गए ऊर्णनकारी आयन की संयोजकता जितनी अधिक होती है, अवक्षेपण करने की उसकी शक्ति उतनी ही अधिक होती है (हार्डी-शुल्त्स नियम)।</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Coagulation and Hardy-Schulze Rule</strong><br/>
Coagulation of a colloidal sol by an electrolyte occurs when counter-ions neutralize the electrical charges on the colloidal particles, allowing them to aggregate and precipitate. According to the Hardy-Schulze rule, the coagulating (or flocculating) power of an electrolyte is directly dependent on the valency of the active flocculating ion carrying a charge opposite to that of the colloidal particles: greater the valency of the flocculating ion added, greater is its power to cause precipitation.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of the Assertion and Reason</strong><br/>
The aluminum cation carries a charge of \(+3\) (valency of \(3\)), whereas the sodium cation carries a charge of \(+1\) (valency of \(1\)). In accordance with the Hardy-Schulze rule, the coagulation power of a trivalent ion is vastly greater than that of a monovalent ion. Thus, aluminum ion has a significantly higher coagulating power than sodium ion.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relationship between Assertion and Reason</strong><br/>
The higher valency of aluminum compared to sodium directly explains why aluminum ions exhibit greater coagulation power than sodium ions. Hence, the reason is the correct scientific explanation of the assertion.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Both the assertion and the reason are correct, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.
</p>

<p><strong>चरण 1: स्कंदन का संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत और हार्डी-शुल्त्स नियम</strong><br/>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Detergent Action and CMC</strong><br/>
The cleansing action of surface-active agents like detergents involves the aggregation of surfactant ions into spherical or cylindrical clusters termed micelles. These micelles encapsulate hydrophobic dirt, oil, and grease particles within their non-polar interior hydrocarbon core while exposing hydrophilic polar groups to the surrounding water, thus solubilizing and removing greasy stains.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Threshold Concentration Requirement</strong><br/>
Micelle formation takes place only when the surfactant concentration in the wash liquor equals or exceeds a characteristic threshold known as the critical micelle concentration. Below this concentration, surfactant molecules remain dispersed as individual ions and cannot effectively solubilize greasy soils.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Relationship between CMC and Economy</strong><br/>
When a detergent has a lower critical micelle concentration, a substantially smaller quantity of detergent is needed per unit volume of water to reach the micelle-forming regime and perform effective cleaning. Consequently, less detergent product is consumed per wash, making low-CMC detergents significantly more economical to use. Hence, the reason provides the direct scientific justification for the assertion.
</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Both the assertion and the reason are correct, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.
</p>

<p><strong>चरण 1: अपमार्जक क्रिया और CMC का संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
अपमार्जक जैसे पृष्ठसक्रियक पदार्थों की स्वच्छीकारक क्रिया में पृष्ठसक्रियक आयनों का पुंजित होकर मिसेल नामक संरचनाओं का निर्माण शामिल होता है। ये मिसेल जलविरोधी गंदगी, तेल और ग्रीस के कणों को अपने अध्रुवीय आंतरिक हाइड्रोकार्बन भाग में फँसा लेते हैं जबकि जलरागी ध्रुवीय समूहों को चारों ओर के जल के संपर्क में रखते हैं, जिससे चिकनाई वाले दाग घुलकर बाहर निकल जाते हैं।

<p><strong>चरण 2: सीमांत सांद्रता की आवश्यकता</strong><br/>

</p>
