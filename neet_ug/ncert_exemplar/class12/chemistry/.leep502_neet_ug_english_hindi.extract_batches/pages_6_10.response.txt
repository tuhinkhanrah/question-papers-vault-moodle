  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The solubility of a gas in a liquid depends on three main factors: the nature of the gas (solute) and solvent, the temperature, and the pressure of the gas above the liquid.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>At constant temperature \(T\), the solubility of a gas in a fixed volume of liquid solvent depends on the nature of the solute and the pressure (according to Henry's law, \(p = K_{\mathrm{H}} x\)).   <name><text>NCERTEXEMPLAR_CHEMISTRY_SOLUTIONS_MCQII_Q28 - Ideal solution benzene and toluene properties</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>Intermolecular forces between two benzene molecules are nearly of same strength as those between two toluene molecules. For a mixture of benzene and toluene, which of the following are <strong>not</strong> true?</p><hr/><p>दो बेंजीन अणुओं के मध्य अंतराअणुक बल लगभग उतने ही प्रबल होते हैं जितने दो टॉलूईन अणुओं के मध्य होते हैं। बेंजीन तथा टॉलूईन के मिश्रण के लिए निम्नलिखित में से कौन-से कथन सत्य <strong>नहीं</strong> हैं?</p>]]></text></questiontext>
