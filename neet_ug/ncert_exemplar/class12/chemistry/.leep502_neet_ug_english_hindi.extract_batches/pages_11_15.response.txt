  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>Osmosis is the spontaneous net flow of solvent molecules through a semi-permeable membrane from pure solvent (or a more dilute solution, where solvent concentration is higher) into a more concentrated solution.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation</strong><br/>When a solution is separated from pure solvent by a semi-permeable membrane, solvent molecules move from the pure solvent side to the solution side due to osmosis, so the assertion is correct. While diffusion in general is described as movement from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration, osmosis specifically involves the movement of solvent molecules from higher solvent concentration (pure solvent or dilute solution) to lower solvent concentration (concentrated solution) across a semi-permeable membrane, rather than movement from a high-solute-concentration solution to a low-solute-concentration solution.
