<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Therefore, primitive cubic unit cell matches with having one atom and \(a = b = c\); body centred cubic matches with \(a = b = c\) and one additional body atom; face centred cubic matches with \(a = b = c\) and three additional face atoms; and end centred orthorhombic matches with \(a \neq b \neq c\) and one additional end-face atom.</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>

In semiconductors, the valence band is completely filled at absolute zero and the conduction band is completely empty, with a small energy gap between them. Thermal energy at higher temperatures excites electrons across this small gap into the conduction band, imparting intermediate conductivity. A partially filled valence band is characteristic of metallic conductors, not semiconductors.</p>
