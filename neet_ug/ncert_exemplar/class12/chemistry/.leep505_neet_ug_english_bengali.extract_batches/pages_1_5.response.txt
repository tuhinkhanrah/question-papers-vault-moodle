  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>The spontaneity of adsorption at constant temperature and pressure is governed by the Gibbs free energy change, \(\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S\).</p><p><strong>Step 2: Application</strong><br/>At the equilibrium position, the Gibbs free energy change becomes zero (\(\Delta G = 0\)).
