Chromatography is a separation technique based on the differential adsorption or partition of mixture components on a stationary phase, yielding visible separated coloured bands along the adsorbent column. In the Mond process, impure nickel is converted into a volatile carbonyl complex, \( \mathrm{Ni(CO)}_{4} \), by heating with carbon monoxide, which on subsequent thermal decomposition yields pure nickel. Zone refining operates on the principle that impurities are substantially more soluble in the molten zone than in the corresponding solid state, which makes it ideal for producing ultrapure semiconductor elements such as germanium and silicon. Mercury is a volatile metal with a low boiling point (\( 357^\circ\mathrm{C} \)) that is purified from non-volatile impurities via fractional distillation.</p>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Principles of Metallurgical Operations</strong><br/>
The cyanide process (MacArthur-Forrest process) is a hydrometallurgical technique in which native gold (Au) is leached using dilute sodium cyanide solution in the presence of air to form the soluble dicyanoaurate(I) complex, \( [\mathrm{Au(CN)}_{2}]^{-} \), from which gold is subsequently recovered by zinc displacement. The froth flotation process exploits preferential wetting of sulphide mineral particles by collectors to concentrate or dress sulphide ores like zinc blende (\( \mathrm{ZnS} \)). Electrolytic reduction in the Hall-Héroult process reduces alumina dissolved in fused cryolite to yield aluminium metal. Zone refining is used for producing ultrapure semiconductor specimens of germanium (Ge) and silicon.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis and Pairing</strong><br/>
- Cyanide process pairs with Extraction of Au.<br/>
- Froth Floatation Process pairs with Dressing of ZnS.<br/>
- Electrolytic reduction pairs with Extraction of Al.<br/>
- Zone refining pairs with Ultrapure Ge.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The correct correlation pairs Cyanide process with Extraction of Au, Froth Floatation Process with Dressing of ZnS, Electrolytic reduction with Extraction of Al, and Zone refining with Ultrapure Ge.</p>

<p><strong>चरण 1: धातुकर्म प्रक्रमों के सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
सायनाइड प्रक्रम एक जलधातुकर्म विधि है जिसमें वायु की उपस्थिति में तनु सोडियम सायनाइड विलयन द्वारा स्वर्ण (Au) का निक्षालन करके विलेय डाइसायनाइडोऑरेट(I) संकुल \( [\mathrm{Au(CN)}_{2}]^{-} \) बनाया जाता है, जिससे बाद में जिंक द्वारा विस्थापन करके सोना प्राप्त किया जाता है। फेन प्लवन प्रक्रम सल्फाइड खनिजों के तेल द्वारा तरलता पर आधारित है, जिसका उपयोग जिंक ब्लेंड (\( \mathrm{ZnS} \)) जैसे सल्फाइड अयस्कों के सांद्रण (प्रसाधन) में होता है। विद्युत-अपघटनी अपचयन (हॉल-हेरॉल्ट प्रक्रम) में गलित क्रायोलाइट में घुली ऐलुमिना के अपचयन से ऐलुमिनियम धातु प्राप्त की जाती है। मंडल परिष्करण का उपयोग जर्मेनियम (Ge) और सिलिकॉन की अतिशुद्ध अवस्था प्राप्त करने के लिए किया जाता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: विश्लेषण एवं सुमेलन</strong><br/>

  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Vapour Phase Refining</strong><br/>
Vapour phase refining requires that the crude metal readily forms a volatile compound with an available reagent at a moderate temperature, and that this volatile compound readily decomposes at a higher temperature to regenerate the pure metal.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Assertion and Reason</strong><br/>
In the Mond process, crude nickel is treated with carbon monoxide gas at \( 330\text{--}350\mathrm{K} \) to form the volatile complex nickel tetracarbonyl: \( \mathrm{Ni} + 4\mathrm{CO} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Ni(CO)}_{4} \). This confirms the assertion.<br/>
The vapours of \( \mathrm{Ni(CO)}_{4} \) are subsequently heated to \( 450\text{--}470\mathrm{K} \) (approximately \( 460\mathrm{K} \)), where the complex decomposes to deposit pure nickel metal while releasing carbon monoxide gas: \( \mathrm{Ni(CO)}_{4} \xrightarrow{460\mathrm{K}} \mathrm{Ni} + 4\mathrm{CO} \). This confirms the reason.<br/>
Because the purification of nickel by the Mond process relies precisely on the formation and thermal decomposition of volatile \( \mathrm{Ni(CO)}_{4} \) at \( 460\mathrm{K} \), the reason constitutes the exact and correct scientific explanation of the assertion.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>

Hydrometallurgy is the branch of extractive metallurgy that involves the use of aqueous chemistry for the recovery of metals from ores, concentrates, and recycled materials. It fundamentally consists of leaching (dissolving the target metal from the ore into an aqueous solution using a suitable chemical or biological reagent) followed by recovery of the metal through displacement/precipitation using a more electropositive metal (or another reducing agent).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Assertion and Reason</strong><br/>
