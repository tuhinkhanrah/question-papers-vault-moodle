  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>In angiosperms, the ovule functions as the megasporangium and houses the female gametophyte, which is the embryo sac.</p><p><strong>Analogous Evaluation</strong><br/>Similarly, the anther contains microsporangia which produce and house the male gametophyte, which is the pollen grain.
