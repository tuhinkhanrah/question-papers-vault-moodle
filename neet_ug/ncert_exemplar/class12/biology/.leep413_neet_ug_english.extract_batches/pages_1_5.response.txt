Initial population, \(N_0 = 50\)<br/>
Increase in population, \(\Delta N = 100\)<br/>
Time interval, \(\Delta t = 1\text{ hour}\)</p>
<p><strong>Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>
The per cent growth or birth rate per individual per hour represents the per capita rate expressed as a percentage.</p>
<p><strong>Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The per capita growth rate is:
\[r = \frac{\Delta N}{N_0 \times \Delta t}\]
The percentage growth rate per individual per hour is:
\[\text{Percentage growth rate} = \left(\frac{\Delta N}{N_0}\right) \times 100\%\]
</p>
<p><strong>Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Calculate the per capita growth:
\[\frac{\Delta N}{N_0} = \frac{100}{50} = 2\]
Express the value as a percentage:
\[2 \times 100\% = 200\%\]
</p>
<p><strong>Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
An addition of 100 individuals across 50 initial individuals yields a 200 per cent growth rate per individual per hour.</p>]]></text>
