    <text><![CDATA[<p>A hemisphere is uniformly charged positively. The electric field at a point on a diameter away from the centre is directed</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>A hemisphere has a uniform positive charge distribution.<br/>Point P lies on the base diameter away from the center of the hemisphere.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>Let the flat circular base of the hemisphere lie in the \(xy\)-plane with the origin \(O(0, 0, 0)\) at the center of the base.<br/>The diameter lies along the \(x\)-axis, and point P is located at \((x_0, 0, 0)\) with \(x_0 \neq 0\).<br/>The curved dome of the hemisphere lies in the region \(z > 0\).</p><p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>The electric field produced by an element of charge \(dq\) at displacement vector \(\mathbf{r}\) is:<br/>\[d\mathbf{E} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{dq}{r^2}\hat{r}\]<br/>By symmetry considerations, if a charge distribution exhibits reflectional symmetry with respect to a plane, the integrated field component perpendicular to that symmetry plane must vanish.</p><p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>Consider the plane passing through point P that is perpendicular to the diameter (the \(yz\)-plane shifted to \(x = x_0\)).<br/>Due to the reflection symmetry of the hemisphere about the plane perpendicular to the diameter and passing through the center, charge elements can be grouped in symmetric pairs.<br/>When integrating the Coulomb contributions over the entire uniformly charged hemisphere, the components of the electric field along the diameter cancel out completely:<br/>\[E_{\parallel} = 0\]<br/>Since the positive charge distribution is situated entirely on the positive \(z\) side of the base plane, each charge element exerts a repulsive electric field with a non-zero component pointing away from the hemisphere (along \(-z\)).<br/>Hence, the net electric field at point P has only a non-zero component directed normal to the flat base plane, which is perpendicular to the diameter.</p><p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>Any non-zero component parallel to the diameter would violate the symmetry of the charge configuration.<br/>Therefore, the electric field at a point on a diameter away from the centre is directed perpendicular to the diameter.</p>]]></text>
