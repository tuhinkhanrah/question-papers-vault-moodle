<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>Therefore, \(m\) is kept less than or equal to \(1\) because \(m > 1\) indicates an amplitude of the message signal greater than that of the carrier signal resulting in distortion, and it results in overlapping of both sidebands resulting in loss of information.</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>
