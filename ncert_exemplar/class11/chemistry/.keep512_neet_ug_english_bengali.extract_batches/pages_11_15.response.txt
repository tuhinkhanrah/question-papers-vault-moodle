  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Evaluate the Reason (R)</strong><br/>Simple distillation is suitable for separating miscible liquids whose boiling points differ significantly (generally by more than \(20^{\circ}\mathrm{C}\) to \(25^{\circ}\mathrm{C}\)) so that the lower-boiling component vaporises and distils over first in a relatively pure state. Thus, Reason (R) is correct.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Evaluate the Assertion (A)</strong><br/>The boiling point of propan-1-ol is \(97^{\circ}\mathrm{C}\) and that of propanone is \(56^{\circ}\mathrm{C}\).
