  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>Two positive charges \(q_2 &gt; 0\) and \(q_3 &gt; 0\) are fixed on the \(y\)-axis symmetrically on either side of the origin \(O\). The electric field due to a positive charge points radially away from it.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>According to Coulomb's law and the principle of superposition, the net force on \(q_1\) is \(\mathbf{F}_{\text{net}} = q_1 \mathbf{E}_{\text{net}}\), where \(\mathbf{E}_{\text{net}}\) is the vector sum of electric fields at \(q_1\) due to all other charges.</p><p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>Since \(q_2 &gt; 0\) and \(q_3 &gt; 0\) are located on the \(y\)-axis and \(q_1\) is on the negative \(x\)-axis, the electric field vectors at \(q_1\) due to \(q_2\) and \(q_3\) both have components along the \(-x\) direction. Thus, their resultant electric field at \(q_1\) points along the \(-x\) direction.<br/>However, the net force on \(q_1\) is given to be in the \(+x\) direction (opposite to the electric field). Because \(\mathbf{F} = q_1 \mathbf{E}\), the charge \(q_1\) must be negative (\(q_1 &lt; 0\)).<br/>When a positive charge \(Q &gt; 0\) is placed at \((x, 0)\) on the positive \(x\)-axis, it exerts an attractive force on the negative charge \(q_1\), which is also directed along the \(+x\) direction.</p><p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>By superposition, the new force adds constructively to the existing force along the \(+x\) axis.   <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>A positive charge \(Q\) is uniformly distributed along a circular ring of radius \(R\).   <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>A circular ring of radius \(R\) carries a uniformly distributed positive charge \(Q\). The repulsive force from the nearer part of the ring becomes stronger than that from the farther part, pushing \(q\) back towards the centre. Hence, \(q &gt; 0\) is in stable equilibrium in the plane of the ring.<br/>2. <strong>Displacement in the plane of the ring for \(q &lt; 0\):</strong> When \(q &lt; 0\) is displaced towards one side in the plane of the ring, the attractive force from the nearer part of the positively charged ring exceeds that from the farther part. Thus, it experiences a net force away from the centre and continues moving until it hits the ring.<br/>3. <strong>Small displacement along the axis for \(q &lt; 0\):</strong> For \(|z| \ll R\), the axial electric field approximates to:<br/>\[E_z \approx \frac{Qz}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 R^3}\]<br/>For \(q &lt; 0\), the force is:<br/>\[F_z = qE_z = -\frac{|q|Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 R^3} z\]<br/>Since \(F_z \propto -z\) is a restoring force directed towards the centre, the charge \(q &lt; 0\) performs simple harmonic motion (SHM) for small displacements along the axis.</p><p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>Therefore: for \(q &gt; 0\) displaced in the plane, it is pushed back towards the centre (stable equilibrium in the plane); for \(q &lt; 0\) displaced in the plane, it continues moving till it hits the ring; and for \(q &lt; 0\), it performs SHM for small displacements along the axis.</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और शर्तें</strong><br/>\(R\) त्रिज्या के एक वृत्ताकार वलय पर धनात्मक आवेश \(Q\) एकसमान रूप से वितरित है। वलय के केंद्र पर एक छोटा परीक्षण आवेश \(q\) रखा गया है।</p><p><strong>चरण 2: मान्यताएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>वलय के तल में विस्थापन त्रिज्यीय है, जबकि वलय के अक्ष के अनुदिश विस्थापन को \(z\) से दर्शाया गया है (छोटे विस्थापन के लिए \(|z| \ll R\))।</p><p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>1. \(R\) त्रिज्या तथा \(Q\) आवेश वाले एकसमान रूप से आवेशित वलय के केंद्र से अक्षीय दूरी \(z\) पर वैद्युत क्षेत्र है:<br/>\[E_z = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{Qz}{(R^2 + z^2)^{3/2}}\]<br/>जो अक्ष के अनुदिश केंद्र से दूर की ओर दिष्ट होता है।<br/>2. परीक्षण आवेश \(q\) पर लगने वाला बल \(\mathbf{F} = q\mathbf{E}\) होता है।</p><p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>1. <strong>\(q &gt;
