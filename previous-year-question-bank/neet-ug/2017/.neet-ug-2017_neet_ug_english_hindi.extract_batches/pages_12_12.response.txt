  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Which of the following facilitates opening of stomatal aperture?</p>
<hr/>
<p>निम्नलिखित में से कौन रंध्र छिद्र (स्टोमेटा) के खुलने को सुगम बनाता है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Stomatal Mechanics</strong><br/>
The opening and closing of the stomatal pore is driven by reversible turgor changes in the flanking specialized epidermal guard cells, supported by specific cell wall architectures.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Anatomical Alignment of Guard Cell Microfibrils</strong><br/>
Guard cells possess differential wall thickness: the outer walls facing adjacent subsidiary cells are thin and elastic, whereas the inner walls flanking the stomatal pore are thick and inelastic. Within the cell walls, cellulose microfibrils are oriented <strong>radially</strong> rather than longitudinally around the cell axis. When guard cells absorb water and become turgid, the radial micellation prevents the cells from expanding radially; instead, it forces them to buckle longitudinally. The expanding thin outer walls pull the thick inner crescentic walls outward, thereby opening the stomatal aperture.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Evaluation of Other Factors</strong><br/>
- Longitudinal orientation would cause the guard cells to shorten rather than bow apart.<br/>
- A decrease in turgidity causes the guard cells to become flaccid, which closes the stomatal pore.<br/>
- Outward bulging (not contraction) of the thin outer wall occurs during stomatal opening.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Opening of the stomatal aperture is structurally facilitated by the radial orientation of cellulose microfibrils in the cell wall of guard cells.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: रंध्र गतििकी का सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
रंध्र छिद्र का खुलना और बंद होना द्वार कोशिकाओं (गार्ड सेल्स) की स्फीति (turgidity) में होने वाले परिवर्तनों तथा उनकी भित्ति की विशिष्ट संरचनात्मक व्यवस्था पर निर्भर करता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: सेल्यूलोज सूक्ष्मतंतुओं का त्रिज्य विन्यास</strong><br/>
द्वार कोशिकाओं की बाहरी भित्ति पतली और लचीली होती है, जबकि रंध्र छिद्र की ओर वाली भीतरी भित्ति अत्यधिक मोटी और दृढ़ होती है। द्वार कोशिकाओं की भित्ति में सेल्यूलोज के सूक्ष्मतंतु (माइक्रोफाइब्रिल्स) लंबवत होने के बजाय <strong>त्रिज्य रूप से (radially)</strong> व्यवस्थित होते हैं। जब अंतःपरासरण द्वारा द्वार कोशिकाएं स्फीत होती हैं, तो यह त्रिज्य विन्यास उन्हें चौड़ाई में फूलने से रोकता है और उन्हें लंबाई में धनुषाकार मुड़ने पर विवश करता है। इससे पतली बाहरी भित्ति बाहर की ओर फूलती है और भीतरी मोटी भित्ति को बाहर खींचकर रंध्र छिद्र को खोल देती है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: अन्य विकल्पों का विश्लेषण</strong><br/>

- स्फीति में कमी होने पर द्वार कोशिकाएं शिथिल हो जाती हैं और रंध्र बंद हो जाता है।</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p>The genotypes of a Husband and Wife are \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^B\) and \(\text{I}^A i\). Among the blood types of their children, how many different genotypes and phenotypes are possible?</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक पति एवं पत्नी का जीनोटाइप क्रमशः \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^B\) और \(\text{I}^A i\) है। उनकी संतानों के रक्त समूहों में कितने विभिन्न जीनोटाइप तथा फीनोटाइप संभव हैं?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Parental Genotypes</strong><br/>
- Husband's genotype: \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^B\) (codominant, blood group AB), producing gametes \(\text{I}^A\) and \(\text{I}^B\) in equal proportions.<br/>
- Wife's genotype: \(\text{I}^A i\) (heterozygous, blood group A), producing gametes \(\text{I}^A\) and \(i\) in equal proportions.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Punnett Square Analysis</strong><br/>
Combining the gametes in a \(2 \times 2\) grid yields four offspring genotypes:
1. \(\text{I}^A \times \text{I}^A \to \text{I}^A\text{I}^A\)
2. \(\text{I}^A \times i \to \text{I}^A i\)
3. \(\text{I}^B \times \text{I}^A \to \text{I}^A\text{I}^B\)
4. \(\text{I}^B \times i \to \text{I}^B i\)</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Determining Genotypes and Phenotypes</strong><br/>
- <strong>Distinct Genotypes:</strong> There are 4 distinct genotypes: \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^A\), \(\text{I}^A i\), \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^B\), and \(\text{I}^B i\).<br/>
- <strong>Distinct Phenotypes:</strong>
  - \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^A\) and \(\text{I}^A i\) express <strong>Blood group A</strong>.
  - \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^B\) expresses <strong>Blood group AB</strong>.
  - \(\text{I}^B i\) expresses <strong>Blood group B</strong>.<br/>
Thus, there are 3 distinct blood group phenotypes (A, AB, and B).</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The cross yields exactly 4 genotypes and 3 phenotypes.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: जनकों के जीनोटाइप</strong><br/>
- पति का जीनोटाइप: \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^B\) (युग्मक: \(\text{I}^A\) तथा \(\text{I}^B\))<br/>
- पत्नी का जीनोटाइप: \(\text{I}^A i\) (युग्मक: \(\text{I}^A\) तथा \(i\))</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: पनेट वर्ग द्वारा संकरण</strong><br/>
युग्मकों के संयोजन से संतानों में निम्नलिखित चार संयोजन प्राप्त होते हैं:
1. \(\text{I}^A \times \text{I}^A \to \text{I}^A\text{I}^A\)
2. \(\text{I}^A \times i \to \text{I}^A i\)
3. \(\text{I}^B \times \text{I}^A \to \text{I}^A\text{I}^B\)
4. \(\text{I}^B \times i \to \text{I}^B i\)</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: जीनोटाइप और फीनोटाइप की गणना</strong><br/>
- <strong>संभावित जीनोटाइप (4 प्रकार):</strong> \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^A\), \(\text{I}^A i\), \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^B\), \(\text{I}^B i\)।<br/>
- <strong>संभावित फीनोटाइप (3 प्रकार):</strong>
  - \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^A\) एवं \(\text{I}^A i\) \(\to\) रुधिर वर्ग A
  - \(\text{I}^A\text{I}^B\) \(\to\) रुधिर वर्ग AB
  - \(\text{I}^B i\) \(\to\) रुधिर वर्ग B</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: सत्यापन एवं अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>
संतानों में 4 जीनोटाइप तथा 3 फीनोटाइप (4 genotypes; 3 phenotypes) संभव हैं।</p>]]></text>

    <text><![CDATA[<p>Plants which produce characteristic pneumatophores and show vivipary belong to:</p>
<hr/>
<p>विशिष्ट श्वसन मूल (न्यूमेटोफोर) उत्पन्न करने वाले तथा जरायुजता (विवीपैरी) दर्शाने वाले पादप किससे संबंधित होते हैं?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Halophytic Adaptations</strong><br/>
Halophytes are specialized plants adapted to saline environments such as coastal estuaries, tidal marshes, and mangrove swamps characterized by high salinity and waterlogged, anoxic soils.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Characteristic Morphological and Reproductive Traits</strong><br/>
Mangrove halophytes (e.g., <i>Rhizophora</i>, <i>Avicennia</i>, and <i>Sonneratia</i>) display two key adaptations:<br/>
1. <strong>Pneumatophores (respiratory roots):</strong> Because waterlogged marshy soils are severely deficient in oxygen, underground roots send specialized negatively geotropic, vertical aerial branches out of the mud containing lenticels to facilitate atmospheric gas exchange.<br/>
2. <strong>Vivipary:</strong> Seeds germinate precociously while still attached to the parent plant because high osmotic pressure and hypoxia in saline mud would inhibit seed germination. Once a heavy seedling with a developed hypocotyl forms, it detaches and embeds directly into the soil.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Verification of Other Plant Categories</strong><br/>
- Hydrophytes are adapted to aquatic freshwater habitats.<br/>
- Mesophytes grow in habitats with moderate moisture.<br/>
- Psammophytes grow in sandy soils and dunes.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Plants exhibiting pneumatophores and vivipary are halophytes.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: लवणोद्भिदों (Halophytes) का अनुकूलन सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
लवणोद्भिद अत्यधिक लवणयुक्त दलदली तथा तटीय क्षेत्रों में उगने वाले पादप हैं, जहाँ की मृदा में लवणों की अत्यधिक सांद्रता तथा ऑक्सीजन की भारी कमी होती है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: विशिष्ट अनुकूलन</strong><br/>
मैंग्रोव पादपों (जैसे <i>राइजोफोरा</i>) में दो प्रमुख अनुकूलन पाए जाते हैं:<br/>
1. <strong>श्वसन मूल (Pneumatophores):</strong> दलदल में ऑक्सीजन की कमी के कारण जड़ें गुरुत्वाकर्षण के विपरीत ऊपर वायु में निकल आती हैं। इनमें वातरंध्र (लेंटिसेल) पाए जाते हैं जो गैसीय विनिमय करते हैं।<br/>
2. <strong>जरायुजता (Vivipary):</strong> खारे दलदल में बीजों का सामान्य अंकुरण संभव न होने के कारण बीज का अंकुरण मातृ पौधे पर लगे रहते हुए ही हो जाता है। बाद में अंकुरित भ्रूण सीधे दलदल में गिरकर स्थापित हो जाता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: अन्य पादप समूहों की तुलना</strong><br/>
जलोद्भिद जल में, समोद्भिद सामान्य परिस्थितियों में, तथा बालुकोद्भिद (सैमोफाइट्स) बालू में उगते हैं।</p>

    <text>NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_2017_MCQ_Q167 - Alexander von Humboldt and species area relationship</text>
  </name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Alexander Von Humboldt described for the first time:</p>
<hr/>
<p>एलेक्जेंडर वॉन हम्बोल्ट ने पहली बार किसका वर्णन किया था?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Biogeographical Ecology</strong><br/>
The Prussian naturalist and geographer Alexander von Humboldt pioneered empirical studies relating geographic area to biotic diversity during his extensive explorations of South American wildernesses.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Species-Area Relationship</strong><br/>
Humboldt observed that within a given geographical region, species richness increases with expanding explored area, but only up to a limiting threshold. On a normal linear scale, this relationship forms a rectangular hyperbola, expressed mathematically as:
\[S = C A^Z\]
On a logarithmic scale, it linearizes into:
\[\log S = \log C + Z \log A\]
where \(S\) is species richness, \(A\) is sampled area, \(Z\) is the slope of the regression line, and \(C\) is the \(y\)-intercept.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Verification of Other Principles</strong><br/>
- The law of limiting factors was proposed by F.F. Blackman in 1905.<br/>
- The logistic population growth equation was formulated by Pierre François Verhulst.<br/>
- The modern term 'Biodiversity' was popularized by Edward O. Wilson.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Alexander von Humboldt described the species-area relationships for the first time.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: जैव-भौगोलिक पारिस्थितिकी का सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
प्रसिद्ध जर्मन प्रकृतिविद एवं भूगोलवेत्ता एलेक्जेंडर वॉन हम्बोल्ट ने दक्षिण अमेरिका के वर्षा वनों में खोज के दौरान क्षेत्रफल तथा जातीय समृद्धि के संबंधों का अन्वेषण किया।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: जाति-क्षेत्र संबंध का विवरण</strong><br/>
हम्बोल्ट ने देखा कि किसी क्षेत्र में अन्वेषण किए गए क्षेत्र को बढ़ाने पर जातीय समृद्धि बढ़ती है, लेकिन केवल एक सीमा तक। यह संबंध आयताकार अतिपरवलय (rectangular hyperbola) होता है:
\[\log S = \log C + Z \log A\]
यहाँ \(S\) = जातीय समृद्धि, \(A\) = क्षेत्रफल, \(Z\) = समाश्रयण गुणांक, तथा \(C\) = स्थिरांक है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: अन्य सिद्धांतों की तुलना</strong><br/>
- सीमाकारी कारकों का नियम 1905 में एफ. एफ. ब्लैकमैन ने दिया था।<br/>
- समष्टि वृद्धि समीकरण वर्हुल्स्ट द्वारा विकसित किया गया।<br/>
- जैव विविधता शब्द का प्रचार एडवर्ड विल्सन ने किया था।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
एलेक्जेंडर वॉन हम्बोल्ट ने सर्वप्रथम जाति-क्षेत्र संबंधों (species area relationships) का वर्णन किया था।</p>]]></text>

<p><strong>Step 2: Mechanics of Bacterial Binary Fission</strong><br/>
Bacterial multiplication takes place via binary fission. The single, circular, double-stranded DNA chromosome is attached to a mesosome or cell membrane site. Organ abscission (the detachment of leaves, flowers, and fruits) is mediated by an abscission zone where pectin-degrading enzymes dissolve middle lamellae. This process is regulated antagonistically by plant growth regulators.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Action of Auxins on Abscission</strong><br/>
Young, actively growing leaves and developing fruits synthesize high levels of endogenous <strong>auxins</strong> (such as indole-3-acetic acid, IAA). High auxin concentrations in juvenile tissues render the cells of the abscission zone insensitive to ethylene, thereby preventing premature abscission. When synthetic auxins (such as NAA or 2,4-D at low concentrations) are applied exogenously to orchards, they prevent early fruit drop and leaf fall. <p><strong>Step 3: Verification of Other Regulators</strong><br/>
- Ethylene strongly accelerates abscission zone differentiation and promotes organ drop.<br/>
- Gibberellins induce internodal elongation and break seed dormancy.<br/>

Archaebacteria are primitive prokaryotes distinct from eubacteria, adapted to survive under harsh, hostile environmental conditions.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Physiological Adaptations of Archaebacteria</strong><br/>
Archaebacteria possess a specialized cell wall structure lacking peptidoglycan (composed instead of pseudomurein or complex proteins) and a cell membrane containing branched ether-linked lipids. These molecular adaptations confer stability against extreme denaturation. Specifically, halophilic archaebacteria (<strong>halophiles</strong>, e.g., <i>Halobacterium</i> and <i>Halococcus</i>) flourish in saturated hypersaline environments (such as the Great Salt Lake and the Dead Sea) by accumulating intracellular \(\text{K}^+\) ions to balance external osmotic pressure.</p>

Simple fleshy or fibrous fruits are classified according to the differentiation of their pericarp layers (epicarp, mesocarp, and endocarp).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Botanical Definition of a Drupe</strong><br/>
A <strong>drupe</strong> (stone fruit) is a simple fruit developing from a monocarpellary or multicarpellary syncarpous, superior ovary containing a single seed, where the inner pericarp layer (endocarp) becomes stony and hard.<br/>
In the coconut (<i>Cocos nucifera</i>), known as a fibrous drupe:<br/>
- The epicarp is the outer thin membranous skin.<br/>
- The mesocarp is fibrous (from which commercial coir is obtained).<br/>
- The endocarp is the hard, stony inner shell surrounding the seed.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Verification of Other Fruit Types</strong><br/>
- A berry possesses a fleshy endocarp without a stony shell (e.g., tomato).<br/>
- A capsule is a dry, dehiscent multi-seeded fruit (e.g., <i>Datura</i>).<br/>
- A nut is a dry, indehiscent one-seeded fruit with a woody pericarp derived from a multicarpellary ovary (e.g., oak acorn).</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The coconut fruit is botanically classified as a drupe.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Staining Mechanism of Ethidium Bromide</strong><br/>
The gel is stained with <strong>ethidium bromide (EtBr)</strong>, a planar fluorescent dye that intercalates between stacked nitrogenous base pairs of double-stranded DNA. When exposed to ultraviolet (UV) radiation (\(\approx 300\text{ nm}\)), the bound ethidium bromide absorbs UV light and fluoresces intensely in the visible spectrum, emitting bright orange-colored bands indicating the exact electrophoretic positions of the DNA fragments.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Verification of Other Biological Stains</strong><br/>
- Bromophenol blue is a tracking dye used to monitor electrophoretic front movement, but does not stain DNA bands.<br/>
- Acetocarmine is a nuclear stain used for chromosome squashes in cytology.<br/>
- Aniline blue stains callose and fungal hyphae.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Separated DNA fragments are visualized after staining with ethidium bromide.</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Myelination</strong><br/>
The myelin sheath is a multilayered dielectric lipoprotein membrane wrapped concentrically around neuronal axons to insulate them, preventing current leakage and facilitating saltatory nerve impulse conduction.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Glial Synthesis Across Nervous Divisions</strong><br/>
Myelination is carried out by two distinct classes of specialized neuroglial cells:<br/>
1. <strong>Schwann cells (neurolemmocytes):</strong> Produce the myelin sheath surrounding axons in the <strong>Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)</strong>, wrapping around a single axonal internode.<br/>
2. <strong>Oligodendrocytes:</strong> Produce the myelin sheath surrounding axons in the <strong>Central Nervous System (CNS)</strong> (brain and spinal cord), extending cytoplasmic processes that myelinate multiple adjacent axons simultaneously.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Verification of Other Glial Types</strong><br/>
- Astrocytes maintain the blood-brain barrier and regulate interstitial homeostasis.<br/>
- Osteoclasts are bone-resorbing multinucleated cells derived from monocytes, not neural components.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The myelin sheath is produced by Schwann cells and oligodendrocytes.</p>

  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Mitotic Stages</strong><br/>
Mitosis is equational somatic nuclear division occurring through four continuous, coordinated phases: Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, and Telophase.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Sequential Progression of Events</strong><br/>
1. <strong>Prophase:</strong> Chromatin material initiates progressive spiral coiling and <strong>condensation</strong> into distinct rod-shaped chromosomes. Concurrently, the nucleolus, Golgi complex, and ER disappear, accompanied by <strong>nuclear membrane disassembly</strong>.<br/>
2. <strong>Metaphase:</strong> Spindle fibers attach to the kinetochores of chromosomes, aligning them at the equatorial plane (<strong>arrangement at equator</strong> or metaphase plate).<br/>
3. <strong>Anaphase:</strong> Begins with simultaneous <strong>centromere division</strong> (splitting of cohesin linkages), leading to the poleward separation (<strong>segregation</strong>) of sister chromatids (now individual daughter chromosomes).<br/>
4. <strong>Telophase:</strong> Chromosomes reach opposite spindle poles, decondense back into diffuse chromatin, and a new nuclear envelope reassembles around each daughter genome.<br/>
(Note: Crossing over is strictly restricted to pachytene of meiotic prophase I and does not occur in mitosis).</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The correct sequence is: Condensation → nuclear membrane disassembly → arrangement at equator → centromere division → segregation → telophase.</p>

2. <strong>मध्यावस्था:</strong> गुणसूत्र मध्य रेखा पर व्यवस्थित हो जाते हैं (<strong>मध्य रेखा पर विन्यास / arrangement at equator</strong>)।<br/>

(पारगमन या क्रॉसिंग ओवर केवल अर्धसूत्री विभाजन में होता है, समसूत्री विभाजन में नहीं)।</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Chromosomal Aneuploidy</strong><br/>
Non-disjunction is the failure of paired homologous chromosomes or sister chromatids to segregate properly during meiotic cell division, producing gametes with an extra chromosome (\(n+1\)) or a missing chromosome (\(n-1\)).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of the Listed Genetic Disorders</strong><br/>
- <strong>Down's syndrome:</strong> First described by Langdon Down in 1866, this condition is caused by primary meiotic non-disjunction of an <strong>autosome</strong> (chromosome 21), producing an egg or sperm with an extra autosome. Fertilization results in <strong>trisomy 21</strong> (\(2n+1 = 47\) chromosomes). Thus, it is an autosomal primary non-disjunction disorder.<br/>
- <strong>Klinefelter's syndrome:</strong> An allosomal (sex chromosome) aneuploidy caused by non-disjunction of sex chromosomes, yielding an \(\text{XXY}\) karyotype (\(47,\text{XXY}\)).<br/>
- <strong>Turner's syndrome:</strong> An allosomal monosomy caused by sex chromosome non-disjunction, yielding an \(\text{XO}\) karyotype (\(45,\text{XO}\)).<br/>
- <strong>Sickle cell anemia:</strong> A single-gene mendelian autosomal recessive mutation, not an aneuploidy arising from non-disjunction.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The disease caused by autosomal primary non-disjunction is Down's syndrome.</p>

  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Which one of the following is related to Ex-situ conservation of threatened animals and plants?</p>
<hr/>
<p>निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा संकटग्रस्त जंतुओं और पादपों के बाह्य-स्थाने (Ex-situ) संरक्षण से संबंधित है?</p>]]></text>

  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Biodiversity Conservation</strong><br/>
Conservation strategies are categorized into <i>In-situ</i> (on-site conservation within natural ecosystems) and <i>Ex-situ</i> (off-site conservation outside natural habitats).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Comparison of In-situ versus Ex-situ Strategies</strong><br/>
- <strong><i>In-situ</i> conservation:</strong> Involves preserving endangered species within their native ecosystems. Examples include Biodiversity hotspots (e.g., Western Ghats, Himalayan region), National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Biosphere Reserves, and intact biomes such as the Amazon rainforest.<br/>
- <strong><i>Ex-situ</i> conservation:</strong> Involves removing threatened animals and plants from their vulnerable natural habitats and housing them in carefully controlled artificial enclosures under human supervision. Examples include <strong>Wildlife Safari parks</strong>, Zoological gardens, Botanical gardens, Seed banks, and cryopreservation gene banks.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Wildlife Safari parks represent an ex-situ conservation strategy.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: जैव विविधता संरक्षण का सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
संकटग्रस्त जातियों के संरक्षण हेतु दो मुख्य रणनीतियां अपनाई जाती हैं: स्व-स्थाने (In-situ) संरक्षण तथा बाह्य-स्थाने (Ex-situ) संरक्षण।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: संरक्षण विधियों का वर्गीकरण</strong><br/>
- <strong>स्व-स्थाने (In-situ) संरक्षण:</strong> जीवों को उनके प्राकृतिक आवास में ही संरक्षण दिया जाता है। उदाहरण: जैव विविधता के तप्त स्थल (हॉटस्पॉट्स, जैसे हिमालयी क्षेत्र), राष्ट्रीय उद्यान, वन्यजीव अभयारण्य तथा अमेज़न वर्षा वन।<br/>
- <strong>बाह्य-स्थाने (Ex-situ) संरक्षण:</strong> जब संकटग्रस्त जीवों को उनके प्राकृतिक आवास से निकालकर मानव द्वारा निर्मित विशेष सुरक्षित वातावरण में रखा जाता है। उदाहरण: <strong>वन्यजीव सफारी पार्क (Wildlife Safari parks)</strong>, चिड़ियाघर, वानस्पतिक उद्यान तथा बीज बैंक।</p>
