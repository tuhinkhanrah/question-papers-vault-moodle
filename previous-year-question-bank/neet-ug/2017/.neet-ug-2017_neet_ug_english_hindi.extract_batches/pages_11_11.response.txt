    <text><![CDATA[<p>What is the criterion for DNA fragments movement on agarose gel during gel electrophoresis?</p>
<hr/>
<p>जेल वैद्युतकणसंचलन (जेल इलेक्ट्रोफोरेसिस) के दौरान एग्रोस जेल पर डीएनए खंडों की गति का क्या मापदंड है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Gel Electrophoresis</strong><br/>
Agarose gel electrophoresis separates charged biomacromolecules under an applied electric field through a porous molecular matrix.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Mechanism of Separation by Size</strong><br/>
Because the phosphodiester backbone imparts a uniform negative charge-to-mass ratio to DNA, all fragments migrate toward the positive anode. The cross-linked polymer network of the agarose gel provides a physical molecular sieving matrix. Smaller DNA fragments encounter less frictional resistance within the gel pores, migrating faster and traversing a greater distance toward the anode than larger, bulky fragments.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The criterion governing fragment mobility is that the smaller the fragment size, the farther it moves.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: जेल इलेक्ट्रोफोरेसिस का सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
एग्रोस जेल वैद्युतकणसंचलन में विद्युत क्षेत्र के प्रभाव में आवेशित अणुओं को उनके आकार के आधार पर जेल के छिद्रों के माध्यम से पृथक किया जाता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: आकार के आधार पर गति की क्रियाविधि</strong><br/>

  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of the Krebs Cycle</strong><br/>
The citric acid cycle (Krebs cycle or TCA cycle) is a closed cyclic pathway occurring in the mitochondrial matrix that oxidizes acetyl units derived from carbohydrates, fats, and proteins.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Key Steps in the Cycle</strong><br/>
- <strong>Initiating condensation:</strong> The cycle begins when the two-carbon acetyl group of acetyl-CoA condenses with a four-carbon dicarboxylic acid, <strong>oxaloacetic acid (OAA)</strong>, in the presence of water and citrate synthase to form a six-carbon molecule, citric acid. It does <i>not</i> condense with pyruvic acid (pyruvate has already undergone oxidative decarboxylation to produce acetyl-CoA prior to cycle entry).<br/>
- <strong>Reduction of \(\text{NAD}^+\):</strong> Occurs at three distinct enzymatic steps: isocitrate to \(\alpha\)-ketoglutarate, \(\alpha\)-ketoglutarate to succinyl-CoA, and malate to oxaloacetate.<br/>
- <strong>Reduction of \(\text{FAD}^+\):</strong> Occurs at one step: succinate to fumarate catalyzed by succinate dehydrogenase.<br/>
- <strong>Substrate-level phosphorylation:</strong> Cleavage of the high-energy thioester bond during the conversion of succinyl-CoA to succinate is coupled with the synthesis of GTP (or ATP).</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The statement asserting that the cycle starts with the condensation of acetyl group (acetyl CoA) with pyruvic acid to yield citric acid is incorrect.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: क्रेब्स चक्र का संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
क्रेब्स चक्र (सिट्रिक अम्ल चक्र अथवा TCA चक्र) माइटोकॉन्ड्रिया के मैट्रिक्स में संपन्न होने वाला एक चक्रीय प्रक्रम है जो एसिटाइल-CoA का पूर्ण ऑक्सीकरण करता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: चक्र के चरणों का विश्लेषण</strong><br/>
- <strong>आरंभिक संघनन:</strong> चक्र का आरंभ 2-कार्बन वाले एसिटाइल-CoA के 4-कार्बन वाले <strong>ऑक्सेलोएसिटिक अम्ल (OAA)</strong> के साथ संघनन द्वारा होता है, जिससे 6-कार्बन वाला सिट्रिक अम्ल बनता है। यह पाइरुविक अम्ल के साथ संघनित नहीं होता (पाइरुवेट पहले ही एसिटाइल-CoA में परिवर्तित हो चुका होता है)।<br/>
- <strong>\(\text{NAD}^+\) का अपचयन:</strong> चक्र में तीन स्थानों पर \(\text{NAD}^+\) अपचयित होकर \(\text{NADH} + \text{H}^+\) बनाता है (आइसोसिट्रेट से \(\alpha\)-कीटोग्लूटेरेट, \(\alpha\)-कीटोग्लूटेरेट से सक्सिनाइल-CoA, तथा मैलेट से ऑक्सेलोएसिटेट)।<br/>
- <strong>\(\text{FAD}^+\) का अपचयन:</strong> एक स्थान पर सक्सिनेट से फ्यूमरेट बनते समय \(\text{FADH}_2\) बनता है।<br/>
- <strong>क्रियाधार-स्तरीय फॉस्फोरिलीकरण:</strong> सक्सिनाइल-CoA से सक्सिनिक अम्ल बनने के दौरान GTP का एक अणु संश्लेषित होता है।</p>

  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Artificial selection to obtain cows yielding higher milk output represents:</p>
<hr/>
<p>अधिक दुग्ध उत्पादन वाली गायों को प्राप्त करने के लिए किया गया कृत्रिम चयन क्या प्रदर्शित करता है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Selection Types</strong><br/>
Selection operating on a quantitative polygenic phenotypic distribution can produce three main trajectories: stabilizing selection, directional selection, and disruptive selection.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Dynamics of the Three Trajectories</strong><br/>
- <strong>Stabilizing selection:</strong> Favors intermediate mean phenotypes, eliminating individuals with extreme values on both tails, narrowing the phenotypic variance without changing the mean.<br/>
- <strong>Disruptive selection:</strong> Favors both phenotypic extremes simultaneously while selecting against intermediate phenotypes, producing a bimodal distribution with two distinct peaks.<br/>
- <strong>Directional selection:</strong> Selectively favors individuals at one extreme tail of the phenotypic distribution (e.g., maximum milk volume). Over generations, breeding only individuals possessing the highest expression pushes the population mean progressively in that single direction, causing a directional shift in the curve peak.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Artificial selection for higher milk output represents directional selection as it pushes the mean of the character in one direction.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: वरण (चयन) के प्रकारों का सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
किसी समष्टि में किसी मात्रात्मक लक्षण पर कार्य करने वाले चयन को तीन श्रेणियों में विभाजित किया जाता है: स्थायीकारी वरण, दिशात्मक वरण तथा विदारक वरण।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: चयन के विभिन्न रूपों का विश्लेषण</strong><br/>
- <strong>स्थायीकारी वरण (Stabilizing selection):</strong> यह औसत लक्षण प्ररूप का वरण करता है तथा दोनों छोरों के चरम लक्षणों को हटाता है।<br/>
- <strong>विदारक वरण (Disruptive selection):</strong> यह दोनों चरम छोरों के लक्षणों का वरण करता है और औसत को हटाकर दो शिखर बनाता है।<br/>
- <strong>दिशात्मक वरण (Directional selection):</strong> जब कृत्रिम या प्राकृतिक रूप से किसी एक चरम लक्षण (जैसे सर्वाधिक दुग्ध उत्पादन) वाले जीवों का ही लगातार चयन किया जाता है, तो पूरी समष्टि का औसत मान उसी एक दिशा की ओर विस्थापित हो जाता है।</p>

  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>The region of Biosphere Reserve which is legally protected and where no human activity is allowed is known as:</p>
<hr/>
<p>बायोस्फीयर रिजर्व (जैवमंडल आरक्षित क्षेत्र) का वह क्षेत्र जो कानूनी रूप से संरक्षित है और जहाँ किसी भी मानवीय गतिविधि की अनुमति नहीं होती, क्या कहलाता है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Biosphere Reserve Zonation</strong><br/>
Biosphere reserves established under UNESCO's Man and the Biosphere (MAB) programme are demarcated into three concentric spatial management zones.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Zonal Distinctions</strong><br/>
- <strong>Transition zone (manipulation zone):</strong> Outermost perimeter where sustainable human settlements, cropping, forestry, and recreation occur in harmony with conservation objectives.<br/>
- <strong>Buffer zone:</strong> Surrounds the core zone; strictly regulated human activities compatible with conservation (e.g., non-destructive ecological research, environmental monitoring, and education) are permitted.<br/>
- <strong>Core zone:</strong> The pristine innermost sanctuary devoted entirely to the preservation of biodiversity and ecological processes. It is strictly protected by statutory law; no destructive exploitation or human interference of any kind (forestry, tourism, grazing, or habitation) is legally permitted.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The legally protected zone devoid of all human activity is the core zone.</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Water Potential</strong><br/>
Water potential (\(\Psi_w\)) quantifies the free energy of water molecules per unit volume relative to pure water under standard conditions.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Thermodynamic Convention</strong><br/>
In pure liquid water at atmospheric pressure and standard temperature (\(298\text{ K}\)), water molecules possess maximum kinetic free energy. By scientific convention, the water potential of pure water under standard temperature and pressure is defined as exactly <strong>zero</strong> (\(\Psi_w = 0\)).<br/>
Dissolving any solute decreases the free energy of the water molecules, lowering the solute potential (\(\Psi_s\)) below zero, making the overall water potential of any solution negative.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The water potential of pure water is zero.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Physiological Mechanism of Capacitation</strong><br/>
Capacitation is the physiological conditioning process that takes place inside the <strong>female reproductive tract</strong> (uterus and fallopian tubes). <p><strong>Step 3: Verification of Male Duct System</strong><br/>
In the male reproductive system (rete testis, epididymis, and vas deferens), spermatozoa undergo morphological maturation, acquire motility, and are stored in an inhibited, decapacitated state.</p>

A cloning vector contains a <strong>selectable marker</strong>—typically a gene conferring resistance to an antibiotic such as ampicillin, tetracycline, kanamycin, or chloramphenicol, or an enzyme producing a chromogenic reaction (e.g., \(\beta\)-galactosidase).     <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Atmospheric Aerosols</strong><br/>
Aerosols are colloidal suspensions of minute liquid droplets or fine solid particulate matter dispersed in the atmosphere, originating from industrial emissions, automobile exhaust, volcanic eruptions, and biomass combustion.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Ecological and Physiological Impacts</strong><br/>
- <strong>Health:</strong> Aerosols penetrate deep into the alveoli, exacerbating respiratory diseases such as bronchitis, asthma, and chronic pulmonary damage.<br/>
- <strong>Climate:</strong> Aerosols scatter and absorb solar radiation and act as cloud condensation nuclei, altering cloud albedo, rainfall, and regional monsoon patterns.<br/>
- <strong>Agriculture:</strong> Aerosol accumulation forms atmospheric brown clouds that reduce surface solar irradiance (solar dimming) reaching crop canopies and deposit toxic acidic heavy metal residues onto agricultural land, substantially decreasing photosynthetic rates and crop productivity.</p>

The claim that aerosols cause increased agricultural productivity is invalid.</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of the Counter-Current Multiplier</strong><br/>
The hairpin loop of Henle in juxtamedullary nephrons establishes an osmotic concentration gradient across the renal medulla through differential tubular permeabilities.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Functional Differences of Henle's Loop Limbs</strong><br/>
- <strong>Descending limb:</strong> The thin descending limb is permeable to water (due to high aquaporin density) but almost entirely impermeable to electrolytes (\(\text{NaCl}\) and urea). As tubular fluid travels down toward the hyperosmotic papilla, water leaves osmotically, concentrating the filtrate.<br/>
- <strong>Ascending limb:</strong> The ascending limb (both thin and thick segments) is strictly <strong>impermeable to water</strong> while permitting active and passive transport of electrolytes (\(\text{Na}^+\), \(\text{K}^+\), and \(\text{Cl}^-\)) into the medullary interstitium. As tubular fluid ascends toward the cortex, solutes are removed without water reabsorption, progressively diluting the filtrate.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The correct statement is that the ascending limb of the loop of Henle is impermeable to water.</p>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of Wastewater Treatment</strong><br/>
Municipal sewage treatment proceeds through progressive physical, biological, and chemical purifying stages: primary, secondary, and tertiary treatments.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Processing Stages</strong><br/>
- <strong>Primary treatment:</strong> Consists entirely of physical operations designed to remove coarse particulate matter and suspended solids. Floating litter and debris are first removed through sequential mesh filtration, followed by grit chambers and primary settling tanks where suspended soil, pebble, and organic solid particles settle gravimetrically to form primary sludge.<br/>
- <strong>Secondary treatment:</strong> Biological digestion of dissolved organic matter using aerobic microbial flocs in aeration basins to lower biological oxygen demand (BOD).<br/>
- <strong>Tertiary treatment:</strong> Physicochemical disinfection (e.g., chlorination, ozonation, UV) targeting pathogenic microorganisms and dissolved phosphates.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Suspended solids in sewage are removed during primary treatment.</p>

<p>जनन के लिए आवश्यक हाइपोथैलेमस का हार्मोन GnRH किस पर कार्य करता है?</p>]]></text>

  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle of the Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Gonadal Axis</strong><br/>
Reproductive endocrine physiology is orchestrated by neurohormones synthesized by peptidergic neurons of the hypothalamus and transported via the hypophyseal portal circulation to the adenohypophysis.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Target Organ and Hormonal Actions of GnRH</strong><br/>
Gonadotropin-Releasing Hormone (GnRH) binds specifically to G-protein-coupled receptors on gonadotroph cells situated exclusively in the <strong>anterior pituitary gland</strong> (adenohypophysis). Upon stimulation, gonadotrophs synthesize and secrete two gonadotropic hormones into the systemic circulation:<br/>
1. <strong>Luteinizing Hormone (LH):</strong> Stimulates Leydig cells to produce testosterone in males and induces ovulation and corpus luteum formation in females.<br/>
2. <strong>Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH):</strong> Stimulates spermatogenesis in males and follicular development in females.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Verification of Posterior Pituitary Secretions</strong><br/>
The posterior pituitary (neurohypophysis) does not synthesize hormones; it stores and releases oxytocin and vasopressin produced by hypothalamic supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
GnRH acts on the anterior pituitary gland and stimulates the secretion of LH and FSH.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: हाइपोथैलेमस-पीयूष-जननग्रंथि अक्ष का सिद्धांत</strong><br/>

<p><strong>चरण 2: GnRH का लक्ष्य और कार्यप्रणाली</strong><br/>
