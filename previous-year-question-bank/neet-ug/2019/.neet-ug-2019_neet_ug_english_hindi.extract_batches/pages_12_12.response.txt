    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Structural Principles of Ecological Pyramids</strong><br/>- <strong>Pyramid of Energy:</strong> Always strictly upright because energy is progressively lost as metabolic heat at each trophic transfer according to the second law of thermodynamics.<br/>- <strong>Pyramid of Numbers in Grassland:</strong> Upright, as numerous grass plants support fewer herbivores, which support still fewer carnivores.<br/>- <strong>Pyramid of Biomass in a Forest:</strong> Upright, as huge standing woody trees support smaller herbivore and carnivore biomass.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Marine Biomass Pyramid</strong><br/>In an open marine/aquatic ecosystem, the primary producers are microscopic phytoplanktons.
