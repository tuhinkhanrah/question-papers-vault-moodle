  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q20 - Wrong statement for two dimensional circular motion</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>The position vector of a particle \(\vec{R}\) as a function of time is given by</p>\[\vec{R} = 4 \sin (2\pi t)\,\hat{i} + 4 \cos (2\pi t)\,\hat{j}\]<p>where \(\vec{R}\) is in meter, \(t\) is in seconds and \(\hat{i}\) and \(\hat{j}\) denote unit vectors along \(x\) and \(y\)-directions, respectively. Which one of the following statements is wrong for the motion of particle?</p><hr/><p>समय के फलन के रूप में किसी कण का स्थिति सदिश \(\vec{R}\) निम्न समीकरण द्वारा दिया गया है:</p>\[\vec{R} = 4 \sin (2\pi t)\,\hat{i} + 4 \cos (2\pi t)\,\hat{j}\]<p>जहाँ \(\vec{R}\) मीटर में, \(t\) सेकंड में है तथा \(\hat{i}\) और \(\hat{j}\) क्रमशः \(x\) और \(y\)-दिशाओं के अनुदिश एकांक सदिश हैं। कण की गति के संबंध में निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा कथन गलत है?</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>- Position vector: \(\vec{R}(t) = 4 \sin(2\pi t)\,\hat{i} + 4 \cos(2\pi t)\,\hat{j}\)<br/>- Units: \(\vec{R}\) is in meters and \(t\) is in seconds.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>- Cartesian coordinate system with orthogonal unit vectors \(\hat{i}\) and \(\hat{j}\).<br/>- Velocity vector is \(\vec{v} = \frac{d\vec{R}}{dt}\) and acceleration vector is \(\vec{a} = \frac{d\vec{v}}{dt}\).</p><p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>- Trajectory equation: squaring and adding the \(x\) and \(y\) components gives the equation of the path.<br/>- Magnitude of velocity: \(v = |\vec{v}| = \sqrt{v_x^2 + v_y^2}\).<br/>- Acceleration: \(\vec{a} = \frac{d^2\vec{R}}{dt^2}\).</p><p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>- Path of the particle: \(x = 4 \sin(2\pi t)\) and \(y = 4 \cos(2\pi t)\).<br/>  \[ x^2 + y^2 = 16 \sin^2(2\pi t) + 16 \cos^2(2\pi t) = 16 = 4^2 \]<br/>  This is a circle of radius \(4\text{ m}\).<br/>- Velocity vector:<br/>  \[ \vec{v} = \frac{d\vec{R}}{dt} = 8\pi \cos(2\pi t)\,\hat{i} - 8\pi \sin(2\pi t)\,\hat{j} \]<br/>- Magnitude of velocity:<br/>  \[ v = \sqrt{(8\pi \cos(2\pi t))^2 + (-8\pi \sin(2\pi t))^2} = 8\pi\text{ m/s} \approx 25.13\text{ m/s} \]<br/>  Notice that the magnitude of the velocity is \(8\pi\text{ m/s}\), not \(8\text{ m/s}\).<br/>- Acceleration vector:<br/>  \[ \vec{a} = \frac{d\vec{v}}{dt} = -16\pi^2 \sin(2\pi t)\,\hat{i} - 16\pi^2 \cos(2\pi t)\,\hat{j} = -(2\pi)^2 \vec{R} \]<br/>  Thus, the acceleration vector is directed radially inward along \(-\vec{R}\).<br/>- Magnitude of acceleration:<br/>  \[ a = 16\pi^2 = \frac{(8\pi)^2}{4} = \frac{v^2}{R} \]</p><p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>- The motion is uniform circular motion with radius \(4\text{ m}\), constant speed \(v = 8\pi\text{ m/s}\), and centripetal acceleration \(a = \frac{v^2}{R}\) directed along \(-\vec{R}\).<br/>- The incorrect statement is that the magnitude of the velocity of the particle is \(8\text{ m/s}\).</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>- स्थिति सदिश: \(\vec{R}(t) = 4 \sin(2\pi t)\,\hat{i} + 4 \cos(2\pi t)\,\hat{j}\)</p><p><strong>चरण 2: अवधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>- वेग सदिश \(\vec{v} = \frac{d\vec{R}}{dt}\) तथा त्वरण सदिश \(\vec{a} = \frac{d\vec{v}}{dt}\) होता है।</p><p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>- कण का पथ: घटकों के वर्गों का योग करने पर वृत्ताकार पथ का समीकरण प्राप्त होता है।<br/>- चाल: \(v = \sqrt{v_x^2 + v_y^2}\)।</p><p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>- \(x^2 + y^2 = 16\), अतः कण का पथ \(4\text{ m}\) त्रिज्या का एक वृत्त है।<br/>- वेग सदिश:<br/>  \[ \vec{v} = 8\pi \cos(2\pi t)\,\hat{i} - 8\pi \sin(2\pi t)\,\hat{j} \]<br/>- वेग का परिमाण \(v = 8\pi\text{ m/s}\) है, जो \(8\text{ m/s}\) के बराबर नहीं है।<br/>- त्वरण सदिश \(\vec{a} = -4\pi^2 \vec{R}\) है, जो कि \(-\vec{R}\) के अनुदिश दिष्ट है।<br/>- त्वरण का परिमाण \(a = \frac{v^2}{R}\) है।</p><p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>- कण के वेग का परिमाण \(8\pi\text{ m/s}\) है, अतः यह कथन गलत है कि वेग का परिमाण \(8\text{ m/s}\) है।</p>]]></text>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q21 - Current and capacitor voltage in series RC circuit</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>A series R-C circuit is connected to an alternating voltage source. Consider two situations</p><ol style="list-style-type: lower-roman;"><li>When capacitor is air filled.</li><li>When capacitor is mica filled.</li></ol><p>Current through resistor is \(i\) and voltage across capacitor is \(V\) then</p><hr/><p>एक श्रेणी R-C परिपथ एक प्रत्यावर्ती वोल्टता स्रोत से जुड़ा है। दो स्थितियों पर विचार कीजिए:</p><ol style="list-style-type: lower-roman;"><li>जब संधारित्र वायु से भरा हो।</li><li>जब संधारित्र अभ्रक (mica) से भरा हो।</li></ol><p>प्रतिरोधक से प्रवाहित धारा \(i\) तथा संधारित्र के सिरों पर वोल्टता \(V\) है, तब:</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>- Situation (a): Air-filled capacitor of capacitance \(C_a\).<br/>- Situation (b): Mica-filled capacitor of capacitance \(C_b\).<br/>- Resistance \(R\) and alternating source voltage \(V_{\text{source}}\) are identical in both cases.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>- Mica has a dielectric constant \(K > 1\), so \(C_b = K C_a > C_a\).<br/>- The alternating source frequency \(\omega\) is constant.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>- Capacitive reactance: \(X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C}\)<br/>- Circuit impedance: \(Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_C^2}\)<br/>- Circuit current: \(i = \frac{V_{\text{source}}}{Z}\)<br/>- Voltage across the capacitor:<br/>\[ V_C = i X_C = \frac{V_{\text{source}} X_C}{\sqrt{R^2 + X_C^2}} = \frac{V_{\text{source}}}{\sqrt{1 + \frac{R^2}{X_C^2}}} = \frac{V_{\text{source}}}{\sqrt{1 + \omega^2 R^2 C^2}} \]</p><p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>- Since mica increases capacitance (\(C_b > C_a\)), the capacitive reactance decreases (\(X_{C,b} < X_{C,a}\)).<br/>- Looking at the expression \(V_C = \frac{V_{\text{source}}}{\sqrt{1 + \omega^2 R^2 C^2}}\): as capacitance \(C\) increases, the term \(\omega^2 R^2 C^2\) increases, which causes the denominator to increase.<br/>- Therefore, the voltage across the capacitor decreases as capacitance increases: \(V_a > V_b\).<br/>- Additionally, because impedance \(Z_b < Z_a\), the current in the second case is larger (\(i_b > i_a\)).</p><p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>- In the limiting case as \(C \to \infty\), \(X_C \to 0\), so the capacitor acts as a short circuit and \(V_C \to 0\). Thus, introducing a dielectric decreases the capacitor voltage.<br/>- The correct relationship is \(V_a > V_b\).</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>- स्थिति (a): वायु संधारित्र की धारिता \(C_a\)<br/>- स्थिति (b): अभ्रक संधारित्र की धारिता \(C_b\)<br/>- अभ्रक का परावैद्युतांक \(K > 1\) होने के कारण \(C_b > C_a\)</p><p><strong>चरण 2: अवधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>- प्रत्यावर्ती स्रोत की वोल्टता तथा आवृत्ति समान हैं।</p><p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>- संधारित्र के सिरों पर वोल्टता:<br/>\[ V_C = \frac{V_{\text{source}}}{\sqrt{1 + \omega^2 R^2 C^2}} \]</p><p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>- चूँकि \(C_b > C_a\) है, अतः हर में स्थित पद \(\sqrt{1 + \omega^2 R^2 C^2}\) का मान स्थिति (b) में बढ़ जाता है।<br/>- अतः संधारित्र के सिरों पर वोल्टता घट जाती है: \(V_a > V_b\)।</p><p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>- संधारित्र के सिरों पर वोल्टताओं का सही संबंध \(V_a > V_b\) है।</p>]]></text>

  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>A string is stretched between fixed points separated by \(75.0\text{ cm}\). It is observed to have resonant frequencies of \(420\text{ Hz}\) and \(315\text{ Hz}\). There are no other resonant frequencies between these two. The lowest resonant frequency for this string is</p><hr/><p>एक डोरी को \(75.0\text{ cm}\) की दूरी पर स्थित दो स्थिर बिंदुओं के बीच खींचा गया है। इसकी अनुनादी आवृत्तियाँ \(420\text{ Hz}\) तथा \(315\text{ Hz}\) प्रेक्षित की जाती हैं। इन दोनों के बीच कोई अन्य अनुनादी आवृत्ति नहीं है। इस डोरी के लिए न्यूनतम अनुनादी आवृत्ति है:</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>- Length between fixed points: \(L = 75.0\text{ cm}\)<br/>- Two consecutive resonant frequencies: \(f_n = 315\text{ Hz}\) and \(f_{n+1} = 420\text{ Hz}\)<br/>- No other resonant frequency exists between these two.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>- The string is fixed at both ends, forming nodes at the boundaries.<br/>- Resonant frequencies are integral multiples of the fundamental (lowest) resonant frequency \(f_1\).</p><p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>- For a string clamped at both ends:<br/>\[ f_n = n f_1 \]<br/>\[ f_{n+1} = (n+1) f_1 \]<br/>- The difference between consecutive resonant frequencies gives the fundamental frequency:<br/>\[ \Delta f = f_{n+1} - f_n = f_1 \]</p><p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>- Calculating the frequency difference:<br/>\[ f_1 = 420\text{ Hz} - 315\text{ Hz} = 105\text{ Hz} \]<br/>- Checking the harmonic numbers:<br/>  - For \(315\text{ Hz}\): \(n = \frac{315}{105} = 3\) (third harmonic)<br/>  - For \(420\text{ Hz}\): \(n+1 = \frac{420}{105} = 4\) (fourth harmonic)<br/>  Both are consecutive integers, confirming that \(f_1 = 105\text{ Hz}\).</p><p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>- The lowest resonant frequency (fundamental frequency) is \(105\text{ Hz}\).</p><hr/><p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए आँकड़े और स्थितियाँ</strong><br/>- स्थिर बिंदुओं के बीच दूरी: \(L = 75.0\text{ cm}\)<br/>- दो क्रमागत अनुनादी आवृत्तियाँ: \(315\text{ Hz}\) और \(420\text{ Hz}\)</p><p><strong>चरण 2: अवधारणाएँ और चिह्न परिपाटी</strong><br/>- दोनों सिरों पर बंधी डोरी के लिए अनुनादी आवृत्तियाँ मूल आवृत्ति की पूर्ण गुणज होती हैं।</p><p><strong>चरण 3: संबंधित सूत्र और सिद्धांत</strong><br/>- \(f_n = n f_1\) तथा \(f_{n+1} = (n+1) f_1\)<br/>- दो क्रमागत आवृत्तियों का अंतर:<br/>\[ \Delta f = f_{n+1} - f_n = f_1 \]</p><p><strong>चरण 4: चरणबद्ध हल</strong><br/>- मूल (न्यूनतम) अनुनादी आवृत्ति:<br/>\[ f_1 = 420 - 315 = 105\text{ Hz} \]<br/>- सन्नादी संख्याएँ क्रमशः \(n = 3\) तथा \(n = 4\) प्राप्त होती हैं।</p><p><strong>चरण 5: जाँच और अंतिम उत्तर</strong><br/>- डोरी के लिए न्यूनतम अनुनादी आवृत्ति \(105\text{ Hz}\) है।</p>]]></text>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2015_MCQ_Q23 - Surroundings temperature from refrigerator COP</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>The coefficient of performance of a refrigerator is 5. If the temperature inside freezer is \(-20^\circ\text{C}\), the temperature of the surroundings to which it rejects heat is</p><hr/><p>एक प्रशीतित्र (रेफ्रिजरेटर) का निष्पादन गुणांक (coefficient of performance) 5 है। यदि फ्रीजर के अंदर का तापमान \(-20^\circ\text{C}\) है, तो उस परिवेश का तापमान, जिसमें यह ऊष्मा निष्कासित करता है, होगा:</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>- Coefficient of performance: \(\beta = 5\)<br/>- Temperature of cold reservoir (freezer): \(T_2 = -20^\circ\text{C} = -20 + 273 = 253\text{ K}\)</p><p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>- The refrigerator operates on an ideal Carnot refrigeration cycle.<br/>- Temperatures must be evaluated in Kelvin.</p><p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>- Coefficient of performance of a Carnot refrigerator:<br/>\[ \beta = \frac{T_2}{T_1 - T_2} \]<br/>where \(T_1\) is the temperature of the hot reservoir (surroundings).</p><p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>- Substituting \(\beta = 5\) and \(T_2 = 253\text{ K}\):<br/>\[ 5 = \frac{253}{T_1 - 253} \]<br/>\[ 5(T_1 - 253) = 253 \]<br/>\[ 5 T_1 - 1265 = 253 \]<br/>\[ 5 T_1 = 1518 \implies T_1 = 303.6\text{ K} \]<br/>- Converting to Celsius:<br/>\[ t_1 = 303.6 - 273 = 30.6^\circ\text{C} \approx 31^\circ\text{C} \]</p><p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>- Checking the coefficient of performance: \(\frac{253}{304 -   <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>- Length of rectangular coil: \(l = 0.12\text{ m}\)<br/>- Width of rectangular coil: \(b = 0.1\text{ m}\)<br/>- Area of coil: \(A = l \times b = 0.12 \times 0.1 = 0.012\text{ m}^2\)<br/>- Number of turns: \(N = 50\)<br/>- Current: \(I = 2\text{ A}\)<br/>- Magnetic field: \(B = 0.2\text{ Wb/m}^2\)<br/>- Angle between the plane of the coil and the magnetic field: \(\alpha = 30^\circ\)</p><p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>- The normal to the plane of the coil (area vector) makes an angle \(\theta = 90^\circ -   <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>An electron move on a straight line path XY as shown. The abcd is a coil adjacent in the path of electron.   <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>An electron moving along the path from X to Y constitutes an electric current in the opposite direction (from Y to X).
