<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2022_SECTION_A_MCQ_Q34 - Physical quantity with dimensions of pressure</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>The physical quantity that has the same dimensional formula as pressure is:</p>
<hr/>
<p>वह भौतिक राशि जिसका विमीय सूत्र दाब के समान है, वह है:</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Dimensional Formula of Pressure</strong><br/>
Pressure is defined as force per unit area:
\[
P = \frac{\text{Force}}{\text{Area}}
\]
The dimensional formula for force is \([\text{M L T}^{-2}]\) and for area is \([\text{L}^2]\). Therefore:
\[
[P] = \frac{[\text{M L T}^{-2}]}{[\text{L}^2]} = [\text{M L}^{-1}\text{T}^{-2}]
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Physical Quantities</strong><br/>
- Force: \([F] = [\text{M L T}^{-2}]\)<br/>
- Linear momentum: \([p] = [\text{mass} \times \text{velocity}] = [\text{M}][\text{L T}^{-1}] = [\text{M L T}^{-1}]\)<br/>
- Young's modulus of elasticity: defined as the ratio of longitudinal stress to longitudinal strain. Since strain is dimensionless (ratio of change in length to original length):
\[
[Y] = \frac{[\text{Stress}]}{[\text{Strain}]} = \frac{[\text{Force} / \text{Area}]}{1} = [\text{M L}^{-1}\text{T}^{-2}]
\]
- Coefficient of viscosity: from Stokes' law or Newton's law of viscosity (\(F = \eta A \frac{dv}{dx}\)):
\[
[\eta] = \frac{[F]}{[A]\left[\frac{dv}{dx}\right]} = \frac{[\text{M L T}^{-2}]}{[\text{L}^2][\text{T}^{-1}]} = [\text{M L}^{-1}\text{T}^{-1}]
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conceptual Conclusion</strong><br/>
Young's modulus of elasticity has the dimensional formula \([\text{M L}^{-1}\text{T}^{-2}]\), which is identical to that of pressure. Thus, Young's modulus of elasticity is the correct physical quantity.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दाब का विमीय सूत्र</strong><br/>
दाब को प्रति एकांक क्षेत्रफल पर लगने वाले बल के रूप में परिभाषित किया जाता है:
\[
P = \frac{\text{बल}}{\text{क्षेत्रफल}}
\]
बल का विमीय सूत्र \([\text{M L T}^{-2}]\) और क्षेत्रफल का विमीय सूत्र \([\text{L}^2]\) होता है। अतः:
\[
[P] = \frac{[\text{M L T}^{-2}]}{[\text{L}^2]} = [\text{M L}^{-1}\text{T}^{-2}]
\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: अन्य भौतिक राशियों का मूल्यांकन</strong><br/>
- बल: \([F] = [\text{M L T}^{-2}]\)<br/>
- संवेग: \([p] = [\text{द्रव्यमान} \times \text{वेग}] = [\text{M L T}^{-1}]\)<br/>
- यंग प्रत्यास्थता गुणांक: अनुदैर्ध्य प्रतिबल और अनुदैर्ध्य विकृति का अनुपात होता है। चूँकि विकृति विमाहीन है:
\[
[Y] = \frac{[\text{प्रतिबल}]}{[\text{विकृति}]} = \frac{[\text{बल} / \text{क्षेत्रफल}]}{1} = [\text{M L}^{-1}\text{T}^{-2}]
\]
- श्यानता गुणांक: \([\eta] = [\text{M L}^{-1}\text{T}^{-1}]\)</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
यंग प्रत्यास्थता गुणांक का विमीय सूत्र \([\text{M L}^{-1}\text{T}^{-2}]\) दाब के विमीय सूत्र के समान है। अतः सही भौतिक राशि यंग प्रत्यास्थता गुणांक है।</p>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2022_SECTION_A_MCQ_Q35 - Moment of inertia of accelerated flywheel</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>An energy of \(484\text{ J}\) is spent in increasing the speed of a flywheel from \(60\text{ rpm}\) to \(360\text{ rpm}\). The moment of inertia of the flywheel is:</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक गतिपालक चक्र (फ्लाईव्हील) की चाल \(60\text{ rpm}\) से \(360\text{ rpm}\) तक बढ़ाने में \(484\text{ J}\) ऊर्जा व्यय होती है। गतिपालक चक्र का जड़त्व आघूर्ण है:</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
- Energy supplied / Work done (\(W = \Delta K\)): \(484\text{ J}\)<br/>
- Initial rotational frequency (\(n_1\)): \(60\text{ rpm} = \frac{60}{60}\text{ rps} = 1\text{ rev/s}\)<br/>
- Final rotational frequency (\(n_2\)): \(360\text{ rpm} = \frac{360}{60}\text{ rps} = 6\text{ rev/s}\)</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
- Initial angular velocity: \(\omega_1 = 2\pi n_1 = 2\pi \times 1 = 2\pi\text{ rad/s}\)<br/>
- Final angular velocity: \(\omega_2 = 2\pi n_2 = 2\pi \times 6 = 12\pi\text{ rad/s}\)<br/>
- Work-energy theorem for rotation:
\[
W = \Delta K = \frac{1}{2} I \left(\omega_2^2 - \omega_1^2\right)
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Substitute the values of \(\omega_1\) and \(\omega_2\) into the equation:
\[
484 = \frac{1}{2} I \left[(12\pi)^2 - (2\pi)^2\right]
\]
\[
484 = \frac{1}{2} I \left[144\pi^2 - 4\pi^2\right] = \frac{1}{2} I \left[140\pi^2\right] = 70\pi^2 I
\]
Using \(\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}\), we find:
\[
\pi^2 \approx \left(\frac{22}{7}\right)^2 = \frac{484}{49}
\]
Substitute \(\pi^2 = \frac{484}{49}\) into the expression:
\[
484 = 70 \times \frac{484}{49} \times I
\]
\[
484 = \frac{10 \times 484}{7} \times I
\]
Divide both sides by \(484\):
\[
1 = \frac{10}{7} I \implies I = \frac{7}{10} = 0.7\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The value is physically reasonable for a standard laboratory flywheel. The moment of inertia of the flywheel is \(0.7\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए मान</strong><br/>
- व्यय की गई ऊर्जा (\(W = \Delta K\)): \(484\text{ J}\)<br/>
- प्रारंभिक घूर्णन आवृत्ति (\(n_1\)): \(60\text{ rpm} = 1\text{ rev/s}\)<br/>
- अंतिम घूर्णन आवृत्ति (\(n_2\)): \(360\text{ rpm} = 6\text{ rev/s}\)</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: प्रयुक्त सूत्र</strong><br/>
- प्रारंभिक कोणीय वेग: \(\omega_1 = 2\pi n_1 = 2\pi\text{ rad/s}\)<br/>
- अंतिम कोणीय वेग: \(\omega_2 = 2\pi n_2 = 12\pi\text{ rad/s}\)<br/>
- घूर्णन गतिज ऊर्जा में परिवर्तन:
\[
W = \frac{1}{2} I (\omega_2^2 - \omega_1^2)
\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: गणना</strong><br/>
\[
484 = \frac{1}{2} I \left[(12\pi)^2 - (2\pi)^2\right] = 70\pi^2 I
\]
\(\pi \approx \frac{22}{7} \implies \pi^2 \approx \frac{484}{49}\) रखने पर:
\[
484 = 70 \times \frac{484}{49} \times I = \frac{10}{7} \times 484 \times I
\]
\[
I = \frac{7}{10} = 0.7\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2
\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
गतिपालक चक्र का जड़त्व आघूर्ण \(0.7\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2\) है।</p>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2022_SECTION_B_MCQ_Q36 - Magnitude of induced electromotive force</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>The magnetic flux linked to a circular coil of radius \(R\) is:</p>
\[
\phi = 2 t^3 + 4 t^2 + 2 t + 5\text{ Wb}
\]
<p>The magnitude of induced emf in the coil at \(t = 5\text{ s}\) is:</p>
<hr/>
<p>त्रिज्या \(R\) की एक वृत्ताकार कुंडली से सम्बद्ध चुंबकीय फ्लक्स है:</p>
\[
\phi = 2 t^3 + 4 t^2 + 2 t + 5\text{ Wb}
\]
<p>\(t = 5\text{ s}\) पर कुंडली में प्रेरित विद्युत वाहक बल (emf) का परिमाण है:</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
Magnetic flux as a function of time:
\[
\phi(t) = 2t^3 + 4t^2 + 2t + 5\text{ Wb}
\]
Time instant: \(t = 5\text{ s}\).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
According to Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction, the magnitude of induced electromotive force (\(\mathcal{E}\)) is given by the time rate of change of magnetic flux:
\[
|\mathcal{E}| = \left|\frac{d\phi}{dt}\right|
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Differentiate \(\phi(t)\) with respect to \(t\):
\[
\frac{d\phi}{dt} = \frac{d}{dt}\left(2t^3 + 4t^2 + 2t + 5\right)
\]
\[
\frac{d\phi}{dt} = 6t^2 + 8t + 2
\]
Substitute \(t = 5\text{ s}\) into the derivative:
\[
|\mathcal{E}| = 6(5)^2 + 8(5) + 2
\]
\[
|\mathcal{E}| = 6(25) + 40 + 2 = 150 + 40 + 2 = 192\text{ V}
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The units are Weber per second, which equals Volts. The magnitude of induced emf at \(t = 5\text{ s}\) is \(192\text{ V}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिया गया मान</strong><br/>
समय के फलन के रूप में चुंबकीय फ्लक्स:
\[
\phi(t) = 2t^3 + 4t^2 + 2t + 5\text{ Wb}
\]
समय: \(t = 5\text{ s}\)</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: प्रयुक्त नियम</strong><br/>
फैराडे के विद्युत चुंबकीय प्रेरण के नियमानुसार, प्रेरित विद्युत वाहक बल का परिमाण:
\[
|\mathcal{E}| = \left|\frac{d\phi}{dt}\right|
\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: गणना</strong><br/>
समय \(t\) के सापेक्ष अवकलन करने पर:
\[
\frac{d\phi}{dt} = 6t^2 + 8t + 2
\]
\(t = 5\text{ s}\) रखने पर:
\[
|\mathcal{E}| = 6(5)^2 + 8(5) + 2 = 150 + 40 + 2 = 192\text{ V}
\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
\(t = 5\text{ s}\) पर प्रेरित विद्युत वाहक बल का परिमाण \(192\text{ V}\) है।</p>

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2022_SECTION_B_MCQ_Q37 - Characteristics of astronomical refracting telescope</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>An astronomical refracting telescope is being used by an observer to observe planets in normal adjustment. The focal lengths of the objective and eye piece used in the construction of the telescope are \(20\text{ m}\) and \(2\text{ cm}\) respectively. Consider the following statements about the telescope:</p>
<p>(a) The distance between the objective and eye piece is \(20.02\text{ m}\)<br/>
(b) The magnification of the telescope is \((-)\,1000\)<br/>
(c) The image of the planet is erect and diminished<br/>
(d) The aperture of eye piece is smaller than that of objective</p>
<p>The correct statements are:</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक खगोलीय अपवर्ती दूरदर्शक का उपयोग सामान्य समायोजन में किसी प्रेक्षक द्वारा ग्रहों को देखने के लिए किया जा रहा है। दूरदर्शक के निर्माण में प्रयुक्त अभिदृश्यक और नेत्रिका की फोकस दूरियाँ क्रमशः \(20\text{ m}\) और \(2\text{ cm}\) हैं। दूरदर्शक के बारे में निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:</p>
<p>(a) अभिदृश्यक और नेत्रिका के बीच की दूरी \(20.02\text{ m}\) है<br/>
(b) दूरदर्शक का आवर्धन \((-)\,1000\) है<br/>
(c) ग्रह का प्रतिबिम्ब सीधा और छोटा बनता है<br/>
(d) नेत्रिका का द्वारक अभिदृश्यक के द्वारक की तुलना में छोटा होता है</p>
<p>सही कथन हैं:</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
- Focal length of objective lens (\(f_o\)): \(20\text{ m}\)<br/>
- Focal length of eyepiece lens (\(f_e\)): \(2\text{ cm} = 0.02\text{ m}\)<br/>
- Normal adjustment (final image formed at infinity).</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of Statement (a) - Length of the Telescope Tube</strong><br/>
In normal adjustment, the distance between the objective and the eyepiece is the sum of their focal lengths:
\[
L = f_o + f_e = 20\text{ m} + 0.02\text{ m} = 20.02\text{ m}
\]
Hence, statement (a) is correct.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Analysis of Statement (b) - Magnifying Power</strong><br/>
The magnifying power of an astronomical refracting telescope in normal adjustment is:
\[
m = -\frac{f_o}{f_e} = -\frac{20\text{ m}}{0.02\text{ m}} = -1000
\]
The negative sign signifies an inverted image. Hence, statement (b) is correct.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Analysis of Statement (c) - Nature of Image</strong><br/>
An astronomical refracting telescope forms an inverted real intermediate image, and the final image viewed by the observer is inverted (not erect) with respect to the distant object. Hence, statement (c) is incorrect.</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Analysis of Statement (d) - Aperture Size</strong><br/>
To collect sufficient light from distant faint celestial objects and achieve high resolving power, the objective lens must have a large aperture. The eyepiece needs only a small aperture to match the pupil of the human eye. Thus, the aperture of the eyepiece is smaller than that of the objective. Hence, statement (d) is correct.</p>

<p><strong>Step 6: Check and Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Statements (a), (b), and (d) are correct.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए मान</strong><br/>
- अभिदृश्यक की फोकस दूरी (\(f_o\)): \(20\text{ m}\)<br/>
- नेत्रिका की फोकस दूरी (\(f_e\)): \(2\text{ cm} = 0.02\text{ m}\)<br/>
- सामान्य समायोजन (अंतिम प्रतिबिम्ब अनंत पर बनता है)।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: कथन (a) का विश्लेषण</strong><br/>
सामान्य समायोजन में दूरदर्शक की नली की लंबाई:
\[
L = f_o + f_e = 20 + 0.02 = 20.02\text{ m}
\]
अतः कथन (a) सही है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: कथन (b) का विश्लेषण</strong><br/>
सामान्य समायोजन में कोणीय आवर्धन:
\[
m = -\frac{f_o}{f_e} = -\frac{20}{0.02} = -1000
\]
अतः कथन (b) सही है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: कथन (c) का विश्लेषण</strong><br/>

  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>At any instant, two elements \(X_1\) and \(X_2\) have same number of radioactive atoms. If the decay constant of \(X_1\) and \(X_2\) are \(10\lambda\) and \(\lambda\) respectively, then the time when the ratio of their atoms becomes \(\frac{1}{e}\) respectively will be:</p>
<hr/>
<p>किसी क्षण, दो तत्वों \(X_1\) और \(X_2\) में रेडियोऐक्टिव परमाणुओं की संख्या समान है। यदि \(X_1\) और \(X_2\) के क्षय नियतांक क्रमशः \(10\lambda\) और \(\lambda\) हैं, तो वह समय जब उनके परमाणुओं का अनुपात क्रमशः \(\frac{1}{e}\) हो जाता है, होगा:</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
- Initial number of atoms of both elements is equal: \(N_{01} = N_{02} = N_0\)<br/>
- Decay constant of \(X_1\): \(\lambda_1 = 10\lambda\)<br/>
- Decay constant of \(X_2\): \(\lambda_2 = \lambda\)<br/>
- Target ratio of undecayed atoms at time \(t\): \(\frac{N_1(t)}{N_2(t)} = \frac{1}{e} = e^{-1}\)</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
According to the law of radioactive decay:
\[
N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}
\]
Therefore, at time \(t\):
\[
N_1(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda_1 t} = N_0 e^{-10\lambda t}
\]
\[
N_2(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda_2 t} = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>
Take the ratio of \(N_1(t)\) to \(N_2(t)\):
\[
\frac{N_1(t)}{N_2(t)} = \frac{N_0 e^{-10\lambda t}}{N_0 e^{-\lambda t}} = e^{-(10\lambda - \lambda)t} = e^{-9\lambda t}
\]
Equating this ratio to the given condition \(e^{-1}\):
\[
e^{-9\lambda t} = e^{-1}
\]
Taking natural logarithms on both sides:
\[
-9\lambda t = -1 \implies 9\lambda t = 1 \implies t = \frac{1}{9\lambda}
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
Since \(\lambda_1 > \lambda_2\), \(X_1\) decays much faster than \(X_2\), so the ratio \(\frac{N_1}{N_2}\) starts at \(1\) and continuously decreases. It reaches \(e^{-1}\) at a positive finite time \(t = \frac{1}{9\lambda}\).</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए मान</strong><br/>

N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}

समय \(t\) पर परमाणुओं का अनुपात:

\frac{N_1(t)}{N_2(t)} = \frac{N_0 e^{-10\lambda t}}{N_0 e^{-\lambda t}} = e^{-9\lambda t}
\]
दिया गया है:

e^{-9\lambda t} = \frac{1}{e} = e^{-1}

<p><strong>Step 2: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
- Electrostatic potential at the center due to a system of point charges:
\[
V_{\text{center}} = \sum_{i=1}^{6} \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{q_i}{r_i}

  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2022_SECTION_B_MCQ_Q40 - Resonance frequency variation with gas temperature</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>An organ pipe filled with a gas at \(27^\circ\text{C}\) resonates at its fundamental frequency of \(400\text{ Hz}\). At \(90^\circ\text{C}\), the same pipe filled with the same gas resonates at a frequency of:</p>
<hr/>
<p>\(27^\circ\text{C}\) पर एक गैस से भरा आर्गन पाइप अपनी मूल आवृत्ति \(400\text{ Hz}\) पर अनुनाद करता है। \(90^\circ\text{C}\) पर, उसी गैस से भरा वही पाइप किस आवृत्ति पर अनुनाद करेगा:</p>
    ]]></text>

    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
- Initial temperature: \(T_1 = 27^\circ\text{C} = 27 + 273 = 300\text{ K}\)<br/>
- Fundamental resonance frequency at \(T_1\): \(f_1 = 400\text{ Hz}\)<br/>
- Final temperature: \(T_2 = 90^\circ\text{C} = 90 + 273 = 363\text{ K}\)<br/>
- The length of the pipe \(L\) and the nature of the gas (\(\gamma\) and \(M\)) remain unchanged.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
The speed of sound in an ideal gas is given by Laplace's formula:
\[

\]
The fundamental frequency of an organ pipe (whether open or closed) is directly proportional to the speed of sound:
\[

\]

\[
f \propto \sqrt{T} \implies \frac{f_2}{f_1} = \sqrt{\frac{T_2}{T_1}}

\[

\]
Now calculate \(f_2\):
\[
f_2 = 1.1 \times f_1 = 1.1 \times 400\text{ Hz} = 440\text{ Hz}
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
As temperature increases from \(300\text{ K}\) to \(363\text{ K}\), the speed of sound increases by \(10\%\), leading to a corresponding \(10\%\) increase in resonant frequency. The frequency at \(90^\circ\text{C}\) is \(440\text{ Hz}\).</p>

v = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma R T}{M}} \propto \sqrt{T}

f \propto v \propto \sqrt{T}

<question type="multichoice">
  <name><text>NEET_UG_PHYSICS_2022_SECTION_B_MCQ_Q41 - Position time graph for positive acceleration</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p>Which of the following graphs represents the position-time (\(x-t\)) graph of a particle moving along a straight line with positive acceleration?</p>
<hr/>
<p>निम्नलिखित में से कौन सा ग्राफ धनात्मक त्वरण के साथ एक सरल रेखा में गतिमान कण के स्थिति-समय (\(x-t\)) ग्राफ को दर्शाता है?</p>
    ]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principles of Position-Time Graphs</strong><br/>
In one-dimensional kinematics along a straight line:
- The slope of the tangent to the position-time (\(x-t\)) curve represents instantaneous velocity:
\[
v = \frac{dx}{dt}
\]
- Acceleration is the time rate of change of velocity, which corresponds to the second derivative of position with respect to time:
\[
a = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{d^2 x}{dt^2}
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Curvature and Sign of Acceleration</strong><br/>
- For positive acceleration (\(a > 0\)), the second derivative is positive (\(\frac{d^2 x}{dt^2} > 0\)). This means the slope (\(\frac{dx}{dt}\)) increases with time, and the \(x-t\) curve is concave upward (opens upward like a parabola).<br/>
- For negative acceleration (\(a < 0\)), \(\frac{d^2 x}{dt^2} < 0\), so the slope decreases with time, and the \(x-t\) curve is concave downward.<br/>
- For straight-line \(x-t\) graphs, the slope is constant (\(v = \text{constant}\)), which means acceleration is zero (\(a = 0\)).</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conceptual Conclusion</strong><br/>
The position-time graph that curves upward with an increasing slope (concave upward) correctly represents motion with positive acceleration.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: स्थिति-समय ग्राफ के मूल सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
एकविमीय सरल रेखा गति में:
- स्थिति-समय (\(x-t\)) वक्र की प्रवणता (ढाल) तात्क्षणिक वेग को दर्शाती है:
\[
v = \frac{dx}{dt}
\]
- त्वरण, समय के सापेक्ष स्थिति का द्वितीय अवकलज होता है:
\[
a = \frac{d^2 x}{dt^2}
\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: वक्रता तथा त्वरण का चिह्न</strong><br/>

- ऋणात्मक त्वरण (\(a < 0\)) के लिए, ग्राफ नीचे की ओर अवतल (concave downwards) होता है।<br/>
- सरल रेखा वाले ग्राफों में ढाल नियत रहता है, अतः त्वरण शून्य होता है।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
ऊपर की ओर वक्रित (concave upward) ग्राफ, जिसकी प्रवणता समय के साथ बढ़ रही है, धनात्मक त्वरण को प्रदर्शित करता है।</p>

<p>In a common base n-p-n transistor, the collector current is \(24\text{ mA}\). If \(80\%\) of the electrons emitted by the emitter reach the collector, then the base current is:</p>
<hr/>
<p>एक उभयनिष्ठ आधार n-p-n ट्रांजिस्टर में, संग्राहक धारा \(24\text{ mA}\) है। यदि उत्सर्जक द्वारा उत्सर्जित इलेक्ट्रॉनों का \(80\%\) भाग संग्राहक तक पहुँचता है, तो आधार धारा है:</p>
    ]]></text>

  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[
<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>
- Collector current (\(I_C\)): \(24\text{ mA}\)<br/>
- Fraction of emitted electrons reaching the collector: \(\alpha = 80\% = 0.80\)<br/>
- Configuration: Common base n-p-n transistor.</p>

<p><strong>Step 2: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>
In a transistor, the emitter current is related to the collector current by:
\[
I_C = \alpha I_E \implies I_E = \frac{I_C}{\alpha}
\]
By Kirchhoff's current law applied to the transistor:
\[
I_E = I_B + I_C \implies I_B = I_E - I_C
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Calculation of Magnitude of Base Current</strong><br/>
Calculate \(I_E\):
\[
I_E = \frac{24\text{ mA}}{0.80} = 30\text{ mA}
\]
Now calculate \(I_B\):
\[
I_B = 30\text{ mA} - 24\text{ mA} = 6\text{ mA}
\]</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Direction of Currents in an n-p-n Transistor</strong><br/>
- In an n-p-n transistor, the emitter is n-type, the base is p-type, and the collector is n-type.<br/>
- Electrons are injected from the n-type emitter into the base. Conventional current flows opposite to electron motion, which means conventional emitter current \(I_E\) leaves the emitter terminal.<br/>
- To satisfy \(I_E = I_B + I_C\), both the base current (\(I_B\)) and collector current (\(I_C\)) must enter their respective terminals from the external circuit.<br/>
- Therefore, the base current of \(6\text{ mA}\) enters the base terminal.</p>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>
The base current is \(6\text{ mA}\) and entering the base.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: दिए गए मान</strong><br/>
- संग्राहक धारा (\(I_C\)): \(24\text{ mA}\)<br/>
- संग्राहक तक पहुँचने वाले इलेक्ट्रॉनों का प्रतिशत: \(\alpha = 80\% = 0.80\)<br/>
- ट्रांजिस्टर प्रकार: n-p-n उभयनिष्ठ आधार</p>

<p><strong>चरण 2: प्रयुक्त सूत्र</strong><br/>
\[
I_C = \alpha I_E \implies I_E = \frac{I_C}{\alpha}
\]
\[
I_E = I_B + I_C \implies I_B = I_E - I_C
\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 3: आधार धारा के परिमाण की गणना</strong><br/>
\[
I_E = \frac{24\text{ mA}}{0.80} = 30\text{ mA}
\]
\[
I_B = 30\text{ mA} - 24\text{ mA} = 6\text{ mA}
\]</p>

<p><strong>चरण 4: धारा की दिशा</strong><br/>
n-p-n ट्रांजिस्टर में आधार p-प्रकार का होता है तथा उत्सर्जक n-प्रकार का होता है। पारंपरिक धारा आधार में प्रवेश करती है (\(I_B\) entering the base) और संग्राहक में प्रवेश करती है, जबकि उत्सर्जक से बाहर निकलती है (\(I_E = I_B + I_C\))।</p>

<p><strong>चरण 5: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
आधार धारा \(6\text{ mA}\) है तथा आधार में प्रवेश करती हुई (entering the base) है।</p>
