Members of the class Cyclostomata are jawless aquatic vertebrates belonging to Agnatha. They have an elongated body bearing 6–15 pairs of gill slits for respiration, and their body is devoid of scales and paired fins.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Analysis of Statements</strong><br/>
- Cyclostomes possess a sucking and circular mouth without jaws. Therefore, stating that they possess jaws is incorrect.<br/>
- Cyclostomes are marine organisms that migrate to fresh water (anadromous migration) for spawning, after which they die within a few days. Therefore, stating that they migrate to the deep sea for spawning is incorrect.<br/>

<p>(1) Connect crown of tooth with its root.<br/>
(2) Masticate the food.<br/>
(3) Form bolus.<br/>
(4) Give basic shape to the teeth.</p>
<hr/>
<p>इनेमल (Enamel) का कार्य है :</p>
<p>(1) दाँत के शिखर (crown) को उसकी जड़ (root) से जोड़ना।<br/>
(2) भोजन को चबाना (Masticate the food)।<br/>

  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
Enamel covers the crown of the tooth and is the hardest and most heavily mineralized substance in the human body.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Physiological Function</strong><br/>
The primary function of the hard chewing surface formed by enamel is to break down food mechanically during chewing, a process designated as mastication of food. Formation of a lubricated food bolus is mediated by salivary mucus and tongue movements, while periodontal ligaments and cementum anchor teeth to the alveoli of the jaw.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The primary role of enamel is to masticate the food.</p>
<hr/>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
Connective tissues include specialized supporting skeletal tissues (bones, cartilage), fluid connective tissue (blood), and dense or loose connective tissues (tendons, ligaments, adipose tissue).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Evaluation of Statements</strong><br/>
- Bones form the rigid structural framework of the body, providing physical support and protection to delicate visceral organs and softer tissues (Statement a is correct).<br/>
- The long bones of the lower extremities serve principal weight-bearing functions of the body (Statement b is correct).<br/>
- Blood cells are produced within red bone marrow located in the cavities of certain bones, not within ligaments (Statement c is incorrect).<br/>
- Adipose tissue is a loose connective tissue whose adipocytes are specialized to store neutral fats (Statement d is correct).<br/>
- Tendons are dense regular connective tissues that attach skeletal muscles to bones, whereas ligaments attach bone to bone (Statement e is incorrect).</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
The correct statements are (a), (b), and (d) only.</p>

  <name><text>NEET_UG_ZOOLOGY_2022_SECTION_A_MCQ_Q185 - Bivalent or tetrad formation stage</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p>Bivalent or Tetrad formation is a characteristic feature observed during :</p>
<p>(1) Synaptonemal complex in zygotene stage<br/>
(2) Chiasmata in Diplotene stage<br/>
(3) Synaptonemal complex in Pachytene stage<br/>
(4) Chiasmata in zygotene stage</p>
<hr/>
<p>द्विसंयोजी (Bivalent) या चतुष्क (Tetrad) निर्माण एक विशिष्ट लक्षण है जो देखा जाता है :</p>
<p>(1) जाइगोटीन अवस्था में सिनेप्टोनीमल सम्मिश्र के दौरान<br/>
(2) डिप्लोटीन अवस्था में काएज्मेटा के दौरान<br/>
(3) पैकिटीन अवस्था में सिनेप्टोनीमल सम्मिश्र के दौरान<br/>
(4) जाइगोटीन अवस्था में काएज्मेटा के दौरान</p>]]></text>
  </questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html">
    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Conceptual Principle</strong><br/>
During prophase I of meiosis, chromosomes undergo pairing and genetic exchange across five sequential sub-stages: leptotene, zygotene, pachytene, diplotene, and diakinesis.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Synapsis and Complex Formation</strong><br/>
During the zygotene stage, homologous chromosomes begin pairing together in a process known as synapsis. This synapsis is accompanied by the assembly of an electron-dense nucleoprotein framework termed the synaptonemal complex. The paired homologous chromosomes held together by the synaptonemal complex form a bivalent or tetrad.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Comparison with Other Stages</strong><br/>
Recombination and crossing over occur during pachytene. Dissolution of the synaptonemal complex and appearance of X-shaped chiasmata occur in diplotene.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
Bivalent or tetrad formation is a characteristic feature observed during the formation of the synaptonemal complex in the zygotene stage.</p>
<hr/>
<p><strong>चरण 1: संकल्पनात्मक सिद्धांत</strong><br/>
अर्धसूत्री विभाजन I के पूर्वावस्था I में समजात गुणसूत्र पाँच चरणों (तनुपट्ट/लेप्टोटीन, युग्मपट्ट/जाइगोटीन, स्थूलपट्ट/पैकिटीन, द्विपट्ट/डिप्लोटीन, और पारगतिक्रम/डायकाइनेसिस) से गुजरते हैं।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 2: सूत्रयुग्मन एवं सम्मिश्र निर्माण</strong><br/>
जाइगोटीन अवस्था के दौरान समजात गुणसूत्र जोड़े बनाने लगते हैं, इस प्रक्रिया को सूत्रयुग्मन (synapsis) कहते हैं। इस दौरान एक विशिष्ट संरचना बनती है जिसे सिनेप्टोनीमल सम्मिश्र (synaptonemal complex) कहते हैं। इस प्रकार बने युग्म को द्विसंयोजी (bivalent) या चतुष्क (tetrad) कहा जाता है।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 3: अन्य चरणों से तुलना</strong><br/>
जीन विनिमय (crossing over) पैकिटीन में होता है, तथा सिनेप्टोनीमल सम्मिश्र के विघटन से X-आकार के काएज्मेटा डिप्लोटीन अवस्था में स्पष्ट होते हैं।</p>
<p><strong>चरण 4: अंतिम निष्कर्ष</strong><br/>
द्विसंयोजी या चतुष्क निर्माण जाइगोटीन अवस्था में सिनेप्टोनीमल सम्मिश्र के दौरान देखा जाने वाला एक विशिष्ट लक्षण है।</p>]]></text>

    <text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Conditions and Genotypes</strong><br/>
Haemophilia is a sex-linked (X-linked) recessive genetic disorder. Let \(X^h\) denote the mutant haemophilic allele and \(X\) denote the normal allele.<br/>
- The mother of the girl is haemophilic, so her maternal genotype is \(X^h X^h\).<br/>
- The girl is phenotypically normal, meaning she carries one normal allele \(X\) from her father and must inherit one \(X^h\) allele from her mother. Hence, her genotype is heterozygous carrier female: \(X X^h\).<br/>
- The male partner has no family history of haemophilia, so his genotype is normal hemizygous male: \(X Y\).</p>
<p><strong>Step 2: Punnett Square Analysis</strong><br/>
Cross: \(X X^h \times X Y\)<br/>
The possible combinations of gametes yield:<br/>
1. \(X X\) : Phenotypically normal daughter.<br/>
2. \(X X^h\) : Phenotypically normal carrier daughter.<br/>
3. \(X Y\) : Normal son.<br/>
4. \(X^h Y\) : Haemophilic son.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3: Evaluation of Possible Phenotypic Combinations</strong><br/>
- A haemophilic daughter requires an \(X^h\) allele from both parents (\(X^h X^h\)), which is impossible because the father donates only a normal \(X\) chromosome to daughters. Therefore, outcomes containing a haemophilic daughter cannot occur.<br/>
- Outcomes containing a haemophilic son, carrier daughter, normal daughter, and normal son are possible combinations.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4: Final Conclusion</strong><br/>
